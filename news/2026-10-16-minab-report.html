پنتاگون گزارش حمله به مدرسه‌ی میناب را به تأخیر می‌اندازدPentagon delays its report on the Minab school strike

Pentāgon gozāresh-e hamle be madrese-ye Mināb rā be ta'khir mi-andāzad

World, level B1. Source: Democracy Now / Bloomberg

Show

در روزهای اول جنگ، یک مدرسه در شهر میناب در جنوب ایران هدف حمله قرار گرفت.

dar ruz-hā-ye avval-e jang, yek madrese dar shahr-e Mināb dar jonub-e Irān hadaf-e hamle gharār gereft.

In the first days of the war, a school in the city of Minab in southern Iran was hit by a strike.

دست‌کم ۱۵۶ نفر کشته شدند.

dast-e-kam sad-o-panjāh-o-shesh nafar koshte shodand.

At least 156 people were killed.

در میان آن‌ها ۱۲۰ کودک و ۲۶ معلم بودند.

dar miyān-e ān-hā sad-o-bist kudak o bist-o-shesh mo'allem budand.

Among them were 120 children and 26 teachers.

وزارت دفاع آمریکا درباره‌ی این حمله گزارشی نوشته است.

vezārat-e defā'-e Āmrikā dar-bāre-ye in hamle gozāresh-i neveshte ast.

The US Department of Defense has written a report about this strike.

به گزارش بلومبرگ، این گزارش تا بعد از انتخابات نوامبر منتشر نمی‌شود.

be gozāresh-e Bloomberg, in gozāresh tā ba'd az entekhābāt-e novāmbr montasher nemi-shavad.

According to Bloomberg, the report will not be published until after the November elections.

خانواده‌های قربانیان خواهان حقیقت هستند.

khānevāde-hā-ye ghorbāniyān khāhān-e haghighat hastand.

The victims' families want the truth.

میناب شهری کوچک نزدیک بندرعباس است.

Mināb shahr-i kuchak nazdik-e Bandar-abbās ast.

Minab is a small city near Bandar Abbas.

Background

The strike on a girls' school in Minab, Hormozgan province, on the first day of the war was one of the deadliest single events of the conflict. Iranian media reported about 180 dead at the time; the figure of 156, including 120 children, is from the reporting on the Pentagon's internal review. This story shows how Persian news writes numbers (دست‌کم 'at least', در میان آن‌ها 'among them') and the passive with شدن.
