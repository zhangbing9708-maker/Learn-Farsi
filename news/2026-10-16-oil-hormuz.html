News / 2026-10-16

نفت بالای ۱۰۰ دلار ماند؛ حمله به کشتی‌ها در تنگه‌ی هرمز ادامه داردOil stays above $100 as ships are attacked near the Strait of Hormuz

naft bālā-ye sad dolār mānd; hamle be kashti-hā dar tange-ye Hormoz edāme dārad

Business, level B1. Source: Fox News / IEA

Show

قیمت نفت این هفته بالای صد دلار ماند.

gheymat-e naft in hafte bālā-ye sad dolār mānd.

The price of oil stayed above a hundred dollars this week.

نفت برنت روز جمعه حدود ۱۰۵ دلار بود.

naft-e Brent ruz-e jom'e hodud-e sad-o-panj dolār bud.

Brent oil was about 105 dollars on Friday.

دلیل اصلی، حمله به کشتی‌ها در تنگه‌ی هرمز است.

dalil-e asli, hamle be kashti-hā dar tange-ye Hormoz ast.

The main reason is the attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz.

به گفته‌ی مقام‌های دریایی، بین پنجم و یازدهم اکتبر دوازده کشتی هدف قرار گرفتند.

be gofte-ye maghām-hā-ye daryāyi, beyn-e panjom o yāzdahom-e oktobr davāzdah kashti hadaf gharār gereftand.

According to maritime officials, twelve ships were targeted between the 5th and the 11th of October.

یک کشتی باری این هفته غرق شد.

yek kashti-ye bāri in hafte gharegh shod.

One cargo ship sank this week.

بیش از چهل کشتی در یک روز از تنگه گذشتند.

bish az chehel kashti dar yek ruz az tange gozashtand.

More than forty ships passed through the strait in one day.

آژانس بین‌المللی انرژی چهارصد میلیون بشکه نفت از ذخیره‌های اضطراری آزاد می‌کند.

āzhāns-e beyn-ol-melali-ye enerzhi chahārsad milyun boshke naft az zakhire-hā-ye ezterāri āzād mi-konad.

The International Energy Agency is releasing four hundred million barrels of oil from emergency reserves.

کارشناسان می‌گویند قیمت بنزین در بسیاری از کشورها بالا رفته است.

kārshenāsān mi-guyand gheymat-e benzin dar besyāri az keshvar-hā bālā rafte ast.

Experts say the price of petrol has gone up in many countries.

Background

About a fifth of the world's oil normally passes through the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow channel between Iran and Oman. Since the war between the United States, Israel and Iran began in February 2026, attacks on shipping there have pushed oil above $100 a barrel and raised fuel prices worldwide. In Persian the strait is تنگه‌ی هرمز (tange-ye Hormoz); the island of Hormuz gave it its name.
