الفبای فارسیThe Persian alphabet

alefbā-ye fārsi

Persian (Farsi) is written from right to left with 32 letters. Most letters join the next letter in a word, so their shape changes a little with their position. Tap a letter to hear its example word.

Vowels

Short vowels are small marks that are usually left out; long vowels are written as letters.

  1. برف barf snow
  2. برنج berenj rice
  3. بز boz goat
  4. آسمان āsemān sky
  5. دوست dust friend
  6. ماهی māhi fish

Remember

  • Persian is written and read from right to left, but numbers run left to right.
  • Seven letters never join the letter after them: ا د ذ ر ز ژ و. In دارو (dāru, medicine) all four letters stand apart.
  • Some sounds have several letters: t = ت ط, s = ث س ص, z = ذ ز ض ظ, h = ح ه, gh = غ ق. Learn the spelling with each word.
  • Short vowels (a, e, o) are usually not written: کتاب (ketāb, book) is just k-t-ā-b. Learn every word with its pronunciation.
  • A vowel at the start of a word sits on ا: اسب (asb, horse), ایران (irān). Long ā at the start is written آ: آب (āb, water).
  • Tanvin ـاً is read -an at the end of some words from Arabic: لطفاً (lotfan, please), حتماً (hatman, definitely).
