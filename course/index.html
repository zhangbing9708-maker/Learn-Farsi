دوره‌ی آموزشیThe course

dowre-ye āmuzeshi

Six stages, from your first letter to reading Hafez. Each lesson has a dialogue or reading with recorded audio, the words to learn, clear grammar, a note on culture and exercises that check themselves. 37 lessons are ready; the rest are being written. Tick a lesson off when you finish it and your progress is kept in this browser.
