هوا و فصل‌هاWeather and seasons

havā o fasl-hā

Iran has four real seasons – snow in Tabriz, sun in Bandar Abbas, and both in Tehran. In this lesson you talk about the weather, name the seasons, say what weather you like and understand the little impersonal sentences Iranians use every morning: havā sard ast, bārān mi-āyad.

In this lesson you will learn to

  • say what the weather is like today
  • name the four seasons and link them to the Iranian months
  • say what weather you like and don't like
  • use impersonal sentences with است and verbs like می‌آید and می‌بارد

A cold morning in Tehran

Tom, an Australian student, meets his neighbour Mina at the bus stop on a winter morning.

Tom

سلام، مینا. امروز هوا خیلی سرد است!

salām, Minā. emruz havā kheyli sard ast!

Hello, Mina. The weather is very cold today!

Spoken سلام، مینا. امروز هوا خیلی سرده! salām, Minā. emruz havā kheyli sarde!

Mina

بله، زمستان است. در تهران زمستان‌ها سرد است.

bale, zemestān ast. dar Tehrān zemestān-hā sard ast.

Yes, it's winter. Winters are cold in Tehran.

Tom

برف هم می‌آید؟

barf ham mi-āyad?

Does it snow too?

Spoken برف هم میاد؟ barf ham miyād?

Mina

بله، بعضی وقت‌ها برف می‌بارد. کوه‌ها الان پر از برف هستند.

bale, ba'zi vaght-hā barf mi-bārad. kuh-hā al'ān por az barf hastand.

Yes, sometimes it snows. The mountains are full of snow now.

Tom

در استرالیا الان تابستان است و هوا گرم است.

dar Ostorāliyā al'ān tābestān ast o havā garm ast.

In Australia it is summer now and the weather is warm.

Mina

چه خوب! شما چه فصلی را دوست دارید؟

che khub! shomā che fasl-i rā dust dārid?

How nice! Which season do you like?

Tom

من بهار را دوست دارم. هوا خوب است و همه جا سبز است.

man bahār rā dust dāram. havā khub ast o hame jā sabz ast.

I like spring. The weather is good and everywhere is green.

Mina

من هم بهار را دوست دارم، به خاطر نوروز!

man ham bahār rā dust dāram, be khāter-e nowruz!

I like spring too, because of Nowruz!

Tom

فردا هوا چطور است؟

fardā havā chetor ast?

What is the weather like tomorrow?

Spoken فردا هوا چطوره؟ fardā havā chetore?

Mina

فردا آفتابی است، اما باز هم سرد است. کلاه بیاورید!

fardā āftābi ast, ammā bāz ham sard ast. kolāh biyāvarid!

Tomorrow it's sunny, but still cold. Bring a hat!

Talking about the weather

The Persian word for weather is هوا havā weather; air – the same word means air. The simplest weather sentence is havā + adjective + ast: هوا سرد است. havā sard ast. The weather is cold. هوا گرم است. havā garm ast. The weather is warm. هوا خوب است. havā khub ast. The weather is good.

To ask, use the question word you already know from lesson 1: هوا چطور است؟ havā chetor ast? How is the weather? In speech this becomes havā chetore?

Rain and snow come: باران می‌آید. bārān mi-āyad. It is raining. (literally rain comes) and برف می‌آید. barf mi-āyad. It is snowing. A more written verb is باریدن bāridan to fall (of rain or snow): باران می‌بارد. bārān mi-bārad. Both are correct; in conversation you will hear mi-āyad (spoken miyād) far more often.

Iranians talk about the weather exactly as much as the British do, and for the same reason: with mountains, desert and two seas, it changes a lot.

Sentences without a person: it is cold, it is raining

English needs a dummy it: it is cold, it is raining. Persian simply names the thing: havā (the weather) is cold, bārān (rain) comes. There is no empty it.

With adjectives you use ast (spoken -e): هوا سرد است. havā sard ast. → 🗣 havā sarde. For the negative, nist: هوا سرد نیست. havā sard nist. The weather is not cold.

With rain, snow and wind you use a verb in the present tense, which you learned in lesson 11: باران می‌آید bārān mi-āyad rain comes, برف می‌آید barf mi-āyad snow comes, باد می‌آید bād mi-āyad wind comes. The negative puts na- before mi-: باران نمی‌آید. bārān nemi-āyad. It is not raining.

Temperatures use daraje (degree): امروز بیست درجه است. emruz bist daraje ast. It is twenty degrees today. Below zero is زیر صفر zir-e sefr below zero.

امروز هوا آفتابی است.

Today the weather is sunny.

Spoken امروز هوا آفتابیه. emruz havā āftābiye.

dar Shirāz zemestān kheyli sard nist.

In Shiraz winter is not very cold.

در رشت همیشه باران می‌آید.

dar Rasht hamishe bārān mi-āyad.

In Rasht it always rains.

هوا امروز دو درجه زیر صفر است.

havā emruz do daraje zir-e sefr ast.

It is two degrees below zero today.

The four seasons and their months

You learned the Iranian months in lesson 12. The Iranian year begins with spring, on the first day of فروردین Farvardin (around 21 March), and each season is exactly three months long. That is why Iranians say their calendar follows nature: بهار bahār spring is Farvardin, Ordibehesht, Khordād; تابستان tābestān summer is Tir, Mordād, Shahrivar; پاییز pāyiz autumn is Mehr, Ābān, Āzar; and زمستان zemestān winter is Dey, Bahman, Esfand.

To say in spring, use dar or nothing at all: در بهار هوا خوب است. dar bahār havā khub ast. In spring the weather is good. To say this summer, use این: این تابستان in tābestān this summer. Plurals make general statements: زمستان‌ها سرد است. zemestān-hā sard ast. Winters are cold. – notice the verb stays singular because the subject is a thing, not a person.

Four seasons in one country

Read the text about the weather in different parts of Iran. Then answer the questions.

ایران کشور چهار فصل است.

Irān keshvar-e chahār fasl ast.

Iran is the country of four seasons.

در زمستان، در تبریز برف می‌آید و هوا خیلی سرد است.

dar zemestān, dar Tabriz barf mi-āyad o havā kheyli sard ast.

In winter, it snows in Tabriz and the weather is very cold.

اما در همان روز، در بندرعباس هوا گرم و آفتابی است.

ammā dar hamān ruz, dar Bandar-abbās havā garm o āftābi ast.

But on the same day, in Bandar Abbas the weather is warm and sunny.

در شمال، کنار دریای خزر، همیشه باران می‌آید و همه جا سبز است.

dar shomāl, kenār-e daryā-ye Khazar, hamishe bārān mi-āyad o hame jā sabz ast.

In the north, by the Caspian Sea, it always rains and everywhere is green.

در کویر، در تابستان هوا پنجاه درجه است!

dar kavir, dar tābestān havā panjāh daraje ast!

In the desert, in summer it is fifty degrees!

تهران بین کوه و کویر است: زمستان سرد و تابستان گرم.

Tehrān beyn-e kuh o kavir ast: zemestān sard o tābestān garm.

Tehran is between the mountains and the desert: a cold winter and a hot summer.

بهترین فصل برای سفر به ایران بهار است.

behtarin fasl barā-ye safar be Irān bahār ast.

The best season for a trip to Iran is spring.

Damāvand, the Caspian and the desert

Iran is twice the size of Turkey and has almost every climate on earth. The البرز Alborz mountains run along the north; their highest peak, دماوند Damāvand, is 5,610 m – higher than any mountain in Europe – and is covered in snow all year. On the far side of the Alborz lies the دریای خزر daryā-ye Khazar Caspian Sea, the world's largest lake, with rice fields, tea plantations and rain. South of Tehran begin the two great deserts, دشت کویر dasht-e kavir and دشت لوت dasht-e lut; Lut has recorded some of the hottest ground temperatures ever measured.

Tehranis escape the summer heat by driving north to the Caspian for the weekend – the Chālus road through the mountains is famous for its traffic jams. In winter they ski at Dizin and Tochāl, forty minutes from the city centre. So when someone from Tehran asks هوا چطور است؟ havā chetor ast?, the honest answer is: it depends which way you are facing.

Tips

  • garm means both warm and hot. If you really want to say hot for weather, say خیلی گرم kheyli garm; dāgh is for hot objects and drinks: چای داغ chāy-e dāgh hot tea.
  • khonak is a compliment to the weather – pleasantly cool, like a spring evening. sard is simply cold.
  • The spoken form of mi-āyad is miyād: bārān miyād, barf miyād. You will hear this ten times more often than the written form.
  • Seasons are easy to remember: bahār starts the year at Nowruz, and the autumn month Mehr is also the month schools reopen – the start of the Iranian school year is 1 Mehr (around 23 September).
