سفر به اصفهانA trip to Isfahan

safar be Esfahān

Tom and Reza spend the Nowruz holiday in Isfahan, the old Safavid capital, famous for its blue-tiled mosques, its vast square and its bridges. This travel story practises the past tenses, time phrases (روز اول 'on the first day', عصر 'in the late afternoon', شب 'in the evening'), the verb طول کشیدن 'to take (time)', a که-clause after 'know' and 'understand', and a famous Persian saying.

Elementary (A2), ۱۷۲ کلمه (172 words)

Show

پارسال در تعطیلات نوروز، تام و رضا با اتوبوس به اصفهان رفتند.

pārsāl dar ta'tilāt-e nowruz, Tām o Rezā bā otobus be Esfahān raftand.

Last year, in the Nowruz holidays, Tom and Reza went to Isfahan by bus.

سفر از تهران تا اصفهان حدود پنج ساعت طول کشید.

safar az Tehrān tā Esfahān hodud-e panj sā'at tul keshid.

The journey from Tehran to Isfahan took about five hours.

در راه، رضا گفت: «اصفهان نصف جهان است!»

dar rāh, Rezā goft: «Esfahān nesf-e jahān ast!»

On the way, Reza said: "Isfahan is half the world!"

تام نمی‌دانست که این یک ضرب‌المثل قدیمی است.

Tām nemi-dānest ke in yek zarb-ol-masal-e ghadimi ast.

Tom didn't know that this was an old saying.

روز اول، آن‌ها به میدان نقش جهان رفتند.

ruz-e avval, ān-hā be meydān-e Naghsh-e Jahān raftand.

On the first day they went to Naqsh-e Jahan Square.

میدان خیلی بزرگ بود و دور تا دور آن مغازه بود.

meydān kheyli bozorg bud o dowr tā dowr-e ān maghāze bud.

The square was huge, with shops all the way round it.

تام کاشی‌های آبی مسجد شاه را خیلی دوست داشت.

Tām kāshi-hā-ye ābi-ye masjed-e Shāh rā kheyli dust dāsht.

Tom loved the blue tiles of the Shah Mosque.

بعد به بالای کاخ عالی‌قاپو رفتند و میدان را از آن‌جا تماشا کردند.

ba'd be bālā-ye kākh-e Āli-ghāpu raftand o meydān rā az ān-jā tamāshā kardand.

Then they climbed to the top of the Ali Qapu palace and looked at the square from there.

در بازار، رضا برای مادرش گز خرید.

dar bāzār, Rezā barā-ye mādar-ash gaz kharid.

In the bazaar, Reza bought gaz for his mother.

عصر، آن‌ها کنار زاینده‌رود قدم زدند.

asr, ān-hā kenār-e Zāyande-rud ghadam zadand.

In the late afternoon they took a walk along the Zayandeh River.

وقتی هوا تاریک شد، چراغ‌های سی‌وسه‌پل روشن شدند.

vaghti havā tārik shod, cherāgh-hā-ye Si-o-se-pol rowshan shodand.

When it got dark, the lights of Si-o-se Pol came on.

این پل سی و سه طاق دارد و حدود چهارصد سال پیش آن را ساختند.

in pol si o se tāgh dārad o hodud-e chahār-sad sāl pish ān rā sākhtand.

This bridge has thirty-three arches, and it was built about four hundred years ago.

زیر طاق‌های پل، چند مرد جوان آواز می‌خواندند و مردم گوش می‌دادند.

zir-e tāgh-hā-ye pol, chand mard-e javān āvāz mi-khāndand o mardom gush mi-dādand.

Under the arches, some young men were singing and people were listening.

تام گفت: «حالا می‌فهمم که چرا می‌گویند اصفهان نصف جهان است.»

Tām goft: «hālā mi-fahmam ke cherā mi-guyand Esfahān nesf-e jahān ast.»

Tom said: "Now I understand why they say Isfahan is half the world."

شب، در یک رستوران قدیمی بریانی خوردند.

shab, dar yek resturān-e ghadimi beryāni khordand.

In the evening they ate beryani in an old restaurant.

تام اول نمی‌دانست بریانی چیست، اما بعد از اولین لقمه عاشقش شد.

Tām avval nemi-dānest beryāni chist, ammā ba'd az avvalin loghme āshegh-ash shod.

At first Tom didn't know what beryani was, but after the first bite he fell in love with it.

رضا گفت: «اگر این سفر را دوست داشتی، نوروز بعد با هم به شیراز می‌رویم!»

Rezā goft: «agar in safar rā dust dāshti, nowruz-e ba'd bā ham be Shirāz mi-ravim!»

Reza said: "If you liked this trip, next Nowruz we'll go to Shiraz together!"

About this story

اصفهان نصف جهان (Esfahān nesf-e jahān), 'Isfahan is half the world', goes back to Shah Abbas, who made Isfahan his capital in 1598 and built most of what Tom saw: Naqsh-e Jahan Square, the Shah Mosque (officially the Imam Mosque, but Isfahanis still say مسجد شاه), the Ali Qapu palace and Si-o-se Pol, 'the bridge of thirty-three', finished around 1602. On warm evenings people still gather under its arches to sing. Notice می‌گویند 'they say' and آن را ساختند 'they built it': when the doer does not matter, Persian simply uses 'they'. And in اگر این سفر را دوست داشتی Reza uses the past after اگر because the liking has already happened. گز (gaz) is Isfahan's soft pistachio nougat, and بریانی (beryāni) its famous dish of minced lamb served on flatbread.
