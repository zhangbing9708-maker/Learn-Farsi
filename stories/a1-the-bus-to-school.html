اتوبوس مدرسهThe bus to school

otobus-e madrese

Every morning at seven a yellow bus takes Sara and her friend Leila to school through the Tehran traffic. Today the two girls chat and laugh so much that Sara forgets something important on the bus. The story practises getting on and off (سوار شدن، پیاده شدن), the present tense with 'they' (حرف می‌زنند، می‌خندند) and the negative ندارد, and ends with a kind and funny bus driver.

Beginner (A1), ۸۱ کلمه (81 words)

Show

ساعت هفت صبح است.

sā'at-e haft-e sobh ast.

It is seven o'clock in the morning.

سارا با کیفش جلوی در خانه منتظر است.

Sārā bā kif-ash jelo-ye dar-e khāne montazer ast.

Sara is waiting with her bag in front of the door of the house.

اتوبوس زرد مدرسه می‌آید.

otobus-e zard-e madrese mi-āyad.

The yellow school bus comes.

سارا سوار می‌شود و کنار لیلا می‌نشیند.

Sārā savār mi-shavad o kenār-e Leylā mi-neshinad.

Sara gets on and sits next to Leila.

دو دوست حرف می‌زنند و می‌خندند.

do dust harf mi-zanand o mi-khandand.

The two friends talk and laugh.

اتوبوس به مدرسه می‌رسد.

otobus be madrese mi-resad.

The bus arrives at school.

سارا و لیلا پیاده می‌شوند.

Sārā o Leylā piyāde mi-shavand.

Sara and Leila get off.

اما کیف سارا در اتوبوس است!

ammā kif-e Sārā dar otobus ast!

But Sara's bag is on the bus!

در کلاس، سارا کتاب ندارد و دفتر ندارد.

dar kelās, Sārā ketāb na-dārad o daftar na-dārad.

In class, Sara has no book and no notebook.

او خیلی ناراحت است.

u kheyli nārāhat ast.

She is very upset.

ناگهان کسی در می‌زند.

nāgahān kasi dar mi-zanad.

Suddenly someone knocks at the door.

راننده‌ی اتوبوس با کیف سارا پشت در است!

rānande-ye otobus bā kif-e Sārā posht-e dar ast!

The bus driver is at the door with Sara's bag!

راننده می‌خندد و می‌گوید: «سارا خانم، کیف تو هم می‌خواهد به مدرسه بیاید!»

rānande mi-khandad o mi-guyad: «Sārā khānom, kif-e to ham mi-khāhad be madrese bi-yāyad!»

The driver laughs and says: "Miss Sara, your bag wants to come to school too!"

About this story

Persian gets on and off with two compound verbs: سوار شدن 'to become a rider' (get on a bus, get into a car, mount a horse) and پیاده شدن 'to become a walker' (get off, get out). You will hear the second one at every bus stop and in every taxi. در زدن 'to knock' is literally 'to hit the door'. The driver's سارا خانم 'Miss Sara' is a playful way adults talk to a little girl – خانم after a first name is polite and warm, just like آقا for a boy: امیر آقا. And notice his joke: کیف تو هم می‌خواهد به مدرسه بیاید – after می‌خواهد 'wants', the second verb is in the subjunctive, بیاید '(that) it comes'.
