داستان‌هاStories

dāstān-hā

4 graded readers: folk tales, Mulla Nasreddin, the Shahnameh, history and everyday life in Iran. Every sentence has recorded audio, a transliteration and a translation you can hide. Start at your level and read a little every day.

Beginner (A1)

Very short and in the present tense. You can read these after the first lessons.

  • گربه و ماهیThe cat and the fishEveryday life
  • سارا به مدرسه می‌رودSara goes to schoolEveryday life
  • یک روز در بازارA day in the bazaarEveryday life

Intermediate (B1)

Legends from the Shahnameh, history and modern life, 250–450 words.

  • رستم و سهرابRostam and SohrabLegend
