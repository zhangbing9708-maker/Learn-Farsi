سارا به مدرسه می‌رودSara goes to school

Sārā be madrese mi-ravad

A morning in the life of Sara, a nine-year-old in Tehran. This story practises the present tense of everyday verbs – wakes up, eats, goes, learns – and the words for school and family. Iranian schools start early, around 7:30, and the school week runs from Saturday to Wednesday or Thursday.

Beginner (A1), ۸۲ کلمه (82 words)

سارا نُه سال دارد.

Sārā noh sāl dārad.

Sara is nine years old.

او در تهران زندگی می‌کند.

u dar Tehrān zendegi mi-konad.

She lives in Tehran.

صبح است.

sobh ast.

It is morning.

سارا ساعت شش بیدار می‌شود.

Sārā sā'at-e shesh bidār mi-shavad.

Sara wakes up at six o'clock.

مادر سارا چای و نان و پنیر می‌آورد.

mādar-e Sārā chāy o nān o panir mi-āvarad.

Sara's mother brings tea, bread and cheese.

سارا صبحانه می‌خورد.

Sārā sobhāne mi-khorad.

Sara eats breakfast.

او کیف و کتاب‌هایش را برمی‌دارد.

u kif o ketāb-hā-yash rā bar-mi-dārad.

She picks up her bag and her books.

سارا با پدرش به مدرسه می‌رود.

Sārā bā pedar-ash be madrese mi-ravad.

Sara goes to school with her father.

مدرسه نزدیک خانه است.

madrese nazdik-e khāne ast.

The school is near the house.

دوست سارا، لیلا، هم آن‌جا است.

dust-e Sārā, Leylā, ham ān-jā ast.

Sara's friend Leila is there too.

آن‌ها به معلم سلام می‌کنند.

ān-hā be mo'allem salām mi-konand.

They say hello to the teacher.

معلم می‌گوید: «صبح به خیر، بچه‌ها!»

mo'allem mi-guyad: «sobh be kheyr, bacche-hā!»

The teacher says: "Good morning, children!"

امروز سارا فارسی و ریاضی دارد.

emruz Sārā fārsi o riyāzi dārad.

Today Sara has Persian and maths.

او ریاضی را خیلی دوست دارد.

u riyāzi rā kheyli dust dārad.

She likes maths very much.

ساعت یک، مدرسه تمام می‌شود.

sā'at-e yek, madrese tamām mi-shavad.

At one o'clock, school finishes.

سارا خوشحال به خانه می‌رود.

Sārā khosh-hāl be khāne mi-ravad.

Sara goes home happy.

About this story

Bread, cheese and sweet tea – نان و پنیر و چای – is the classic Iranian breakfast, often with walnuts or herbs. Notice the compound verbs made with شدن: بیدار می‌شود 'wakes up' (literally 'becomes awake'), تمام می‌شود 'finishes' ('becomes complete'). Half of Persian verbs are built this way. Also notice ساعت شش 'six o'clock': ساعت means hour, clock and o'clock all at once.
