صبحانه در تهرانBreakfast in Tehran

sobhāne dar Tehrān

It is Friday, the Iranian weekend, and Tom is invited to breakfast at Reza and Mina's flat. Fresh barbari bread, white cheese, walnuts, butter, honey and glasses of tea – but everyone at the table eats something different. The story practises food words, دوست داشتن 'to like' and می‌خواهد 'wants', and shows you a Persian word for a very Iranian kind of bite.

Beginner (A1), ۸۶ کلمه (86 words)

امروز جمعه است.

emruz jom'e ast.

Today is Friday.

رضا صبح زود به نانوایی می‌رود و نان بربری می‌خرد.

Rezā sobh-e zud be nānvāyi mi-ravad o nān-e barbari mi-kharad.

Early in the morning Reza goes to the bakery and buys barbari bread.

مینا چای درست می‌کند.

Minā chāy dorost mi-konad.

Mina makes tea.

تام هم امروز مهمان آن‌ها است.

Tām ham emruz mehmān-e ān-hā ast.

Today Tom is their guest too.

روی سفره نان، پنیر، گردو، کره و عسل هست.

ru-ye sofre nān, panir, gerdu, kare o 'asal hast.

On the sofreh there is bread, cheese, walnuts, butter and honey.

رضا نان و پنیر و گردو می‌خورد.

Rezā nān o panir o gerdu mi-khorad.

Reza eats bread, cheese and walnuts.

مینا نان و کره و عسل دوست دارد.

Minā nān o kare o 'asal dust dārad.

Mina likes bread, butter and honey.

سارا فقط یک تخم‌مرغ می‌خواهد.

Sārā faghat yek tokhm-e-morgh mi-khāhad.

Sara only wants an egg.

تام می‌پرسد: «صبحانه‌ی ایرانی چیست؟»

Tām mi-porsad: «sobhāne-ye irāni chist?»

Tom asks: "What is an Iranian breakfast?"

رضا می‌گوید: «همه چیز، با یک استکان چای!»

Rezā mi-guyad: «hame chiz, bā yek estekān chāy!»

Reza says: "Everything – with a glass of tea!"

تام یک لقمه‌ی بزرگ درست می‌کند: نان، پنیر، گردو، کره، عسل و تخم‌مرغ!

Tām yek loghme-ye bozorg dorost mi-konad: nān, panir, gerdu, kare, 'asal o tokhm-e-morgh!

Tom makes a big bite: bread, cheese, walnuts, butter, honey and egg!

همه می‌خندند.

hame mi-khandand.

Everyone laughs.

سارا می‌گوید: «این صبحانه‌ی تام است!»

Sārā mi-guyad: «in sobhāne-ye Tām ast!»

Sara says: "That's Tom's breakfast!"

About this story

Bread, white cheese and walnuts – نان و پنیر و گردو – with sweet tea is the classic Iranian breakfast, and many families add butter and honey, jam, dates or fresh herbs. Breakfast is often eaten on a سفره, a cloth spread on the carpet. A لقمه is a little roll of flatbread wrapped around cheese and walnut (or anything else); Iranian mothers make لقمه for their children to take to school, and لقمه‌ی بزرگ 'a big bite' is a bit of a joke at the table. Notice the word order in رضا نان و پنیر و گردو می‌خورد: the verb comes last, and و 'and' (pronounced o) can join every item in a list.
