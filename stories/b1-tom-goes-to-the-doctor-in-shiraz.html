تام در شیراز به دکتر می‌رودTom goes to the doctor in Shiraz

Tām dar Shirāz be doktor mi-ravad

Tom, an Australian student of Persian, falls ill on a Nowruz trip to Shiraz. A guesthouse owner, a pharmacist, a doctor and an old man in a waiting room all help him on his way. Read it for the language of illness and medicine, for reported speech (گفت که اگر… باید…), and for one of the most useful polite phrases in Persian.

Intermediate (B1), ۳۱۲ کلمه (312 words)

تام برای تعطیلات نوروز به شیراز رفته بود تا آرامگاه حافظ و سعدی را ببیند.

Tām barā-ye ta'tilāt-e Nowruz be Shirāz rafte bud tā ārāmgāh-e Hāfez o Sa'di rā be-binad.

Tom had gone to Shiraz for the Nowruz holidays to see the tombs of Hafez and Saadi.

روز دوم، بعد از یک ناهار سنگین در بازار وکیل، حالش بد شد.

ruz-e dovvom, ba'd az yek nāhār-e sangin dar bāzār-e Vakil, hāl-ash bad shod.

On the second day, after a heavy lunch in the Vakil Bazaar, he fell ill.

شب تب کرد، سرش درد می‌کرد و تا صبح نتوانست بخوابد.

shab tab kard, sar-ash dard mi-kard o tā sobh na-tavānest be-khābad.

At night he ran a fever, his head ached, and he could not sleep until morning.

صبح، خانم نوری، صاحب مهمان‌پذیر، که زنی مهربان و پرحرف بود، رنگ پریده‌ی او را دید و گفت: «پسرم، تو مریضی! چرا زودتر نگفتی؟»

sobh, khānom-e Nuri, sāheb-e mehmān-pazir, ke zan-i mehrabān o por-harf bud, rang-e paride-ye u rā did o goft: «pesar-am, to mariz-i! cherā zud-tar na-gofti?»

In the morning Ms Nuri, the owner of the guesthouse, a kind and talkative woman, saw his pale face and said: "My boy, you're ill! Why didn't you say so sooner?"

او برای تام سوپ جو درست کرد و گفت که اگر تا ظهر بهتر نشود، باید پیش دکتر برود.

u barā-ye Tām sup-e jow dorost kard o goft ke agar tā zohr behtar na-shavad, bāyad pish-e doktor be-ravad.

She made Tom barley soup and said that if he was not better by noon, he must go to the doctor.

ظهر تب تام بیشتر شده بود، پس خانم نوری از پسرش خواست که تام را به داروخانه‌ی سر خیابان ببرد.

zohr tab-e Tām bish-tar shode bud, pas khānom-e Nuri az pesar-ash khāst ke Tām rā be dārukhāne-ye sar-e khiyābān be-barad.

By noon Tom's fever had gone up, so Ms Nuri asked her son to take him to the pharmacy at the end of the street.

داروساز جوان دمای بدن تام را گرفت و چند سؤال پرسید.

dārusāz-e javān damā-ye badan-e Tām rā gereft o chand so'āl porsid.

The young pharmacist took Tom's temperature and asked a few questions.

وقتی شنید که تام دیروز در بازار کباب و دوغ و فالوده خورده است، لبخند زد و گفت: «احتمالاً مسمومیت غذایی است.»

vaghti shenid ke Tām diruz dar bāzār kabāb o dugh o fālude khorde ast, labkhand zad o goft: «ehtemālan masmumiyat-e ghazāyi ast.»

When he heard that Tom had eaten kebab, doogh and faloodeh in the bazaar the day before, he smiled and said: "It's probably food poisoning."

او گفت: «برای این تب باید دکتر شما را معاینه کند؛ این قرص‌ها را فعلاً بخورید و یک ساعت دیگر به درمانگاه بروید.»

u goft: «barā-ye in tab bāyad doktor shomā rā mo'āyene konad; in ghors-hā rā fe'lan be-khorid o yek sā'at-e digar be darmāngāh be-ravid.»

He said: "With this fever a doctor should examine you; take these tablets for now, and in an hour go to the clinic."

در درمانگاه، سالن انتظار پر از آدم بود و تام نمی‌دانست کجا باید اسمش را بنویسد.

dar darmāngāh, sālon-e entezār por az ādam bud o Tām nemi-dānest kojā bāyad esm-ash rā be-nevisad.

At the clinic the waiting room was full of people, and Tom did not know where he should put his name down.

پیرمردی که کنارش نشسته بود، از لهجه‌اش فهمید که خارجی است و او را به پذیرش برد.

pir-mard-i ke kenār-ash neshaste bud, az lahje-ash fahmid ke khāreji ast o u rā be paziresh bord.

An old man sitting next to him realised from his accent that he was a foreigner and took him to reception.

دکتر بعد از معاینه گفت که مسمومیت چیز جدی‌ای نیست، اما تام باید سه روز استراحت کند و غذای سبک بخورد.

doktor ba'd az mo'āyene goft ke masmumiyat chiz-e jeddi-i nist, ammā Tām bāyad se ruz esterāhat konad o ghazā-ye sabk be-khorad.

After the examination the doctor said that the food poisoning was nothing serious, but Tom must rest for three days and eat light food.

او برایش نسخه‌ای نوشت و گفت: «اگر آب زیاد بنوشید، زود خوب می‌شوید.»

u barā-yash noskhe-i nevesht o goft: «agar āb-e ziyād be-nushid, zud khub mi-shavid.»

She wrote him a prescription and said: "If you drink plenty of water, you'll soon be well."

وقتی تام خواست پول داروها را بپردازد، دید که پیرمرد پول را قبلاً داده است.

vaghti Tām khāst pul-e dāru-hā rā be-pardāzad, did ke pir-mard pul rā ghablan dāde ast.

When Tom went to pay for the medicines, he found that the old man had already paid.

تام با تعجب گفت: «آقا، این درست نیست، من باید پول شما را پس بدهم.»

Tām bā ta'ajjob goft: «āghā, in dorost nist, man bāyad pul-e shomā rā pas be-daham.»

Tom said in surprise: "Sir, this isn't right, I must pay you back."

پیرمرد دستش را روی سینه گذاشت و گفت: «قابلی ندارد، پسرم؛ تو مهمان شیراز هستی.»

pir-mard dast-ash rā ru-ye sine gozāsht o goft: «ghābel-i na-dārad, pesar-am; to mehmān-e Shirāz hasti.»

The old man put his hand on his chest and said: "It's nothing, my son; you are a guest of Shiraz."

سه روز بعد، تام که حالش کاملاً خوب شده بود، با یک جعبه شیرینی پیش خانم نوری رفت تا از او و پسرش تشکر کند.

se ruz ba'd, Tām ke hāl-ash kāmelan khub shode bud, bā yek ja'be shirini pish-e khānom-e Nuri raft tā az u o pesar-ash tashakkor konad.

Three days later Tom, now completely well, went to Ms Nuri with a box of pastries to thank her and her son.

او در دفترچه‌اش نوشت: «در شیراز مریض شدم، اما بیشتر از دارو، مهربانی مردم مرا خوب کرد.»

u dar daftarche-ash nevesht: «dar Shirāz mariz shodam, ammā bish-tar az dāru, mehrabāni-ye mardom ma-rā khub kard.»

He wrote in his notebook: "I fell ill in Shiraz, but more than the medicine, it was people's kindness that made me well."

About this story

قابلی ندارد – literally 'it has no worth (worthy of you)' – is what shopkeepers, taxi drivers and friends say when you try to pay. Very often it is تعارف, ritual politeness, and you are expected to insist and pay; but sometimes, as with the old man here, it is meant. Iranians tell the difference by context, tone and a second or third refusal. In Iran you can ask a pharmacist (دکتر داروساز) for advice before seeing a doctor, and many medicines are sold over the counter. Grammar to notice: reported speech keeps the tense of the original words – گفت که اگر تا ظهر بهتر نشود، باید پیش دکتر برود 'she said that if he is not better by noon, he must go…' – where English shifts to 'was' and 'must'.
