روز برفی در تهرانSnow day in Tehran

ruz-e barfi dar Tehrān

Sara wakes up to a white city and the best news an Iranian schoolchild can hear: the schools are closed because of the snow. Her uncle Amir comes round, they build a snowman in the yard, and then there is a big bowl of hot ash-e reshteh waiting inside. Look for the past continuous and the past perfect, comparatives with از, and wishes with کاش + the subjunctive: کاش فردا هم برف ببارد 'I wish it would snow tomorrow too'.

Elementary (A2), ۱۷۷ کلمه (177 words)

صبح زود، مینا سارا را بیدار کرد و گفت: «سارا، بیا از پنجره بیرون را ببین!»

sobh-e zud, Minā Sārā rā bidār kard o goft: «Sārā, biyā az panjere birun rā bebin!»

Early in the morning Mina woke Sara and said: "Sara, come and look out of the window!"

همه‌جا سفید بود و برف هنوز می‌بارید.

hame-jā sefid bud o barf hanuz mi-bārid.

Everything was white, and the snow was still falling.

مینا گفت: «اخبار گفت که امروز همه‌ی مدرسه‌های تهران تعطیل هستند.»

Minā goft: «akhbār goft ke emruz hame-ye madrese-hā-ye Tehrān ta'til hastand.»

Mina said: "The news said that all the schools in Tehran are closed today."

سارا از خوشحالی بالا و پایین پرید.

Sārā az khoshhāli bālā o pāyin parid.

Sara jumped up and down with joy.

بعد از صبحانه، دایی امیر آمد.

ba'd az sobhāne, dāyi Amir āmad.

After breakfast her uncle Amir came round.

او هم سر کار نرفته بود، چون خیابان‌ها پر از برف بود.

u ham sar-e kār na-rafte bud, chon khiyābān-hā por az barf bud.

He hadn't gone to work either, because the streets were full of snow.

سارا و امیر لباس گرم پوشیدند و به حیاط رفتند.

Sārā o Amir lebās-e garm pushidand o be hayāt raftand.

Sara and Amir put on warm clothes and went out into the yard.

آن‌ها برف‌ها را جمع کردند و یک آدم‌برفی بزرگ ساختند.

ān-hā barf-hā rā jam' kardand o yek ādam-barfi-ye bozorg sākhtand.

They gathered up the snow and built a big snowman.

امیر یک هویج برای دماغش آورد و سارا شال قرمز خودش را دور گردن آدم‌برفی بست.

Amir yek havij barā-ye damāgh-ash āvard o Sārā shāl-e ghermez-e khod-ash rā dowr-e gardan-e ādam-barfi bast.

Amir brought a carrot for its nose, and Sara tied her own red scarf around the snowman's neck.

سارا گفت: «دایی، آدم‌برفی ما از آدم‌برفی همسایه‌ها قشنگ‌تر است!»

Sārā goft: «dāyi, ādam-barfi-ye mā az ādam-barfi-ye hamsāye-hā ghashang-tar ast!»

Sara said: "Uncle, our snowman is nicer than the neighbours' snowman!"

بعد برف‌بازی کردند تا وقتی که دست‌هایشان یخ کرد.

ba'd barf-bāzi kardand tā vaghti ke dast-hā-yeshān yakh kard.

Then they had a snowball fight until their hands were frozen.

وقتی به خانه برگشتند، بوی آش رشته از آشپزخانه می‌آمد.

vaghti be khāne bar-gashtand, bu-ye āsh-e reshte az āshpaz-khāne mi-āmad.

When they went back inside, the smell of ash-e reshteh was coming from the kitchen.

مینا برای هر کدام یک کاسه آش داغ با کشک و نعناداغ آورد.

Minā barā-ye har kodām yek kāse āsh-e dāgh bā kashk o na'nā-dāgh āvard.

Mina brought each of them a bowl of hot soup with kashk and fried mint.

امیر گفت: «در روز برفی، هیچ غذایی بهتر از آش رشته نیست!»

Amir goft: «dar ruz-e barfi, hich ghazā-yi behtar az āsh-e reshte nist!»

Amir said: "On a snowy day, no food is better than ash-e reshteh!"

شب، سارا از پنجره به آدم‌برفی نگاه کرد.

shab, Sārā az panjere be ādam-barfi negāh kard.

That night Sara looked out of the window at the snowman.

او گفت: «کاش فردا هم برف ببارد و مدرسه‌ها تعطیل باشند!»

u goft: «kāsh fardā ham barf bebārad o madrese-hā ta'til bāshand!»

She said: "I wish it would snow tomorrow too, and the schools would be closed!"

مینا خندید و گفت: «شاید! اما حالا باید بخوابی.»

Minā khandid o goft: «shāyad! ammā hālā bāyad bekhābi.»

Mina laughed and said: "Maybe! But now you have to go to sleep."

About this story

Tehran sits at the foot of the Alborz mountains and gets a few heavy snowfalls most winters; when schools close, the news is announced on television and radio the night before or early in the morning, and children all over the city wait for it. آش رشته, a thick soup of herbs, beans and noodles topped with کشک and نعناداغ (dried mint fried in oil), is the classic food for a cold day. Persian has a different word for each uncle and aunt: دایی is the mother's brother, عمو the father's brother, خاله the mother's sister and عمه the father's sister. Notice کاش + subjunctive for a wish (کاش برف ببارد 'I wish it would snow'), the past perfect نرفته بود 'he hadn't gone', and the comparative with از: از آدم‌برفی همسایه‌ها قشنگ‌تر 'nicer than the neighbours' snowman'.
