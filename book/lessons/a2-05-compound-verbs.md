# Compound verbs: I'll help you
## کمکت می‌کنم!
*komak-at mi-konam!* — Elementary (A2), lesson 5. [Open this lesson with audio and interactive exercises](https://zhangbing9708-maker.github.io/Learn-Farsi/course/a2-05-compound-verbs.html)

Persian has only a few hundred simple verbs. For almost everything else – to work, to phone, to decide, to fall over – it joins a noun or an adjective to one of a handful of light verbs: کردن (do), شدن (become), زدن (hit), دادن (give), گرفتن (take) and خوردن (eat). In this lesson you learn how these pairs work, where 'not' and mi- go, and thirty compound verbs that Iranians use every day.

**In this lesson you will learn to:**

- recognise and use compound verbs with کردن، شدن، زدن، دادن، گرفتن and خوردن
- put mi-, na- and be- in the right place: کار نمی‌کنم، زنگ نزدم
- tell apart pairs like تمام کردن / تمام شدن and یاد دادن / یاد گرفتن
- attach an object pronoun to the first part: کمکت می‌کنم
- talk about everyday jobs and chores

### Spring-cleaning before Nowruz

_It is a week before Nowruz. Mahsa phones her friend Leila, who is spending the day cleaning the house with her mother._

- **Mahsa:** الو، لیلا؟ سلام! چه کار می‌کنی؟ صدایت خسته است.  
  *alo, Leylā? salām! che kār mi-koni? sedā-yat khaste ast.* — Hello, Leila? Hi! What are you doing? You sound tired.  
  Spoken: الو، لیلا؟ سلام! چی کار می‌کنی؟ صدات خسته‌ست. *alo, Leylā? salām! chi kār mi-koni? sedā-t khaste-st.*
- **Leila:** سلام مهسا. داریم خانه‌تکانی می‌کنیم! از صبح کار می‌کنم.  
  *salām Mahsā. dārim khāne-tekāni mi-konim! az sobh kār mi-konam.* — Hi Mahsa. We're doing the Nowruz spring-cleaning! I've been working since the morning.  
  Spoken: سلام مهسا. داریم خونه‌تکونی می‌کنیم! از صبح دارم کار می‌کنم. *salām Mahsā. dārim khune-tekuni mi-konim! az sobh dāram kār mi-konam.*
- **Mahsa:** آهان، عید نزدیک است. چه کارهایی کرده‌اید؟  
  *āhān, eyd nazdik ast. che kār-hā-yi karde-id?* — Of course, the New Year's coming. What have you done so far?  
  Spoken: آهان، عید نزدیکه. چه کارایی کردین؟ *āhān, eyd nazdik-e. che kār-ā-yi kardin?*
- **Leila:** پنجره‌ها را تمیز کردم و فرش‌ها را جارو کردم، ولی آشپزخانه هنوز تمام نشده است.  
  *panjare-hā rā tamiz kardam o farsh-hā rā jāru kardam, vali āshpaz-khāne hanuz tamām na-shode ast.* — I've cleaned the windows and vacuumed the carpets, but the kitchen still isn't finished.  
  Spoken: پنجره‌ها رو تمیز کردم، فرشا رو جارو کردم، ولی آشپزخونه هنوز تموم نشده. *panjare-hā ro tamiz kardam, farsh-ā ro jāru kardam, vali āshpaz-khune hanuz tamum na-shode.*
- **Mahsa:** می‌خواهی بیایم کمکت کنم؟ من امروز کاری ندارم.  
  *mi-khāhi biyāyam komak-at konam? man emruz kār-i na-dāram.* — Do you want me to come and help you? I've got nothing to do today.  
  Spoken: می‌خوای بیام کمکت کنم؟ من امروز کاری ندارم. *mi-khāy biyām komak-et konam? man emruz kār-i na-dāram.*
- **Leila:** وای، دستت درد نکند! ولی زحمت نکش، تو هم حتماً کار داری.  
  *vāy, dast-at dard na-konad! vali zahmat na-kesh, to ham hatman kār dāri.* — Oh, that's so kind of you! But don't go to any trouble, you must have things to do too.  
  Spoken: وای، دستت درد نکنه! ولی زحمت نکش، تو هم حتماً کار داری. *vāy, dast-et dard na-kone! vali zahmat na-kesh, to ham hatman kār dāri.*
- **Mahsa:** نه بابا، تعارف نکن! نیم ساعت دیگر آن‌جا هستم.  
  *na bābā, ta'ārof na-kon! nim sā'at-e digar ān-jā hastam.* — Oh, come on, don't stand on ceremony! I'll be there in half an hour.  
  Spoken: نه بابا، تعارف نکن! نیم ساعت دیگه اون‌جام. *na bābā, ta'ārof na-kon! nim sā'at-e dige un-jā-m.*
- **Leila:** باشد، پس بیا! فقط سر راه نان بگیر؛ نان نداریم.  
  *bāshad, pas biyā! faghat sar-e rāh nān begir; nān na-dārim.* — OK, then come! Just pick up some bread on the way; we've got no bread.  
  Spoken: باشه، پس بیا! فقط سر راه نون بگیر؛ نون نداریم. *bāshe, pas biyā! faghat sar-e rāh nun begir; nun na-dārim.*
- **Mahsa:** چشم. راستی، دیروز به رضا زنگ زدی؟ تولدش بود.  
  *chashm. rāsti, diruz be Rezā zang zadi? tavallod-ash bud.* — Will do. By the way, did you ring Reza yesterday? It was his birthday.  
  Spoken: چشم. راستی، دیروز به رضا زنگ زدی؟ تولدش بود. *chashm. rāsti, diruz be Rezā zang zadi? tavallod-esh bud.*
- **Leila:** وای نه! یادم رفت. اصلاً به او زنگ نزدم! حتماً ناراحت شده است.  
  *vāy na! yād-am raft. aslan be u zang na-zadam! hatman nārāhat shode ast.* — Oh no! I forgot. I didn't ring him at all! He must be upset.  
  Spoken: وای نه! یادم رفت. اصلاً بهش زنگ نزدم! حتماً ناراحت شده. *vāy na! yād-am raft. aslan behesh zang na-zadam! hatman nārāhat shode.*
- **Mahsa:** نگران نباش. امروز به او پیام بده و عذرخواهی کن. رضا زود فراموش می‌کند!  
  *negarān na-bāsh. emruz be u payām bedeh o ozrkhāhi kon. Rezā zud farāmush mi-konad!* — Don't worry. Send him a message today and say sorry. Reza forgets quickly!  
  Spoken: نگران نباش. امروز بهش پیام بده و عذرخواهی کن. رضا زود فراموش می‌کنه! *negarān na-bāsh. emruz behesh payām bede o ozrkhāhi kon. Rezā zud farāmush mi-kone!*
- **Leila:** حق با توست. خب، دیگر قطع می‌کنم. زود بیا!  
  *hagh bā to-st. khob, digar ghat' mi-konam. zud biyā!* — You're right. Well, I'll hang up now. Come soon!  
  Spoken: حق با توئه. خب، دیگه قطع می‌کنم. زود بیا! *hagh bā to-e. khob, dige ghat' mi-konam. zud biyā!*

### Two-word verbs

Count the verbs in the dialogue: خانه‌تکانی می‌کنیم (*khāne-tekāni mi-konim*), کار می‌کنم (*kār mi-konam*), تمیز کردم (*tamiz kardam*), زنگ زدی (*zang zadi*), پیام بده (*payām bedeh*), تمام نشده (*tamām na-shode*)… Almost every one is made of **two words**: a noun or an adjective that carries the meaning, and a short, common verb that carries the grammar.

Persian has only about two hundred simple verbs in everyday use. Everything else is a **compound verb** (فعل مرکب (*fe'l-e morakkab*)): کار (*kār*, ‘work’) + کردن (*kardan*, ‘to do’) = *to work*; زنگ (*zang*, ‘bell’) + زدن (*zadan*, ‘to hit’) = *to ring*; تمیز (*tamiz*, ‘clean’) + کردن (*kardan*) = *to clean*. The light verb is conjugated in the usual way, and everything you have learned – *mi-*, *na-*, *be-*, the endings, the perfect – happens to it alone: کار می‌کنم (*kār mi-konam*, ‘I work’), کار نکردم (*kār na-kardam*, ‘I didn't work’), کار کرده‌ام (*kār karde-am*, ‘I have worked’).

The first word never changes. So once you know the six light verbs, you can already conjugate thousands of verbs.

### How a compound verb works

A compound verb has two parts: a **first part** – a noun or an adjective, sometimes an adverb – and a **light verb**. All the grammar goes on the light verb; the first part never changes and never takes a prefix. The table shows one compound verb, کار کردن (*kār kardan*, ‘to work’), in all the tenses you know.

In the subjunctive and the imperative, compounds with کردن (*kardan*) and شدن (*shodan*) normally drop *be-*: کار کن! (*kār kon!*, ‘work!’), بیدار شو! (*bidār sho!*, ‘wake up!’). The other light verbs keep it: زنگ بزن! (*zang bezan!*, ‘ring!’), گوش بده! (*gush bedeh!*, ‘listen!’), تصمیم بگیر! (*tasmim begir!*, ‘decide!’).

An object pronoun goes on the **first** part: کمکت می‌کنم (*komak-at mi-konam*, ‘I'll help you’), صدایش کردم (*sedā-yash kardam*, ‘I called him’). A direct object comes before the whole verb: در را باز کن (*dar rā bāz kon*, ‘open the door’).

| Form | Persian | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| present | کار می‌کنم (*kār mi-konam*) | I work, I'm working |
| present, negative | کار نمی‌کنم (*kār nemi-konam*) | I don't work |
| simple past | کار کردم (*kār kardam*) | I worked |
| simple past, negative | کار نکردم (*kār na-kardam*) | I didn't work |
| past continuous | کار می‌کردم (*kār mi-kardam*) | I was working, I used to work |
| present perfect | کار کرده‌ام (*kār karde-am*) | I have worked |
| subjunctive | کار کنم (*kār konam*) | (that) I work |
| imperative | کار کن! (*kār kon!*) | work! |
| negative imperative | کار نکن! (*kār na-kon!*) | don't work! |

### Six verbs do most of the work

Each light verb has a feel that helps you guess and remember:

- کردن (*kardan*, ‘do, make’) – by far the most common; with an adjective it means *make something …*: تمیز کردن (*tamiz kardan*, ‘make clean’), باز کردن (*bāz kardan*, ‘make open’).
- شدن (*shodan*, ‘become’) – a change that *happens*: تمیز شدن (*tamiz shodan*, ‘get clean’), باز شدن (*bāz shodan*, ‘come open’).
- زدن (*zadan*, ‘hit’) – sounds and quick actions: زنگ زدن (*zang zadan*), حرف زدن (*harf zadan*).
- دادن (*dādan*, ‘give’) – something goes out from you: یاد دادن (*yād dādan*, ‘teach’), جواب دادن (*javāb dādan*, ‘answer’).
- گرفتن (*gereftan*, ‘take, get’) – something comes to you: یاد گرفتن (*yād gereftan*, ‘learn’), دوش گرفتن (*dush gereftan*, ‘take a shower’).
- خوردن (*khordan*, ‘eat’) – something happens *to* you: زمین خوردن (*zamin khordan*, ‘fall over’), سرما خوردن (*sarmā khordan*, ‘catch a cold’).

### Not, mi- and the pronoun: what goes where

Because the first part never changes, all the small pieces you know go **inside** the compound – on the light verb:

- the negative: زنگ نزدم (*zang na-zadam*, ‘I didn't ring’), گوش نده (*gush na-deh*, ‘don't listen’);
- *mi-* and *nemi-*: جواب نمی‌دهی (*javāb nemi-dahi*, ‘you don't answer’);
- the perfect: تصمیم نگرفته‌ام (*tasmim na-gerefte-am*, ‘I haven't decided’);
- the *be-* of the subjunctive: باید زنگ بزنم (*bāyad zang bezanam*, ‘I must ring’).

The object pronoun, on the other hand, sits on the first word: کمکت می‌کنم (*komak-at mi-konam*, ‘I'll help you’), صدایش کردم (*sedā-yash kardam*, ‘I called her’). You can always say the same with به (*be*) + pronoun instead: به تو کمک می‌کنم (*be to komak mi-konam*). Words like خیلی (*kheyli*, ‘a lot’) and زود (*zud*, ‘early’) go in front of the whole compound: خیلی فکر کردم (*kheyli fekr kardam*, ‘I thought a lot’).

| Persian | English | What to notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| کار نمی‌کنم (*kār nemi-konam*) | I don't work | nemi- on the light verb |
| زنگ نزدم (*zang na-zadam*) | I didn't ring | na- on the light verb |
| دوش نگرفته‌ام (*dush na-gerefte-am*) | I haven't had a shower | the perfect, negative |
| گوش نده! (*gush na-deh!*) | don't listen! | negative imperative |
| باید تصمیم بگیری (*bāyad tasmim begiri*) | you have to decide | be- kept with gereftan |
| باید کار کنی (*bāyad kār koni*) | you have to work | no be- with kardan |
| کمکت می‌کنم (*komak-at mi-konam*) | I'll help you | pronoun on the first part |
| به تو کمک می‌کنم (*be to komak mi-konam*) | I'll help you | or be + pronoun |
| خیلی فکر کردم (*kheyli fekr kardam*) | I thought a lot | adverb before the whole verb |

  *emruz aslan televiziyon negāh na-kardam.* — I didn't watch TV at all today.
- چرا جواب نمی‌دهی؟  
  *cherā javāb nemi-dahi?* — Why aren't you answering?  
  Spoken: چرا جواب نمی‌دی؟ *cherā javāb nemi-di?*
- فردا به تو زنگ می‌زنم.  
  *fardā be to zang mi-zanam.* — I'll ring you tomorrow.  
  Spoken: فردا بهت زنگ می‌زنم. *fardā behet zang mi-zanam.*
- هنوز تصمیم نگرفته‌ام.  
  *hanuz tasmim na-gerefte-am.* — I haven't decided yet.  
  Spoken: هنوز تصمیم نگرفتم. *hanuz tasmim na-gereftam.*
- لطفاً این‌جا عکس نگیرید.  
  *lotfan in-jā aks na-girid.* — Please don't take photos here.

### Reza's Saturday

_Saturday is the first working day of the Iranian week. Read about Reza's Saturday and pick out the compound verbs – there are more than fifteen._

- شنبه اولین روز هفته در ایران است.  
  *shanbe avvalin ruz-e hafte dar Irān ast.* — Saturday is the first day of the week in Iran.
- رضا ساعت شش و نیم بیدار می‌شود و دوش می‌گیرد.  
  *Rezā sā'at-e shesh o nim bidār mi-shavad o dush mi-girad.* — Reza wakes up at half past six and has a shower.
- بعد صبحانه می‌خورد و با مترو به سر کار می‌رود.  
  *ba'd sobhāne mi-khorad o bā metro be sar-e kār mi-ravad.* — Then he has breakfast and goes to work by metro.
- در ایستگاه سوار مترو می‌شود و در میدان ولیعصر پیاده می‌شود.  
  *dar istgāh savār-e metro mi-shavad o dar meydān-e Vali-asr piyāde mi-shavad.* — At the station he gets on the metro, and he gets off at Valiasr Square.
- رضا در یک شرکت کامپیوتری کار می‌کند.  
  *Rezā dar yek sherkat-e kāmpiyuteri kār mi-konad.* — Reza works for a computer company.
- صبح‌ها به ایمیل‌ها جواب می‌دهد و با همکارانش صحبت می‌کند.  
  *sobh-hā be imeyl-hā javāb mi-dahad o bā ham-kārān-ash sohbat mi-konad.* — In the mornings he answers emails and talks with his colleagues.
- ظهر با دوستش امیر در یک رستوران کوچک ناهار می‌خورد.  
  *zohr bā dust-ash Amir dar yek resturān-e kuchak nāhār mi-khorad.* — At noon he has lunch with his friend Amir in a small restaurant.
- بعد از ظهر جلسه دارد و باید گزارشش را تمام کند.  
  *ba'd az zohr jalase dārad o bāyad gozāresh-ash rā tamām konad.* — In the afternoon he has a meeting and has to finish his report.
- ساعت شش کارش تمام می‌شود و با تاکسی به خانه برمی‌گردد.  
  *sā'at-e shesh kār-ash tamām mi-shavad o bā tāksi be khāne bar-mi-gardad.* — At six his work is over and he goes home by taxi.
- شب‌ها معمولاً به مادرش زنگ می‌زند و کمی تلویزیون تماشا می‌کند.  
  *shab-hā ma'mulan be mādar-ash zang mi-zanad o kam-i televiziyon tamāshā mi-konad.* — In the evenings he usually rings his mother and watches a bit of TV.
- ولی امشب خیلی خسته شده و زود می‌خوابد.  
  *vali emshab kheyli khaste shode o zud mi-khābad.* — But tonight he's very tired, so he goes to bed early.

**Choose the right answer**

1. How does Reza get to work? — by car / by metro / on foot
2. What does he do at work in the mornings? — he answers emails and talks with colleagues / he has meetings / he goes for a walk
3. What does he have to do in the afternoon? — ring his mother / finish his report / buy bread
4. Which verb means 'to get off' (a bus, the metro)? — سوار شدن / پیاده شدن / بیدار شدن

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. by metro
2. he answers emails and talks with colleagues
3. finish his report
4. پیاده شدن

</details>

### Shaking the house

In the last weeks of winter, before نوروز (*nowruz*, ‘the Persian New Year’) at the spring equinox around 21 March, Iranian homes turn upside down. خانه‌تکانی (*khāne-tekāni*) – literally *house-shaking* – is much more than a spring-clean: carpets go to the carpet-washer, curtains come down, every cupboard is emptied, windows are polished and old things are given away. The idea is simple: nothing old and dusty should follow you into the new year.

It is a family job, and the verbs of this lesson fill the house: فرش‌ها را به قالیشویی بدهیم (*farsh-hā rā be ghālishuyi bedahim*, ‘let's send the carpets to the carpet-washer’), پرده‌ها را بشوییم (*parde-hā rā beshuyim*, ‘let's wash the curtains’) and, to anyone who is working hard, خسته نباشید! (*khaste na-bāshid!*, ‘may you not be tired!’). Meanwhile someone sets lentils or wheat to sprout for the سبزه (*sabze*) of the haft-sin table, and the children wait for their new clothes for the New Year.

### Tips

- Learn the light verb together with the first word – you can't swap them. *zang zadan* is 'to ring', but *zang kardan* means nothing.
- Many compounds come in pairs: with **کردن** someone does it, with **شدن** it happens by itself. *dar rā bāz kardam* 'I opened the door' – *dar bāz shod* 'the door opened'; *kār-am rā tamām kardam* 'I finished my work' – *kelās tamām shod* 'the class ended'.
- **یاد دادن** (give memory) is *to teach*, **یاد گرفتن** (take memory) is *to learn* – picture the knowledge going out or coming in.
- New verbs are still made this way: *chat kardan* 'to chat', *lāyk kardan* 'to like (a post)', *dānlod kardan* 'to download'. Any new word + کردن becomes a Persian verb.

4. پیاده شدن — ?
5. زمین خوردن — ?
6. تصمیم گرفتن — ?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. یاد گرفتن = to learn
2. یاد دادن = to teach
3. سوار شدن = to get on
4. پیاده شدن = to get off

</details>

### Choose the right form

**Choose the right answer**

1. 'I don't work on Fridays.' — جمعه‌ها نمی‌کار کنم. / جمعه‌ها کار نمی‌کنم. / جمعه‌ها کار نکردم.
2. Which light verb goes with زنگ (to phone)? — کردن / دادن / زدن
3. 'I learned Persian.' — فارسی یاد دادم. / فارسی یاد گرفتم. / فارسی یاد کردم.
4. 'The film finished at ten.' — فیلم ساعت ده تمام شد. / فیلم ساعت ده تمام کرد. / فیلم ساعت ده تمام زد.
5. 'I haven't decided yet.' — هنوز تصمیم نکرده‌ام. / هنوز تصمیم نداده‌ام. / هنوز تصمیم نگرفته‌ام.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. جمعه‌ها کار نمی‌کنم. — nemi- goes on the light verb: kār nemi-konam.
2. زدن — zang zadan – literally 'to hit the bell'.
3. فارسی یاد گرفتم. — yād gereftan (take memory) is 'to learn'; yād dādan (give memory) is 'to teach'.
4. فیلم ساعت ده تمام شد. — Something that ends by itself takes shodan: tamām shod.
5. هنوز تصمیم نگرفته‌ام. — tasmim goes with gereftan, and na- goes on the light verb.

</details>

### Complete the compound verbs

**Fill in the missing word**

1. دیروز به مادرم زنگ ___. (*diruz be mādar-am zang ___.*) — Yesterday I didn't ring my mother. (hint: zadan, I, past, negative)
2. صبح‌ها ساعت هفت ___ می‌شوم. (*sobh-hā sā'at-e haft ___ mi-shavam.*) — In the mornings I wake up at seven. (hint: awake)
