# Academic Persian: how scholars write
## فارسی دانشگاهی
*fārsi-ye dāneshgāhi* — Advanced (C1), lesson 1. [Open this lesson with audio and interactive exercises](https://zhangbing9708-maker.github.io/Learn-Farsi/course/c1-01-academic-persian.html)

Persian scholarly prose has its own sound: long chains of nouns, verbs that hide the person doing the work, careful phrases that soften every claim, and a vocabulary in which Arabic loans and twentieth-century Persian coinages sit side by side. In this lesson you read about Persian as a language of science, learn the habits of the academic style – and when to resist them – and then read a model abstract sentence by sentence, the way a supervisor would read it with you.

**In this lesson you will learn to:**

- turn verbs into nouns the way academic Persian does (بررسی، تحلیل، ارزیابی) and turn them back into clear English
- recognise the passive and impersonal patterns of scholarly prose and choose an active alternative when it reads better
- hedge a claim and cite a source with به نظر می‌رسد، چنان‌که … اشاره کرده است، بر اساس یافته‌ها، از این رو
- tell Arabic loans from Persian coinages in academic vocabulary (تحقیق/پژوهش، دانشگاه، رایانه)
- read and write the standard moves of a Persian research abstract

### Persian, a language of science

_A short essay in the register of a university textbook. Read it once for the argument, then a second time for the style: count the nouns ending in ـی and ـش, notice how rarely a person is the subject of a verb, and watch the linking phrases (با این حال، از این رو، با این همه) that carry the reader from one claim to the next._

- زبان فارسی بیش از هزار سال است که زبان شعر و ادب بوده است، اما کمتر به این نکته توجه می‌شود که این زبان زبان علم نیز بوده است.  
  *zabān-e fārsi bish az hezār sāl ast ke zabān-e she'r o adab bude ast, ammā kam-tar be in nokte tavajjoh mi-shavad ke in zabān zabān-e elm niz bude ast.* — Persian has been a language of poetry and letters for more than a thousand years, but less attention is paid to the fact that it has also been a language of science.
- در سده‌های چهارم و پنجم هجری، دانشمندانی چون ابن‌سینا و بیرونی بخشی از آثار خود را به فارسی نوشتند.  
  *dar sade-hā-ye chahārom o panjom-e hejri, dāneshmandān-i chon Ebn-e-Sinā o Biruni bakhsh-i az āsār-e khod rā be fārsi neveshtand.* — In the fourth and fifth centuries of the Hijra (the 10th and 11th centuries AD), scholars such as Avicenna and Biruni wrote some of their works in Persian.
- دانشنامه‌ی علائی، اثر ابن‌سینا، از نخستین کتاب‌های فلسفی به زبان فارسی است.  
  *dāneshnāme-ye Alā'i, asar-e Ebn-e-Sinā, az nakhostin ketāb-hā-ye falsafi be zabān-e fārsi ast.* — The Dāneshnāme-ye Alā'i, a work by Avicenna, is one of the first books of philosophy in the Persian language.
- با این حال، در قرن‌های بعد زبان عربی همچنان زبان اصلی علم در جهان اسلام باقی ماند.  
  *bā in hāl, dar gharn-hā-ye ba'd zabān-e arabi hamchenān zabān-e asli-ye elm dar jahān-e eslām bāghi mānd.* — Even so, in the centuries that followed Arabic remained the main language of science in the Islamic world.
- از این رو، بخش بزرگی از واژگان علمی فارسی امروز ریشه‌ی عربی دارد.  
  *az in ru, bakhsh-e bozorg-i az vāzhegān-e elmi-ye fārsi-ye emruz rishe-ye arabi dārad.* — For this reason, a large part of the scientific vocabulary of today's Persian has Arabic roots.
- در سده‌ی چهاردهم خورشیدی، با تأسیس دانشگاه تهران در سال ۱۳۱۳، نیاز به واژه‌های تازه بیش از پیش احساس شد.  
  *dar sade-ye chahārdahom-e khorshidi, bā ta'sis-e dāneshgāh-e Tehrān dar sāl-e hezār-o-sisad-o-sizdah, niyāz be vāzhe-hā-ye tāze bish az pish ehsās shod.* — In the fourteenth century of the solar calendar (the 20th century AD), with the founding of the University of Tehran in 1313 (1934), the need for new words was felt more than ever.
- «فرهنگستان ایران» در سال ۱۳۱۴ تأسیس شد تا برای مفاهیم تازه معادل فارسی بیابد.  
  *«farhangestān-e Irān» dar sāl-e hezār-o-sisad-o-chahārdah ta'sis shod tā barā-ye mafāhim-e tāze mo'ādel-e fārsi biyābad.* — The Academy of Iran was founded in 1314 (1935) to find Persian equivalents for new concepts.
- واژه‌های «هواپیما»، «شهرداری» و «دادگستری» در همین دوره جای «طیاره»، «بلدیه» و «عدلیه» را گرفتند.  
  *vāzhe-hā-ye «havāpeymā», «shahrdāri» o «dādgostari» dar hamin dowre jā-ye «tayyāre», «baladiye» o «adliye» rā gereftand.* — In this same period the words havāpeymā (aeroplane), shahrdāri (municipality) and dādgostari (the justice system) took the place of the Arabic tayyāre, baladiye and adliye.
- با این همه، بسیاری از واژه‌های عربی‌تبار، مانند «تحقیق» و «علم»، همچنان در کنار معادل‌های فارسی به کار می‌روند.  
  *bā in hame, besyār-i az vāzhe-hā-ye arabi-tabār, mānand-e «tahghigh» o «elm», hamchenān dar kenār-e mo'ādel-hā-ye fārsi be kār mi-ravand.* — Even so, many words of Arabic origin, such as tahghigh (research) and elm (science), are still used alongside their Persian equivalents.
- امروزه پژوهشگر ایرانی می‌تواند میان «تحقیق» و «پژوهش» یکی را برگزیند، و این انتخاب خود نشانه‌ای از سبک نوشتار اوست.  
  *emruze pazhuheshgar-e Irāni mi-tavānad miyān-e «tahghigh» o «pazhuhesh» yek-i rā bargozinad, va in entekhāb khod neshāne-i az sabk-e neveshtār-e ust.* — Today an Iranian researcher can choose between tahghigh and pazhuhesh, and the choice is itself a sign of his or her style of writing.
- نثر دانشگاهی فارسی ویژگی‌های آشکاری دارد که خواننده آن‌ها را زود می‌شناسد.  
  *nasr-e dāneshgāhi-ye fārsi vizhegi-hā-ye āshkār-i dārad ke khānande ān-hā rā zud mi-shenāsad.* — Persian academic prose has clear features that a reader soon recognises.
- نخست، نویسنده به جای فعل، از اسم استفاده می‌کند: به جای «داده‌ها را بررسی کردیم» می‌نویسد «بررسی داده‌ها انجام شد».  
  *nakhost, nevisande be jā-ye fe'l, az esm estefāde mi-konad: be jā-ye «dāde-hā rā barrasi kardim» mi-nevisad «barrasi-ye dāde-hā anjām shod».* — First, the writer uses nouns instead of verbs: instead of 'we examined the data' they write 'an examination of the data was carried out'.
- دوم، فاعل اغلب پنهان می‌ماند و جمله‌ها با فعل‌های مجهول یا غیرشخصی ساخته می‌شوند.  
  *dovvom, fā'el aghlab penhān mi-mānad o jomle-hā bā fe'l-hā-ye majhul yā gheyr-e-shakhsi sākhte mi-shavand.* — Second, the doer often stays hidden, and sentences are built with passive or impersonal verbs.
- سوم، نویسنده با احتیاط سخن می‌گوید و ادعاهای خود را با عبارت‌هایی چون «به نظر می‌رسد» نرم می‌کند.  
  *sevvom, nevisande bā ehtiyāt sokhan mi-guyad o edde'ā-hā-ye khod rā bā ebārat-hā-yi chon «be nazar mi-resad» narm mi-konad.* — Third, the writer speaks with caution and softens their claims with phrases such as 'it seems'.
- چهارم، هر سخنی که از دیگران گرفته شده باشد، با ذکر منبع همراه است.  
  *chahārom, har sokhan-i ke az digarān gerefte shode bāshad, bā zekr-e manba' hamrāh ast.* — Fourth, anything taken from others comes with a mention of its source.
- این ویژگی‌ها نثر علمی را دقیق می‌کنند، اما اگر بیش از اندازه به کار روند، متن را سنگین و دیریاب می‌سازند.  
  *in vizhegi-hā nasr-e elmi rā daghigh mi-konand, ammā agar bish az andāze be kār ravand, matn rā sangin o diryāb mi-sāzand.* — These features make scholarly prose precise, but used to excess they make a text heavy and hard to get into.
- از همین رو، بسیاری از استادان به دانشجویان توصیه می‌کنند که تا جای ممکن جمله‌های کوتاه و فعل‌های معلوم به کار ببرند.  
  *az hamin ru, besyār-i az ostādān be dāneshjuyān towsiye mi-konand ke tā jā-ye momken jomle-hā-ye kutāh o fe'l-hā-ye ma'lum be kār bebarand.* — For this very reason many professors advise their students to use short sentences and active verbs wherever they can.
- نوشتن به فارسی دانشگاهی، در واقع، هنر یافتن تعادل میان دقت و روشنی است.  
  *neveshtan be fārsi-ye dāneshgāhi, dar vāghe', honar-e yāftan-e ta'ādol miyān-e deghghat o roshani ast.* — Writing academic Persian is, in fact, the art of finding a balance between precision and clarity.

### Four habits of the Persian scholar

The essay names the four habits you will meet in every Persian thesis, journal article and textbook – and this lesson takes them one at a time.

1. **Nominalisation** (اسم‌سازی (*esm-sāzi*)): the action becomes a noun, and a colourless verb such as انجام شدن (*anjām shodan*, ‘to be carried out’) or صورت گرفتن (*surat gereftan*, ‘to take place’) is added to carry the tense. *We examined the data* becomes بررسی داده‌ها انجام شد (*barrasi-ye dāde-hā anjām shod*, ‘an examination of the data was carried out’).
2. **The hidden doer**: passives with شدن, impersonal phrases (می‌توان گفت (*mi-tavān goft*, ‘one can say’)) and the famous مورد بررسی قرار گرفتن (*mowred-e barrasi gharār gereftan*, ‘to be made the object of study’).
3. **Hedging**: claims arrive wrapped in caution – به نظر می‌رسد (*be nazar mi-resad*, ‘it seems’), احتمالاً (*ehtemālan*, ‘probably’), تا حدی (*tā hadd-i*, ‘to some extent’).
4. **Citing**: چنان‌که … اشاره کرده است (*chenān-ke … eshāre karde ast*, ‘as … has pointed out’), به گفته‌ی (*be gofte-ye*, ‘according to’), بر اساس (*bar asās-e*, ‘on the basis of’).

The essay's last sentences carry the most useful advice. Persian editors and style manuals have argued for decades that academic prose overuses these habits, and many university style guides now ask for *active verbs, short sentences and a visible author*. So learn the habits in order to read – and then use them with a light hand when you write. When you translate academic Persian into English, the rule of thumb is simple: **turn the nouns back into verbs**.

### Nominalisation: saying it with nouns

Academic Persian likes to turn an action into a noun and let a weak verb carry the tense. Most of the nouns are simply the first half of a compound verb – بررسی کردن (*barrasi kardan*) gives بررسی (*barrasi*) – or a noun in ـش or ـی made from a verb stem (افزایش (*afzāyesh*, ‘increase’), یادگیری (*yādgiri*, ‘learning’)).

The noun then joins its object with an ezafe, and long **ezafe chains** grow: ارزیابی تأثیر تکرار فاصله‌دار (*arzyābi-ye ta'sir-e tekrār-e fāsele-dār*, ‘evaluation of the effect of spaced repetition’). The verbs that carry these nouns are few and you should know them all: انجام شدن / دادن (*anjām shodan / dādan*, ‘to be carried out / carry out’), صورت گرفتن (*surat gereftan*, ‘to take place’), به عمل آمدن (*be amal āmadan*, ‘to be done’), مورد بررسی قرار گرفتن (*mowred-e barrasi gharār gereftan*, ‘to be examined’), انجامیدن (به) (*anjāmidan (be)*, ‘to lead (to)’).

When you translate into English, undo the process: *an evaluation of the effect … was carried out* is almost always better as *we evaluated the effect …* or *the effect … was evaluated*.

### Hiding the doer – and when not to

Scholarly Persian keeps the researcher out of sight. You already know the شدن passive (B1); here are the other tools, from the most neutral to the most criticised.

- **The passive** with شدن: داده‌ها گردآوری شدند (*dāde-hā gerdāvari shodand*, ‘the data were collected’). Natural and neutral in writing.
- **The impersonal modal** with the short infinitive: می‌توان نتیجه گرفت (*mi-tavān natije gereft*, ‘one can conclude’), باید توجه داشت (*bāyad tavajjoh dāsht*, ‘it should be noted’). No person, no ending: the past stem alone follows.
- **The study as subject**: این پژوهش نشان می‌دهد (*in pazhuhesh neshān mi-dahad*, ‘this study shows’) – active, yet impersonal. Often the best choice.
- **The writer in the third person**: نگارنده (*negārande*, ‘the present writer’) or نگارندگان (*negārandegān*, ‘the authors’), the traditional modest 'I'.
- **The plain 'we'**: ما (*mā*) with an active verb – once frowned on, now common, especially in the sciences.

Two patterns are grammatical but widely criticised by Persian editors: توسط (*tavassot-e*, ‘by’) + passive (a calque of English *by*: Persian prefers to make the agent the subject), and the heavy مورد … قرار گرفتن (*mowred-e … gharār gereftan*) where a simple passive or active verb would do.

| Pattern | Example | English | Register |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| passive with شدن | داده‌ها گردآوری شدند (*dāde-hā gerdāvari shodand*) | the data were collected | neutral, written |
| می‌توان + short infinitive | می‌توان نتیجه گرفت (*mi-tavān natije gereft*) | one can conclude | formal |
| باید + short infinitive | باید توجه داشت (*bāyad tavajjoh dāsht*) | it should be noted | formal |
| پیشنهاد می‌شود | پیشنهاد می‌شود که … (*pishnahād mi-shavad ke …*) | it is recommended that … | formal |
| the study as subject | این پژوهش نشان می‌دهد (*in pazhuhesh neshān mi-dahad*) | this study shows | recommended |
| the author in the third person | نگارنده بر این باور است (*negārande bar in bāvar ast*) | the present writer believes | traditional, modest |
| active 'we' | ما نشان می‌دهیم (*mā neshān mi-dahim*) | we show | modern, direct |
| توسط + passive | توسط شرکت‌کنندگان تکمیل شد (*tavassot-e sherkat-konandegān takmil shod*) | was completed by the participants | criticised – make the agent the subject |
| مورد … قرار گرفتن | مورد بررسی قرار گرفت (*mowred-e barrasi gharār gereft*) | was examined | heavy – use sparingly |

- داده‌ها از طریق پرسش‌نامه گردآوری شدند.  
  *dāde-hā az tarigh-e porsesh-nāme gerdāvari shodand.* — The data were collected by means of a questionnaire.
- پرسش‌نامه توسط شرکت‌کنندگان تکمیل شد.  
  *porsesh-nāme tavassot-e sherkat-konandegān takmil shod.* — The questionnaire was completed by the participants.  
  _Grammatical, but an editor would probably change it to the next sentence._
- شرکت‌کنندگان پرسش‌نامه را تکمیل کردند.  
  *sherkat-konandegān porsesh-nāme rā takmil kardand.* — The participants completed the questionnaire.
- می‌توان نتیجه گرفت که انگیزه نقش مهمی دارد.  
  *mi-tavān natije gereft ke angize naghsh-e mohemm-i dārad.* — One can conclude that motivation plays an important role.
- باید توجه داشت که حجم نمونه کوچک بود.  
  *bāyad tavajjoh dāsht ke hajm-e nemune kuchek bud.* — It should be noted that the sample was small.
- نگارنده در این مقاله سه دلیل برای این پدیده پیشنهاد می‌کند.  
  *negārande dar in maghāle se dalil barā-ye in padide pishnahād mi-konad.* — In this article I propose three reasons for this phenomenon.  
  Literally: the writer in this article proposes three reasons for this phenomenon
- در این پژوهش، ما دو روش آموزش واژگان را با هم مقایسه کردیم.  
  *dar in pazhuhesh, mā do ravesh-e āmuzesh-e vāzhegān rā bā ham moghāyese kardim.* — In this study we compared two methods of teaching vocabulary.
- این پژوهش نشان می‌دهد که تکرار فاصله‌دار به یادسپاری کمک می‌کند.  
  *in pazhuhesh neshān mi-dahad ke tekrār-e fāsele-dār be yād-sepāri komak mi-konad.* — This study shows that spaced repetition helps memorisation.

### Hedging, citing and linking

A Persian scholar rarely says *X is true*. The claim is softened (**hedged**), attributed to someone (**cited**), and tied to what came before (**linked**).

**Hedging.** به نظر می‌رسد که (*be nazar mi-resad ke*, ‘it seems that’) is the workhorse. Follow it with the indicative for a mild hedge and with the **subjunctive** for a stronger one: به نظر می‌رسد که مؤثر است (*be nazar mi-resad ke mo'asser ast*, ‘it appears to be effective’) vs به نظر می‌رسد که مؤثر باشد (*be nazar mi-resad ke mo'asser bāshad*, ‘it may well be effective’).

**Citing.** The classic frame is چنان‌که (*chenān-ke*) + name + (year) + اشاره کرده است (*eshāre karde ast*) – the present perfect, because the source still speaks. Iranian journals give years in the solar calendar, so Karimi (۱۳۹۸) published in 2019–20.

**Linking.** از این رو (*az in ru*) and بنابراین (*banābar-in*) (*hence, therefore*), با این حال (*bā in hāl*) (*however*), در مجموع (*dar majmu'*, ‘all in all’) steer the reader. Put a Persian comma after them.

| Phrase | Reading | English | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| به نظر می‌رسد (که) | be nazar mi-resad (ke) | it seems (that) | hedge |
| احتمالاً | ehtemālan | probably | hedge |
| تا حدی | tā hadd-i | to some extent | hedge |
| چنان‌که … اشاره کرده است | chenān-ke … eshāre karde ast | as … has pointed out | cite |
| به گفته‌ی … | be gofte-ye … | according to … (a person) | cite |
| بر اساس یافته‌ها | bar asās-e yāfte-hā | on the basis of the findings | cite your own data |
| از این رو / بنابراین | az in ru / banābar-in | hence / therefore | link: result |
| با این حال | bā in hāl | however, nevertheless | link: contrast |
| بدین منظور | bedin manzur | to this end | link: purpose |
| در مجموع | dar majmu' | all in all, overall | link: summary |

- به نظر می‌رسد که سن آغاز یادگیری عامل تعیین‌کننده‌ای نباشد.  
  *be nazar mi-resad ke senn-e āghāz-e yādgiri āmel-e ta'yin-konande-i na-bāshad.* — It would seem that the age at which learning starts is not a decisive factor.  
  _The subjunctive نباشد makes the hedge stronger than نیست would._
- چنان‌که کریمی (۱۳۹۸) اشاره کرده است، انگیزه با گذشت زمان تغییر می‌کند.  
  *chenān-ke Karimi (hezār-o-sisad-o-navad-o-hasht) eshāre karde ast, angize bā gozasht-e zamān taghyir mi-konad.* — As Karimi (1398/2019) has pointed out, motivation changes over time.
- بر اساس یافته‌های این پژوهش، تمرین روزانه از تمرین فشرده مؤثرتر است.  
  *bar asās-e yāfte-hā-ye in pazhuhesh, tamrin-e ruzāne az tamrin-e feshorde mo'asser-tar ast.* — On the basis of this study's findings, daily practice is more effective than intensive practice.
- به گفته‌ی احمدی، بیشتر زبان‌آموزان از اشتباه کردن می‌ترسند.  
  *be gofte-ye Ahmadi, bish-tar-e zabān-āmuzān az eshtebāh kardan mi-tarsand.* — According to Ahmadi, most language learners are afraid of making mistakes.
- حجم نمونه کوچک بود؛ از این رو، نتایج را باید با احتیاط تعمیم داد.  
  *hajm-e nemune kuchek bud; az in ru, natāyej rā bāyad bā ehtiyāt ta'mim dād.* — The sample was small; the results should therefore be generalised with caution.
- این یافته‌ها تا حدی با نتایج پژوهش‌های پیشین همخوانی دارند.  
  *in yāfte-hā tā hadd-i bā natāyej-e pazhuhesh-hā-ye pishin hamkhāni dārand.* — These findings are to some extent consistent with the results of earlier studies.
- در مجموع، می‌توان گفت که فرضیه‌ی نخست تأیید شد.  
  *dar majmu', mi-tavān goft ke farziye-ye nakhost ta'yid shod.* — Overall, it can be said that the first hypothesis was confirmed.

### A model abstract, sentence by sentence

_Here is the abstract (چکیده) of an imaginary study, written the way a Persian journal would expect. Persian abstracts follow the same moves as English ones – territory, gap, aim, method, results, interpretation, implications, limitations – and each move has its favourite phrases. The note under each sentence names the move and the device._

- تأثیر تکرار فاصله‌دار بر یادگیری واژگان فارسی در میان زبان‌آموزان بزرگسال  
  *ta'sir-e tekrār-e fāsele-dār bar yādgiri-ye vāzhegān-e fārsi dar miyān-e zabān-āmuzān-e bozorgsāl* — The effect of spaced repetition on the learning of Persian vocabulary among adult learners  
  _The title: one noun phrase, an ezafe chain of six links and no verb – typical of Persian titles._
- یادگیری واژگان یکی از دشوارترین بخش‌های فراگیری زبان دوم به شمار می‌رود.  
  *yādgiri-ye vāzhegān yek-i az doshvār-tarin bakhsh-hā-ye farāgiri-ye zabān-e dovvom be shomār mi-ravad.* — Vocabulary learning is regarded as one of the hardest parts of acquiring a second language.  
  _Move 1, the territory. به شمار رفتن 'to be counted as' is a favourite academic verb._
- پژوهش‌های پیشین نشان داده‌اند که مرور واژه‌ها در فاصله‌های زمانی رو به افزایش، به یادسپاری بهتر می‌انجامد.  
  *pazhuhesh-hā-ye pishin neshān dāde-and ke morur-e vāzhe-hā dar fāsele-hā-ye zamāni-ye ru be afzāyesh, be yād-sepāri-ye behtar mi-anjāmad.* — Earlier research has shown that reviewing words at increasing intervals leads to better retention.  
  _Move 2, what is known. The present perfect (نشان داده‌اند) for the literature, as in English._
- با این حال، تأثیر این روش بر زبان‌آموزان فارسی کمتر بررسی شده است.  
  *bā in hāl, ta'sir-e in ravesh bar zabān-āmuzān-e fārsi kam-tar barrasi shode ast.* — However, the effect of this method on learners of Persian has received less attention.  
  _Move 3, the gap, opened with با این حال._
- هدف پژوهش حاضر، ارزیابی تأثیر تکرار فاصله‌دار بر یادگیری و یادسپاری واژگان فارسی است.  
  *hadaf-e pazhuhesh-e hāzer, arzyābi-ye ta'sir-e tekrār-e fāsele-dār bar yādgiri o yād-sepāri-ye vāzhegān-e fārsi ast.* — The aim of the present study is to evaluate the effect of spaced repetition on the learning and retention of Persian vocabulary.  
  _Move 4, the aim. پژوهش حاضر 'the present study'; the aim is a noun (ارزیابی), not a verb._
- بدین منظور، شصت زبان‌آموز بزرگسال از سه کشور به‌طور تصادفی در دو گروه قرار گرفتند.  
  *bedin manzur, shast zabān-āmuz-e bozorgsāl az se keshvar be-towr-e tasādofi dar do goruh gharār gereftand.* — To this end, sixty adult learners from three countries were randomly assigned to two groups.  
  _Move 5, method. قرار گرفتن 'to be placed' works as a passive without شدن._
- گروه نخست واژه‌ها را با برنامه‌ی تکرار فاصله‌دار و گروه دوم با روش سنتی فهرست‌خوانی مرور کرد.  
  *goruh-e nakhost vāzhe-hā rā bā barnāme-ye tekrār-e fāsele-dār o goruh-e dovvom bā ravesh-e sonnati-ye fehrest-khāni morur kard.* — The first group reviewed the words with a spaced-repetition programme, the second with the traditional method of reading through lists.  
  _The verb مرور کرد is said once for both groups – elegant in writing, odd in speech._
- داده‌ها از طریق دو آزمون واژگان، یکی بلافاصله پس از دوره و دیگری شش هفته بعد، گردآوری شد.  
  *dāde-hā az tarigh-e do āzmun-e vāzhegān, yek-i belāfāsele pas az dowre va digari shesh hafte ba'd, gerdāvari shod.* — The data were collected through two vocabulary tests, one immediately after the course and the other six weeks later.  
  _An inanimate plural subject (داده‌ها) can take a singular verb (شد)._
- نتایج تحلیل آماری نشان داد که دو گروه در آزمون نخست تفاوت معناداری با یکدیگر نداشتند.  
  *natāyej-e tahlil-e āmāri neshān dād ke do goruh dar āzmun-e nakhost tafāvot-e ma'nādār-i bā yekdigar na-dāshtand.* — Statistical analysis showed no significant difference between the two groups in the first test.  
  _Move 6, results, in the simple past. English prefers 'showed no difference' to 'showed that they had no difference'._
- اما در آزمون دوم، میانگین نمرات گروه نخست به‌طور معناداری بالاتر بود.  
  *ammā dar āzmun-e dovvom, miyāngin-e nomarāt-e goruh-e nakhost be-towr-e ma'nādār-i bālā-tar bud.* — In the second test, however, the first group's mean score was significantly higher.  
  _به‌طور + adjective + ی makes an adverb: به‌طور معناداری 'significantly'._
- به نظر می‌رسد که تکرار فاصله‌دار بیش از آن‌که به یادگیری اولیه کمک کند، از فراموشی واژه‌ها جلوگیری می‌کند.  
  *be nazar mi-resad ke tekrār-e fāsele-dār bish az ān-ke be yādgiri-ye avvaliye komak konad, az farāmushi-ye vāzhe-hā jelowgiri mi-konad.* — It seems that spaced repetition does less to help initial learning than to stop words being forgotten.  
  _Move 7, interpretation, hedged. بیش از آن‌که + subjunctive: 'rather than, more than'._
- این یافته، چنان‌که ابینگهاوس بیش از یک سده پیش اشاره کرده بود، با منحنی فراموشی همخوانی دارد.  
  *in yāfte, chenān-ke Ebing-hāus bish az yek sade pish eshāre karde bud, bā monhani-ye farāmushi hamkhāni dārad.* — This finding agrees with the forgetting curve, as Ebbinghaus pointed out more than a century ago.  
  _Citing. The past perfect (اشاره کرده بود) because the source lies far back in time._
- از این رو، پیشنهاد می‌شود که برنامه‌های آموزش زبان فارسی مرور فاصله‌دار را در طراحی دوره‌ها لحاظ کنند.  
  *az in ru, pishnahād mi-shavad ke barnāme-hā-ye āmuzesh-e zabān-e fārsi morur-e fāsele-dār rā dar tarrāhi-ye dowre-hā lahāz konand.* — We therefore recommend that Persian-language programmes build spaced review into the design of their courses.  
  _Move 8, implications. پیشنهاد می‌شود 'it is recommended' – the standard impersonal recommendation._
- حجم کوچک نمونه از محدودیت‌های این پژوهش است و تعمیم نتایج را دشوار می‌کند.  
  *hajm-e kuchek-e nemune az mahdudiyat-hā-ye in pazhuhesh ast o ta'mim-e natāyej rā doshvār mi-konad.* — The small sample is one of the study's limitations and makes the results hard to generalise.  
  _Move 9, limitations. از … است 'is (one) of …' – no یکی needed._
- واژگان کلیدی: تکرار فاصله‌دار، یادگیری واژگان، فارسی به‌عنوان زبان دوم، حافظه‌ی بلندمدت.  
  *vāzhegān-e kelidi: tekrār-e fāsele-dār, yādgiri-ye vāzhegān, fārsi be-onvān-e zabān-e dovvom, hāfeze-ye boland-moddat.* — Keywords: spaced repetition, vocabulary learning, Persian as a second language, long-term memory.

### Two vocabularies: Arabic loans and the Academy's words

Every Persian academic writes with two vocabularies at once. One is Arabic, absorbed over a thousand years: علم (*elm*, ‘science’), تحقیق (*tahghigh*, ‘research’), مقاله (*maghāle*, ‘article’), نتیجه (*natije*, ‘result’), فرضیه (*farziye*, ‘hypothesis’). The other is Persian, much of it coined or revived in the twentieth century: دانش (*dānesh*, ‘knowledge, science’), پژوهش (*pazhuhesh*, ‘research’), چکیده (*chekide*, ‘abstract’), یافته (*yāfte*, ‘finding’), ارزیابی (*arzyābi*, ‘evaluation’).

The coining was organised. The first **Academy of Iran** (فرهنگستان ایران, 1935) approved words such as هواپیما (*havāpeymā*, ‘aeroplane’), شهرداری (*shahrdāri*, ‘municipality’) and دادگستری (*dādgostari*, ‘the justice system’). The word دانشگاه (*dāneshgāh*, ‘university’) – *place of knowledge* – became official with the founding of the University of Tehran in 1934. Today's **Academy of Persian Language and Literature** (فرهنگستان زبان و ادب فارسی, founded 1990) continues the work, with mixed success:

- پیامک (*payāmak*, ‘text message’) – a triumph: everyone says it.
- رایانه (*rāyāne*, ‘computer’) – used in writing and in official names; at home people still say کامپیوتر (*kāmpyuter*).
- یارانه (*yārāne*, ‘subsidy’) – fully adopted, because the government used it.
- رایانامه (*rāyānāme*, ‘email’) – correct, official, and rarely heard; people say ایمیل (*imeyl*).
- بالگرد (*bālgard*, ‘helicopter’) – standard in the news, while many speakers still say هلیکوپتر (*helikupter*).

The choice between the two vocabularies is a matter of **style**, sometimes of conviction. Some writers prefer the Persian word wherever one exists; others find a page full of new coinages stiff and prefer the familiar Arabic loan. Most write a mixture – and a good reader notices the mixture, because it tells you something about the writer.

### Tips

- Translating academic Persian into English? Turn nouns back into verbs. «ارزیابی برنامه صورت گرفت» is 'the programme was evaluated', not 'an evaluation of the programme took place'.
- Translating English into academic Persian? Do not copy 'by': «توسط پژوهشگر انجام شد» is a calque. Make the agent the subject («پژوهشگر … انجام داد») or drop it.
- After می‌توان and باید in impersonal use, write the short infinitive (the past stem): می‌توان گفت، باید افزود. Never «می‌توان بگوییم».
- Journal dates are in the solar calendar: add 621 or 622 to get the Western year (۱۳۹۸ = 2019–20). Many Iranian journals now print both.
- Learn academic phrases as chunks: بر اساس یافته‌ها، از این رو، چنان‌که … اشاره کرده است. You will reuse them in every essay, presentation and email to a professor.
- Long ezafe chains are read with a slight rise on each ـِ link: ارزیابیِ تأثیرِ تکرارِ فاصله‌دار. If you lose your way, find the last noun – it is usually the topic.

### Into English – and why

**Choose the right answer**

1. The best English for بررسی داده‌ها در سه مرحله انجام شد. (*barrasi-ye dāde-hā dar se marhale anjām shod.*) — An examination of the data was done in three stages. / The data were examined in three stages. / We will examine the data in three stages.
2. The best English for به نظر می‌رسد که این روش مؤثر باشد. (*be nazar mi-resad ke in ravesh mo'asser bāshad.*) — This method is effective. / In my opinion this method was effective. / This method may well be effective.
3. The best English for پیشنهاد می‌شود که دوره‌ها کوتاه‌تر شوند. (*pishnahād mi-shavad ke dowre-hā kutāh-tar shavand.*) — It is recommended that the courses be shortened. / They proposed short courses. / Courses are suggested to become short.
4. An editor has circled این مقاله توسط نویسنده نوشته شد. (*in maghāle tavassot-e nevisande neveshte shod.*) What will she suggest? — نویسنده این مقاله را نوشت. / نوشتن این مقاله توسط نویسنده انجام شد. / این مقاله به وسیله‌ی نویسنده نوشته گردید.
5. Which of these is a twentieth-century Persian coinage rather than an Arabic loan? — تحقیق / مطالعه / رایانه
6. Which phrase introduces somebody else's claim? — به گفته‌ی … / در مجموع / بدین منظور

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. The data were examined in three stages. — Undo the nominalisation: 'examination … was done' becomes the verb 'were examined'.
2. This method may well be effective. — به نظر می‌رسد plus the subjunctive باشد is a double hedge: 'may well be', 'would seem to be'.
3. It is recommended that the courses be shortened. — پیشنهاد می‌شود is the impersonal recommendation; English can also say 'we recommend shortening the courses'.
4. نویسنده این مقاله را نوشت. — توسط + passive is a calque of English 'by'. Make the agent the subject of an active verb.
5. رایانه — رایانه (from رایانیدن, 'to compute') was coined by the Academy; تحقیق and مطالعه are Arabic.
6. به گفته‌ی … — به گفته‌ی X = 'according to X'. در مجموع sums up; بدین منظور introduces a purpose.

</details>

### Build the sentence

**Put the words in order**

1. این / که / انگیزه / پژوهش / مهم / نشان / می‌دهد / است. — This study shows that motivation is important.
2. نخست / که / نظر / به / می‌رسد / فرضیه‌ی / است. / درست — It seems that the first hypothesis is correct.
3. طریق / از / گردآوری / شدند. / پرسش‌نامه / داده‌ها — The data were collected by means of a questionnaire.
4. نتایج / باید / را / با / داد. / احتیاط / تعمیم — The results should be generalised with caution.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. این پژوهش نشان می‌دهد که انگیزه مهم است.
2. به نظر می‌رسد که فرضیه‌ی نخست درست است.
3. داده‌ها از طریق پرسش‌نامه گردآوری شدند.
4. نتایج را باید با احتیاط تعمیم داد.

</details>

### Listen and choose

**Listening**

1. (Listen on the website.) On the basis of the findings, the second hypothesis was rejected. / The findings confirmed the second hypothesis. / The second hypothesis has not been tested yet.
2. (Listen on the website.) This article has three authors. / This article is arranged in three parts. / This article was written in three weeks.
3. (Listen on the website.) The researchers asked about the participants' ages. / Age is the main factor, the researchers say. / According to the researchers, age is not the main factor.
4. (Listen on the website.) The participants chose at random. / The participants were chosen at random. / Only a few participants were chosen.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. بر اساس یافته‌ها، فرضیه‌ی دوم رد شد. — On the basis of the findings, the second hypothesis was rejected.
2. این مقاله در سه بخش تنظیم شده است. — This article is arranged in three parts.
3. به گفته‌ی پژوهشگران، سن عامل اصلی نیست. — According to the researchers, age is not the main factor.
4. شرکت‌کنندگان به‌طور تصادفی انتخاب شدند. — The participants were chosen at random.

</details>
