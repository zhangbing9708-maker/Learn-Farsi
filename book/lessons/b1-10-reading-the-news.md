# Reading the news: headlines, sources and numbers
## خواندن اخبار
*khāndan-e akhbār* — Intermediate (B1), lesson 10. [Open this lesson with audio and interactive exercises](https://zhangbing9708-maker.github.io/Learn-Farsi/course/b1-10-reading-the-news.html)

Persian news is where everything in B1 comes together – the passive, the formal future, reported speech, Arabic loanwords – packed into a style of its own. In this lesson you learn to decode headlines that have no verb (افزایش قیمت نفت), to recognise the verbs reporters use instead of 'said' (اعلام کرد، افزود، تأکید کرد، اظهار داشت…), and to read numbers, percentages and dates the way a newsreader does. You read two complete news items – a mountaineering report and a science story – with a full gloss, and finish with a guide to finding your way around a Persian news site.

**In this lesson you will learn to:**

- turn a verbless headline (افزایش قیمت نفت) back into a sentence
- recognise the reporting verbs اعلام کرد، افزود، تأکید کرد، گزارش داد، اظهار داشت and به گفته‌ی
- read percentages, millions and billions, and dates such as ۲۶ مهر ۱۴۰۵, aloud
- find your way around a Persian news site

### Climbing season on Damavand

_A sample news item, written for this lesson in the style of an Iranian news agency. Read the overline and the headline, then the first sentence – the lead – which tells you what, where and when. Then notice how each new paragraph hangs on a reporting verb. Every sentence has a gloss._

- فدراسیون کوه‌نوردی هشدار داد:  
  *fedrāsiyon-e kuh-navardi hoshdār dād:* — Mountaineering federation warns:  
  _The overline (روتیتر): a few words above the headline, usually naming the source. هشدار دادن – to warn._
- آغاز فصل صعود به دماوند؛ هشدار درباره‌ی ارتفاع‌زدگی  
  *āghāz-e fasl-e so'ud be Damāvand; hoshdār darbāre-ye ertefā'-zadegi* — Damavand climbing season opens; altitude sickness warning  
  Literally: start of the season of ascent to Damavand; warning about altitude sickness  
  _The headline: two noun phrases and no verb. آغاز – start; صعود – ascent; ارتفاع‌زدگی – altitude sickness (lit. 'being struck by height')._
- تهران – به گزارش خبرگزاری‌ها، فصل صعود به قله‌ی دماوند، بلندترین کوه ایران، با آغاز تابستان رسماً شروع شد.  
  *Tehrān – be gozāresh-e khabar-gozāri-hā, fasl-e so'ud be gholle-ye Damāvand, boland-tarin kuh-e Irān, bā āghāz-e tābestān rasman shoru' shod.* — Tehran – According to news agencies, the climbing season on Mount Damavand, Iran's highest mountain, officially opened with the start of summer.  
  _The lead: what (the climbing season), where (Damavand) and when (the start of summer), all in one sentence. به گزارش… – according to…; رسماً – officially._
- دماوند با ارتفاع ۵۶۱۰ متر، بلندترین آتشفشان آسیا نیز به شمار می‌رود.  
  *Damāvand bā ertefā'-e panj-hezār-o-sheshsad-o-dah metr, boland-tarin ātashfeshān-e Āsiyā niz be shomār mi-ravad.* — At 5,610 metres, Damavand is also counted as the highest volcano in Asia.  
  _به شمار رفتن – to be counted as, to be considered; نیز – also (written). Numbers are read in full: ۵۶۱۰ = panj hezār o sheshsad o dah._
- هر سال هزاران کوه‌نورد از ایران و کشورهای دیگر برای صعود به این قله به منطقه سفر می‌کنند.  
  *har sāl hezārān kuh-navard az Irān va keshvar-hā-ye digar barā-ye so'ud be in gholle be mantaghe safar mi-konand.* — Every year thousands of climbers from Iran and other countries travel to the area to climb the peak.  
  _هزاران – thousands (هزار + the plural -ān); منطقه – region, area._
- رئیس فدراسیون کوه‌نوردی روز یکشنبه در یک نشست خبری اعلام کرد که بیشتر صعودها از مسیر جنوبی انجام می‌شود.  
  *ra'is-e fedrāsiyon-e kuh-navardi ruz-e yekshanbe dar yek neshast-e khabari e'lām kard ke bishtar-e so'ud-hā az masir-e jonubi anjām mi-shavad.* — The head of the mountaineering federation announced at a press conference on Sunday that most ascents are made by the southern route.  
  _نشست خبری – press conference; اعلام کرد که… – announced that…; انجام شدن – to be carried out, a favourite passive of the news._
- وی افزود: «کوه‌نوردان باید دست‌کم یک شب در پناهگاه بارگاه سوم، در ارتفاع ۴۲۰۰ متری، بمانند تا بدنشان به ارتفاع عادت کند.»  
  *vey afzud: «kuh-navardān bāyad dast-e-kam yek shab dar panāhgāh-e Bārgāh-e Sevvom, dar ertefā'-e chahār-hezār-o-devist metri, bemānand tā badan-eshān be ertefā' ādat konad.»* — He added: 'Climbers must spend at least one night at the Bargah-e Sevvom shelter, at 4,200 metres, so that their bodies get used to the altitude.'  
  _وی – the news word for او; افزود – he added, the usual verb for a second quotation. ۴۲۰۰ متری – '4,200-metre', an adjective with -i; پناهگاه – shelter._
- او همچنین بر لزوم همراه داشتن لباس گرم و آب کافی تأکید کرد.  
  *u hamchenin bar lozum-e hamrāh dāshtan-e lebās-e garm va āb-e kāfi ta'kid kard.* — He also stressed the need to carry warm clothes and enough water.  
  _بر … تأکید کرد – stressed (something); لزوم – necessity; همراه داشتن – to carry with you._
- به گفته‌ی کارشناسان، دمای هوا در قله حتی در تابستان ممکن است به ۱۵ درجه زیر صفر برسد.  
  *be gofte-ye kārshenāsān, damā-ye havā dar gholle hattā dar tābestān momken ast be pānzdah daraje zir-e sefr beresad.* — According to experts, even in summer the temperature on the summit can fall to 15 degrees below zero.  
  _به گفته‌ی… – according to (people); ممکن است + subjunctive – may; زیر صفر – below zero._
- کارشناسان می‌گویند که بیشتر حادثه‌ها هنگام فرود و در هوای مه‌آلود رخ می‌دهد.  
  *kārshenāsān mi-guyand ke bishtar-e hādese-hā hengām-e forud va dar havā-ye meh-ālud rokh mi-dahad.* — Experts say that most accidents happen on the way down and in foggy weather.  
  _حادثه – accident (formal plural حوادث); فرود – descent; مه‌آلود – foggy; رخ دادن – to happen (formal)._
- فدراسیون از کوه‌نوردان کم‌تجربه خواست که بدون راهنمای محلی به قله نروند.  
  *fedrāsiyon az kuh-navardān-e kam-tajrobe khāst ke bedun-e rāhnamā-ye mahalli be gholle na-ravand.* — The federation asked inexperienced climbers not to go to the summit without a local guide.  
  _از کسی خواست که + subjunctive – asked someone to (lesson 3); کم‌تجربه – inexperienced._
- نزدیک قله، گازهای گوگردی از دهانه‌ی آتشفشان بیرون می‌آید و نفس کشیدن را سخت می‌کند.  
  *nazdik-e gholle, gāz-hā-ye gogerdi az dahāne-ye ātashfeshān birun mi-āyad va nafas keshidan rā sakht mi-konad.* — Near the summit, sulphurous gases come out of the volcano's crater and make breathing difficult.  
  _گوگرد – sulphur; دهانه – crater (lit. 'mouth'); نفس کشیدن – to breathe._
- فصل صعود تا پایان شهریور ادامه خواهد داشت.  
  *fasl-e so'ud tā pāyān-e Shahrivar edāme khāhad dāsht.* — The climbing season will continue until the end of Shahrivar (late September).  
  _The formal future (lesson 4) – the voice of official announcements._

**Choose the right answer**

3. What did the federation ask inexperienced climbers to do? — to climb only in winter / not to go up without a local guide / to carry a telescope

### News Persian: a style of its own

News Persian is the most formal Persian most people read every day, and it has habits of its own. Almost all of them are things you have learned in B1 – here they simply come all at once:

- **The passive and the impersonal**: اعلام شد (*e'lām shod*, ‘it was announced’), انجام می‌شود (*anjām mi-shavad*, ‘is carried out’), پیش‌بینی می‌شود (*pish-bini mi-shavad*, ‘it is forecast’).
- **The formal future** (lesson 4): ادامه خواهد داشت (*edāme khāhad dāsht*, ‘will continue’).
- **Reported speech** (lesson 3), with a whole family of verbs for *said*, and every claim tied to a source: به گزارش… (*be gozāresh-e…*, ‘according to (the report of)…’), به گفته‌ی… (*be gofte-ye…*, ‘according to (the words of)…’).
- **Top-shelf words** (lesson 8): وی (*vey*) for او, نیز (*niz*) for هم, رخ دادن (*rokh dādan*) for اتفاق افتادن, کاهش یافتن (*kāhesh yāftan*) for کم شدن.
- **Nouns instead of verbs**: not *prices rose* but *the rise of prices* – افزایش قیمت‌ها (*afzāyesh-e gheymat-hā*) – above all in headlines.

The good news: the vocabulary of the news is small and repetitive. The same fifty verbs and nouns come back every day, so after a few weeks of reading, the headlines start to read themselves.

### Headline grammar: nouns instead of verbs

Persian headlines save space by dropping the verb and turning the action into a **noun** – a verbal noun such as افزایش (*afzāyesh*, ‘increase’), کاهش (*kāhesh*, ‘decrease’), ورود (*vorud*, ‘arrival, entry’), برگزاری (*bargozāri*, ‘holding (of an event)’), آغاز (*āghāz*, ‘start’), کشف (*kashf*, ‘discovery’) or اعلام (*e'lām*, ‘announcement’). Everything else hangs off it with ezafe. To understand a headline, find that noun and ask *who did what*:

افزایش قیمت نفت (*afzāyesh-e gheymat-e naft*) – literally *increase of price of oil* – means قیمت نفت افزایش یافت (*gheymat-e naft afzāyesh yāft*, ‘the price of oil rose’).

The verbs that go with these nouns are formal too: افزایش یافتن (*afzāyesh yāftan*, ‘to increase’), کاهش یافتن (*kāhesh yāftan*, ‘to decrease’), برگزار شدن (*bargozār shodan*, ‘to be held’), آغاز شدن (*āghāz shodan*, ‘to begin’). When a headline does keep a verb, it is short – اعلام شد (*e'lām shod*) – and a colon often separates the speaker from what was said: هواشناسی: بارش‌ها ادامه دارد (*havā-shenāsi: bāresh-hā edāme dārad*).

### Who said what: reporting verbs and sources

A news story is mostly a chain of *X said that…*, and Persian reporters hate repeating گفت. In lesson 3 you met اعلام کردن (*e'lām kardan*), تأکید کردن (*ta'kid kardan*) and هشدار دادن (*hoshdār dādan*); here is the rest of the reporter's toolkit, with the job each verb does.

The reported words follow either که (*ke*) (indirect speech, with no change of tense) or a colon and «quotation marks». A story is hung on its source from the very first words: به گزارش ایسنا، (*be gozāresh-e Isnā,*, ‘according to ISNA (a news agency),’)… In the following paragraphs the speaker becomes وی (*vey*) or او (*u*), and a second quotation is introduced with افزود (*afzud*, ‘he added’) or همچنین گفت (*hamchenin goft*, ‘he also said’).

| Verb or phrase | Meaning | How it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| اعلام کرد (*e'lām kard*) | announced | decisions, dates, figures – official news |
| افزود (*afzud*) | added | the second quotation from the same speaker |
| تأکید کرد (*ta'kid kard*) | stressed | with بر: بر لزوم … تأکید کرد (*bar lozum-e … ta'kid kard*) |
| گزارش داد (*gozāresh dād*) | reported | agencies, reporters, institutions |
| اظهار داشت (*ezhār dāsht*) | stated | very formal: اظهار داشت که… (*ezhār dāsht ke…*) |
| خاطرنشان کرد (*khāter-neshān kard*) | pointed out, noted | a reminder or a background fact |
| تصریح کرد (*tasrih kard*) | made clear, stated plainly | a firm statement |
| ابراز امیدواری کرد (*ebrāz-e omidvāri kard*) | expressed the hope | + که and the subjunctive |
| هشدار داد (*hoshdār dād*) | warned | weather, health, safety |
| به گزارش… (*be gozāresh-e…*) | according to (an agency, a report) | the first words of most stories |
| به گفته‌ی… (*be gofte-ye…*) | according to (a person) | at the start of a sentence |
| به نقل از… (*be naghl az…*) | quoting… | a second-hand source |
| بر اساس… (*bar asās-e…*) | on the basis of, according to | figures, studies, reports |
| وی (*vey*) | he, she | the news word for او |

- وزارت بهداشت اعلام کرد که شمار مبتلایان به آنفولانزا کاهش یافته است.  
  *vezārat-e behdāsht e'lām kard ke shomār-e mobtalāyān be ānfolānzā kāhesh yāfte ast.* — The Ministry of Health announced that the number of flu cases has fallen.
- وی افزود: «این روند در هفته‌های آینده نیز ادامه خواهد داشت.»  
  *vey afzud: «in ravand dar hafte-hā-ye āyande niz edāme khāhad dāsht.»* — He added: 'This trend will also continue in the coming weeks.'
- رئیس سازمان آب بر اهمیت صرفه‌جویی در مصرف آب تأکید کرد.  
  *ra'is-e sāzmān-e āb bar ahammiyat-e sarfe-juyi dar masraf-e āb ta'kid kard.* — The head of the water authority stressed the importance of saving water.
- خبرگزاری‌ها گزارش دادند که بیش از ۲ میلیون نفر از نمایشگاه کتاب بازدید کرده‌اند.  
  *khabar-gozāri-hā gozāresh dādand ke bish az do milyun nafar az namāyeshgāh-e ketāb bāzdid karde-and.* — News agencies reported that more than 2 million people have visited the book fair.
- به گفته‌ی کارشناسان، قیمت مسکن تا پایان سال تغییر چندانی نخواهد کرد.  
  *be gofte-ye kārshenāsān, gheymat-e maskan tā pāyān-e sāl taghyir-e chandān-i na-khāhad kard.* — According to experts, house prices will not change much before the end of the year.
- مدیر مدرسه اظهار داشت که کلاس‌ها از شنبه به صورت حضوری برگزار می‌شود.  
  *modir-e madrese ezhār dāsht ke kelās-hā az shanbe be surat-e hozuri bargozār mi-shavad.* — The head teacher stated that classes will be held in person from Saturday.
- او ابراز امیدواری کرد که مسابقات سال آینده در اصفهان برگزار شود.  
  *u ebrāz-e omidvāri kard ke mosābeghāt-e sāl-e āyande dar Esfahān bargozār shavad.* — He expressed the hope that next year's competitions would be held in Isfahan.
- پلیس راه خاطرنشان کرد که بستن کمربند ایمنی برای همه‌ی سرنشینان الزامی است.  
  *polis-e rāh khāter-neshān kard ke bastan-e kamarband-e imeni barā-ye hame-ye sarneshinān elzāmi ast.* — The highway police pointed out that wearing a seat belt is compulsory for everyone in the car.

### Numbers, percentages and dates

News is full of numbers, and newsreaders say them in full – which is how you should read them too.

- **Digits** run left to right even inside right-to-left text, and between the parts of a number you say و (*o*): ۲۵۳ = *devist-o-panjāh-o-se*, ۱۴۰۵ = *hezār-o-chahārsad-o-panj*.
- **Big numbers**: هزار (*hezār*, ‘thousand’), میلیون (*milyun*, ‘million’), میلیارد (*milyārd*, ‘billion’). Iranian money figures are large, so ۲۵۰ هزار میلیارد تومان (250,000 billion tomans) is not an unusual phrase.
- **Per cent**: درصد (*darsad*) after the number, with no sign: ۴۰ درصد. As an adjective it takes -i: افزایش ۴۰ درصدی (*afzāyesh-e chehel darsadi*, ‘a 40 per cent rise’).
- **Decimals** are written with a slash: ۲/۵ is *do momayyez panj* – or, more simply, *do o nim*, 'two and a half'.
- **Dates** follow the Iranian solar calendar, هجری شمسی (*hejri-ye shamsi*): day – month – year, joined by ezafe: ۲۶ مهر ۱۴۰۵ = *bist-o-shesh-e Mehr-e hezār-o-chahārsad-o-panj*. Formal speech sometimes uses the ordinal: *bist-o-sheshom-e Mehr*. Western dates are marked میلادی (*milādi*).
- **Comparisons**: نسبت به سال گذشته (*nesbat be sāl-e gozashte*, ‘compared with last year’), دو برابر (*do barābar*, ‘double, twice as much’), بیش از (*bish az*, ‘more than’), کمتر از (*kam-tar az*, ‘less than’), حدود (*hodud-e*, ‘about’).

### A science story: the Geminids

_A second sample item, this time from the science pages. Notice the headline with no verb, the source formula at the start of the lead, and how each new fact hangs on a reporting phrase._

- اوج بارش شهابی جوزایی در آسمان امشب  
  *owj-e bāresh-e shahābi-ye Jowzāyi dar āsmān-e emshab* — Geminid meteor shower peaks tonight  
  Literally: the peak of the Geminid meteor shower in tonight's sky  
  _The headline: one long noun phrase, no verb. اوج – peak; بارش شهابی – meteor shower (lit. 'shooting-star rain'); جوزا – Gemini._
- به گزارش خبرگزاری‌ها، بارش شهابی جوزایی، یکی از بهترین بارش‌های شهابی سال، امشب به اوج خود می‌رسد.  
  *be gozāresh-e khabar-gozāri-hā, bāresh-e shahābi-ye Jowzāyi, yek-i az behtarin bāresh-hā-ye shahābi-ye sāl, emshab be owj-e khod mi-resad.* — According to news agencies, the Geminid meteor shower, one of the best meteor showers of the year, reaches its peak tonight.  
  _The lead. Commas mark off the inserted phrase یکی از بهترین… ('one of the best…'); به اوج خود رسیدن – to reach its peak._
- به گفته‌ی کارشناسان نجوم، در شرایط مناسب می‌توان در هر ساعت تا ۱۲۰ شهاب را با چشم غیرمسلح دید.  
  *be gofte-ye kārshenāsān-e nojum, dar sharāyet-e monāseb mi-tavān dar har sā'at tā sad-o-bist shahāb rā bā cheshm-e gheyr-e-mosallah did.* — According to astronomers, in good conditions up to 120 meteors an hour can be seen with the naked eye.  
  _کارشناس نجوم – astronomy expert; می‌توان + short infinitive (دید) = 'one can see' (lesson 1); چشم غیرمسلح – 'the unarmed eye', the naked eye._
- این شهاب‌ها ذرات کوچکی هستند که از سیارکی به نام «فایتون» جدا شده‌اند.  
  *in shahāb-hā zarrāt-e kuchak-i hastand ke az sayyārak-i be nām-e «Fāyton» jodā shode-and.* — These meteors are tiny particles that have broken off an asteroid called Phaethon.  
  _ذرات – particles (Arabic plural of ذره); سیارک – asteroid, a 'little planet': سیاره + the diminutive -ak; به نام – called._
- ذرات با سرعت حدود ۳۵ کیلومتر در ثانیه وارد جو زمین می‌شوند و می‌سوزند.  
  *zarrāt bā sor'at-e hodud-e si-o-panj kilometr dar sāniye vāred-e javv-e zamin mi-shavand va mi-suzand.* — The particles enter the Earth's atmosphere at about 35 kilometres a second and burn up.  
  _جو – atmosphere; کیلومتر در ثانیه – kilometres per second; وارد … شدن – to enter._
- بهترین زمان تماشا از نیمه‌شب تا سپیده‌دم است.  
  *behtarin zamān-e tamāshā az nime-shab tā sepide-dam ast.* — The best time to watch is from midnight until dawn.  
  _نیمه‌شب – midnight; سپیده‌دم – dawn (literary; everyday صبح زود)._
- کارشناسان توصیه می‌کنند که علاقه‌مندان از نور شهرها دور شوند.  
  *kārshenāsān towsiye mi-konand ke alāghe-mandān az nur-e shahr-hā dur shavand.* — Experts recommend that enthusiasts get away from the lights of the cities.  
  _توصیه کردن که + subjunctive – to recommend that…; علاقه‌مندان – enthusiasts, 'those who are interested'._
- به گفته‌ی یک ستاره‌شناس، کویرهای مرکزی ایران از بهترین مکان‌های جهان برای رصد آسمان هستند.  
  *be gofte-ye yek setāre-shenās, kavir-hā-ye markazi-ye Irān az behtarin makān-hā-ye jahān barā-ye rasad-e āsmān hastand.* — According to one astronomer, Iran's central deserts are among the best places in the world for observing the sky.  
  _ستاره‌شناس – astronomer; کویر – desert; رصد – observation (of the sky)._
- وی افزود که نور ماه ممکن است دیدن شهاب‌های کم‌نور را دشوار کند.  
  *vey afzud ke nur-e māh momken ast didan-e shahāb-hā-ye kam-nur rā doshvār konad.* — He added that moonlight may make faint meteors hard to see.  
  _وی افزود که… – he added that…; کم‌نور – faint; دشوار – the written word for سخت._
- برای تماشای این پدیده به تلسکوپ نیازی نیست.  
  *barā-ye tamāshā-ye in padide be teleskop niyāz-i nist.* — No telescope is needed to watch this phenomenon.  
  _پدیده – phenomenon; به چیزی نیازی نیست – there is no need for something._
- بارش شهابی جوزایی هر سال در آذرماه رخ می‌دهد.  
  *bāresh-e shahābi-ye Jowzāyi har sāl dar Āzar-māh rokh mi-dahad.* — The Geminid meteor shower happens every year in the month of Azar (December).  
  _رخ دادن – to occur (formal; everyday اتفاق افتادن)._
- به گفته‌ی منجمان، اگر آسمان صاف باشد، تماشای این بارش تجربه‌ای فراموش‌نشدنی خواهد بود.  
  *be gofte-ye monajjemān, agar āsmān sāf bāshad, tamāshā-ye in bāresh tajrobe-i farāmush-na-shodani khāhad bud.* — According to astronomers, if the sky is clear, watching this shower will be an unforgettable experience.  
  _منجم – astronomer (an older word than ستاره‌شناس); صاف – clear; فراموش‌نشدنی – unforgettable, built like the words of lesson 6._

**Choose the right answer**

2. When is the best time to watch? — at sunset / from midnight until dawn / at noon
3. What do you need to see the meteors? — a telescope / a dark, clear sky – nothing else / special glasses

### How to read a Persian news site

Persian has a lively news culture: news agencies, daily papers, radio and TV, and above all websites and apps. Whichever site you open, the layout will feel familiar once you know the words.

- **On the front page**: صفحه‌ی اصلی (*safhe-ye asli*, ‘home page’), آخرین اخبار (*ākharin akhbār*, ‘latest news’), پربازدیدترین‌ها (*por-bāzdid-tarin-hā*, ‘most read’).
- **The sections**: سیاسی (*siyāsi*, ‘politics’), اقتصادی (*eghtesādi*, ‘economy’), اجتماعی (*ejtemā'i*, ‘society’), ورزشی (*varzeshi*, ‘sport’), فرهنگی (*farhangi*, ‘culture’), علمی (*elmi*, ‘science’), بین‌الملل (*beyn-ol-melal*, ‘international’), استان‌ها (*ostān-hā*, ‘the provinces’).
- **In a story**: the روتیتر (*ru-titr*, ‘overline’) above the headline, the تیتر (*titr*, ‘headline’), the لید (*lid*, ‘lead’) – the first sentence or two, which answer *who, what, where, when* – and then the body, which usually begins به گزارش… (*be gozāresh-e…*).

**A reading strategy.** First read the headline and find the action noun (افزایش, کاهش, برگزاری…). Then read the lead: it holds the whole story. Skim the body for the reporting verbs – each one introduces a new speaker or a new fact. Look up only the words you meet twice.

Persian newspapers go back to the 19th century. One of the most famous of the early 20th century, کاوه (*Kāve*), was published by Iranian writers in Berlin from 1916, and its pages helped to shape modern Persian prose. Today the same formal style lives on in agency reports, while opinion columns and social media mix it freely with spoken Persian.

### Tips

- Start with a story you already know from the news in your own language – a football tournament, a space mission, an earthquake. Knowing the facts makes the Persian easy to decode.
- Learn the reporting verbs as a set. When you see اظهار داشت or خاطرنشان کرد, read 'said' and move on.
- Four words make news Persian look familiar at once: وی = او, نیز = هم, سپس = بعد, چنین = این‌طور.
- Headlines love ezafe chains: کاهش قیمت بلیت هواپیما is decrease-of price-of ticket-of plane. Start from the first word and add 'of' at each ezafe.
- Say numbers out loud in full, as newsreaders do: ۲۵ درصد is bist-o-panj darsad, ۱۴۰۵ is hezār-o-chahārsad-o-panj.
- To turn an Iranian year into a Western one, add 621 – or 622 for dates between 1 January and the end of Esfand, which fall in the next Western year.
