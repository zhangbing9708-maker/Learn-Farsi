# Recipe: zereshk polo ba morgh
## زرشک‌پلو با مرغ
*zereshk-polow bā morgh* — [read on the website with audio](https://zhangbing9708-maker.github.io/Learn-Farsi/culture/food-recipe-zereshk-polo-ba-morgh.html)

_Cook along with Iran's favourite party dish: fluffy steamed rice crowned with saffron rice and sweet-sour barberries, served with chicken braised in a saffron and tomato sauce._

زرشک‌پلو با مرغ (*zereshk-polow bā morgh*, ‘barberry rice with chicken’) is the dish you are most likely to be served at an Iranian wedding, a party or a big family lunch. It is beautiful on the table: a mound of white rice topped with saffron-yellow rice and ruby-red barberries (زرشک (*zereshk*, ‘barberries’)), scattered with pistachios and almonds, with pieces of chicken in a golden sauce beside it.

Barberries are tiny, very sour red berries. Almost all of the world's barberries are grown in Iran, most of them in South Khorasan around Birjand and Qaen, where they are picked in autumn and dried in the sun. Cooked briefly with butter and sugar, they become sweet, sour and shiny.

### Ingredients (for 4)

**For the chicken**

- 4 chicken legs, about 1.2 kg (ران مرغ (*rān-e morgh*, ‘chicken leg’))
- 2 onions (پیاز (*piyāz*, ‘onion’))
- 1 teaspoon turmeric (زردچوبه (*zardchube*, ‘turmeric’))
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste (optional) (رب گوجه‌فرنگی (*rob-e gowje-farangi*, ‘tomato paste’))
- salt and black pepper

**For the rice**

- 3 cups (600 g) basmati rice (برنج (*berenj*, ‘rice’))
- 5 tablespoons oil (روغن (*rowghan*, ‘oil’))
- ½ teaspoon ground saffron (زعفران (*za'farān*, ‘saffron’)) dissolved in 5 tablespoons hot water
- ⅔ cup (60 g) dried barberries (زرشک (*zereshk*, ‘barberries’))
- 1–2 tablespoons sugar (شکر (*shekar*, ‘sugar’))
- 2 tablespoons (30 g) butter (کره (*kare*, ‘butter’))
- 2 tablespoons slivered pistachios or almonds (خلال پسته (*khalāl-e peste*, ‘pistachio slivers’))

### Method

1. Wash the rice until the water runs clear and soak it in salted water for 1–2 hours.
2. Slice the onions and fry them in 2 tablespoons of oil until golden. Add the turmeric, then the chicken, and brown it on all sides.
3. Stir in the tomato paste, 1 cup (250 ml) of water, a third of the saffron water, salt and pepper. Cover and simmer for 45–60 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the sauce has thickened.
4. Boil the drained rice in plenty of salted water for 6–8 minutes, until soft outside but firm at the centre, and drain it.
5. Put 3 tablespoons of oil and 3 of water in the bottom of the pot. Spoon the rice back in, building it into a mound, and poke 4–5 holes in it with the handle of a spoon.
6. Wrap the lid in a clean tea towel – a دم‌کنی (*dam-koni*, ‘lid cover’) – and put it on. Cook on medium heat for 10 minutes until the rice steams, then on the lowest heat for 45 minutes. This is دم کشیدن (*dam keshidan*, ‘to steam’).
7. Pick over the barberries for stems and grit, rinse them and soak them in cold water for 10 minutes. Drain well.
8. Melt the butter on low heat, add the barberries and sugar and stir for 1–2 minutes, just until they swell and shine.
9. Mix a cupful of the rice with the rest of the saffron water.
10. Spread the white rice on a platter, top it with the saffron rice, barberries and nuts, and serve the chicken and the broken pieces of ته‌دیگ (*tah-dig*, ‘tahdig’) alongside.

### Tips

- Barberries go dark and bitter if they cook too long or too hot. Take them off the heat the moment they plump up.
- Grind saffron with a pinch of sugar before adding the hot water; it gives more colour.
- The tea towel catches the steam so that no drops fall back onto the rice and make it soggy.
- Some cooks put a few spoons of the chicken sauce over the rice at the table; others prefer it dry.

### Variations

Many families mix the barberries with fried slivered onion or orange peel, and restaurants often use pieces of chicken breast instead of legs. For a quicker meal, the chicken can be roasted in the oven with the same onion and saffron sauce. Leftover barberries keep in the freezer for months.

### Facts

- Iran grows almost all of the world's barberries, most of them in South Khorasan around Birjand and Qaen.
- Dried barberries must be picked over and rinsed before cooking, because they often contain stems and grit.
- A dam-koni, a cloth cover on the lid, keeps drops of steam from falling back onto the rice.
- Zereshk polo ba morgh is one of the most common dishes at Iranian weddings and parties.
- Iranian cooks grind saffron with a little sugar and 'brew' it in hot water before using it.

### Say it in Persian

- زرشک را پاک کنید و خوب بشویید.  
  *zereshk rā pāk konid va khub be-shuyid.* — Pick over the barberries and rinse them well.  
  Spoken: زرشکو پاک کن و خوب بشور. *zereshk-o pāk kon o khub be-shur.*
- زرشک را ده دقیقه در آب سرد خیس کنید.  
  *zereshk rā dah daghighe dar āb-e sard khis konid.* — Soak the barberries in cold water for ten minutes.
- مرغ را با پیاز و زردچوبه سرخ کنید.  
  *morgh rā bā piyāz o zardchube sorkh konid.* — Brown the chicken with onion and turmeric.
- یک لیوان آب بریزید و درش را بگذارید.  
  *yek livān āb be-rizid va dar-ash rā be-gozārid.* — Add a glass of water and put the lid on.  
  Spoken: یه لیوان آب بریز و درشو بذار. *ye livān āb be-riz o dar-esh-o be-zār.*
- بگذارید مرغ یک ساعت آرام بپزد.  
  *be-gozārid morgh yek sā'at ārām be-pazad.* — Let the chicken cook gently for an hour.
- با دسته‌ی قاشق چند سوراخ در برنج درست کنید.  
  *bā daste-ye ghāshogh chand surākh dar berenj dorost konid.* — Make a few holes in the rice with the handle of a spoon.
- دم‌کنی را روی در قابلمه بگذارید.  
  *dam-koni rā ru-ye dar-e ghāblame be-gozārid.* — Put the cloth cover over the lid of the pot.
- شعله را کم کنید و بگذارید برنج دم بکشد.  
  *sho'le rā kam konid va be-gozārid berenj dam be-keshad.* — Turn the heat down and let the rice steam.  
  Spoken: شعله رو کم کن و بذار برنج دم بکشه. *sho'le ro kam kon o be-zār berenj dam be-keshe.*
- زرشک زود می‌سوزد، پس فقط یک دقیقه تفتش بدهید.  
  *zereshk zud mi-suzad, pas faghat yek daghighe taft-ash be-dahid.* — Barberries burn quickly, so fry them for only a minute.
- کمی از برنج را با زعفران رنگ کنید.  
  *kami az berenj rā bā za'farān rang konid.* — Colour some of the rice with saffron.
- زرشک‌پلو را با مرغ و ته‌دیگ سرو کنید.  
  *zereshk-polow rā bā morgh o tah-dig serv konid.* — Serve the barberry rice with the chicken and tahdig.
- زرشک‌پلو با مرغ غذای مهمانی است.  
  *zereshk-polow bā morgh ghazā-ye mehmāni ast.* — Barberry rice with chicken is a party dish.
