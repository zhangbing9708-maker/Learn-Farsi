# Modern Persian poets
## شاعران معاصر
*shā'erān-e mo'āser* — [read on the website with audio](https://zhangbing9708-maker.github.io/Learn-Farsi/culture/people-modern-poets.html)

_In the 20th century Persian poetry broke with a thousand years of fixed metres and rhymes. Six poets who made the change – Nima, Shamlu, Akhavan-Sales, Forough, Sepehri and Simin Behbahani – and what their work is like._

### A new kind of poem

For a thousand years a Persian poem meant regular metre, lines of equal length and a fixed rhyme. In the early 20th century, after the Constitutional Revolution and with new contact with European literature, young poets began to feel that the old forms could not hold modern life. The result was شعر نو (*she'r-e now*, ‘'new poetry'’). The poets on this page are recent, and their poems are still under copyright, so we describe their work here instead of quoting it – their collections are easy to find in any Iranian bookshop.

### Nima Yushij

Ali Esfandiari (1897–1960) took his pen-name from his village, Yush, in the mountains of Mazandaran. His long poem *Afsāne* ('Legend', 1922) is usually taken as the beginning of modern Persian poetry. Nima kept the old metres but let the length of each line follow the thought, and used rhyme only where it was needed. His poems are full of the forests, rain and night of the north, often as images of a society waiting for change. Traditional critics mocked him for years; today he is called the father of شعر نیمایی (*she'r-e nimāyi*, ‘'Nimaic' poetry’).

### Shamlu and Akhavan-Sales

![Ahmad Shamlou](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b5/Ahmad_Shamlu.jpg)
<sub>Ahmad Shamlou. Photo: [Mostafa Azizi](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ahmad_Shamlu.jpg), Public domain.</sub>

Ahmad Shamlu (1925–2000), who often signed himself A. Bāmdād ('dawn'), went further and gave up regular metre altogether. His شعر سپید (*she'r-e sepid*, ‘'white' or free verse’) draws its rhythm from old Persian prose. He wrote angry political poems and tender love poems for his wife Aida, translated widely, and spent decades on *Ketāb-e Kuche* ('The Book of the Alley'), a vast encyclopedia of Persian folklore and everyday speech.

Mehdi Akhavan-Sales (1928–1990), pen-name M. Omid ('hope'), used Nima's free line with the grand old vocabulary of Khorasan. His collection *Zemestān* ('Winter', 1956) captured the bleak mood after the coup of 1953. He is buried at Tus, near Ferdowsi.

### Forough Farrokhzad

![Forugh Farrokhzad in the 1960s](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/71/Forough_Farrokhzad,_1960s.jpg)
<sub>Forugh Farrokhzad in the 1960s. Photo: [Unknown author](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Forough_Farrokhzad,_1960s.jpg), Public domain.</sub>

Forough Farrokhzad (1935–1967) wrote about love, desire and a woman's inner life with a frankness Persian poetry had never heard from a woman. Her early books – *The Captive*, *The Wall*, *Rebellion* – caused scandal; *Another Birth* (1964) showed a mature voice in free, conversational lines. She also made the short documentary *The House Is Black* (1962) in a leper colony near Tabriz. She died in a car accident in Tehran at the age of 32.

### Sohrab Sepehri

![Sohrab Sepehri in 1975](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/Sohrab_Sepehri_(1975).jpg)
<sub>Sohrab Sepehri in 1975. Photo: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sohrab_Sepehri_(1975).jpg), Public domain.</sub>

Sohrab Sepehri (1928–1980) was a painter as well as a poet. Born in Kashan, he travelled in India and Japan and was drawn to Buddhist and Zen thought. His poems use simple words to look at the world with a child's wonder – water, trees, a mother, the light of the desert edge – and his long poem *The Sound of Water's Footsteps* (1964) is one of the best loved in the language. He gathered his work in *Hasht Ketāb* ('Eight Books', 1976) and is buried at Mashhad-e Ardehal near Kashan.

### Simin Behbahani

Simin Behbahani (1927–2014) chose the oldest lyric form, the غزل (*ghazal*), and renewed it from inside. She wrote ghazals in metres that classical poets had rarely or never used, about subjects they had never touched: war, poverty, street life and the lives of women. Admirers called her 'the Nima of the ghazal'.

### Facts

- Nima Yushij's long poem Afsaneh (1922) is often taken as the starting point of modern Persian poetry.
- Nima's pen-name comes from his home village, Yush, in Mazandaran.
- Ahmad Shamlu signed many of his poems 'A. Bamdad' – bāmdād means 'dawn'.
- Forough Farrokhzad's documentary The House Is Black (1962) was filmed in a leper colony near Tabriz.
- Sohrab Sepehri collected his poetry in one volume, Hasht Ketab ('Eight Books'), in 1976.
- Mehdi Akhavan-Sales is buried at Tus, near the tomb of Ferdowsi.
- Simin Behbahani wrote ghazals in metres that classical poets had rarely or never used.

### Say it in Persian

- نیما یوشیج را «پدر شعر نو» می‌نامند.  
  *Nimā Yushij rā «pedar-e she'r-e now» mi-nāmand.* — Nima Yushij is called 'the father of new poetry'.
- نیما در روستای یوش در مازندران به دنیا آمد.  
  *Nimā dar rustā-ye Yush dar Māzandarān be donyā āmad.* — Nima was born in the village of Yush in Mazandaran.
- در شعر نو طول مصراع‌ها یکسان نیست.  
  *dar she'r-e now tul-e mesrā'-hā yeksān nist.* — In new poetry the lines are not all the same length.
- احمد شاملو شعر سپید را رواج داد.  
  *Ahmad Shāmlu she'r-e sepid rā ravāj dād.* — Ahmad Shamlu made free verse popular.
- شاملو سال‌ها روی «کتاب کوچه» کار کرد.  
  *Shāmlu sāl-hā ru-ye «Ketāb-e Kuche» kār kard.* — Shamlu worked for years on 'The Book of the Alley'.
- تخلص مهدی اخوان ثالث «امید» بود.  
  *takhallos-e Mahdi Akhavān-e Sāles «Omid» bud.* — Mehdi Akhavan-Sales's pen-name was 'Omid' (hope).
- اخوان ثالث در توس کنار آرامگاه فردوسی به خاک سپرده شد.  
  *Akhavān-e Sāles dar Tus kenār-e ārāmgāh-e Ferdowsi be khāk seporde shod.* — Akhavan-Sales was buried in Tus, next to Ferdowsi's tomb.
- فروغ فرخزاد از زندگی و احساسات یک زن نوشت.  
  *Forugh Farrokhzād az zendegi va ehsāsāt-e yek zan nevesht.* — Forough Farrokhzad wrote about a woman's life and feelings.
- فروغ فیلم کوتاه «خانه سیاه است» را ساخت.  
  *Forugh film-e kutāh-e «Khāne siyāh ast» rā sākht.* — Forough made the short film 'The House Is Black'.
- سهراب سپهری هم شاعر بود و هم نقاش.  
  *Sohrāb Sepehri ham shā'er bud va ham naghghāsh.* — Sohrab Sepehri was both a poet and a painter.
- شعرهای سپهری درباره‌ی طبیعت و صلح است.  
  *she'r-hā-ye Sepehri darbāre-ye tabi'at va solh ast.* — Sepehri's poems are about nature and peace.
- سیمین بهبهانی غزل فارسی را نو کرد.  
  *Simin Behbahāni ghazal-e fārsi rā now kard.* — Simin Behbahani renewed the Persian ghazal.
