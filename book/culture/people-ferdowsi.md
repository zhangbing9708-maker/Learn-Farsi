# Ferdowsi
## فردوسی

### A landowner from Tus

Abu'l-Qasem Ferdowsi was born around 940 near توس (*Tus*), an old town in خراسان (*Khorāsān*, ‘Khorasan’), close to today's Mashhad. His family were دهقان (*dehghān*, ‘landowning gentry’) – the class that, three centuries after the Arab conquest, still kept alive the stories of Iran's ancient kings and heroes. Khorasan was then ruled by the Samanids, a Persian dynasty that encouraged poetry in Persian rather than Arabic.

### Thirty years on the Shahnameh

![The Court of Gayumars, Shahnameh of Shah Tahmasp](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ec/The_Court_of_Gayumars.jpg/960px-The_Court_of_Gayumars.jpg)
<sub>The Court of Gayumars, Shahnameh of Shah Tahmasp. Photo: [Attributed to Sultan Muhammad](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Court_of_Gayumars.jpg), Public domain.</sub>

In the 970s Ferdowsi set out to put the old stories into verse. He worked from a Persian prose book of kings compiled in Tus in 957, and took over about a thousand couplets written by an earlier poet, Daqiqi, who had been murdered. Tradition says the work took him thirty years; he finished the final version in 1010.

The result, the شاهنامه (*Shāhnāme*, ‘Book of Kings’), has about 50,000 بیت (*beyt*, ‘couplets’), all in one swinging metre. It runs from the first king, Kayumars, through the age of heroes – رستم (*Rostam*), his son Sohrab, the innocent prince Siyavash – to the Sasanian kings and the Arab conquest of the 7th century. Its first line is known to every Iranian schoolchild: به نام خداوند جان و خرد (*be nām-e khodāvand-e jān o kherad*, ‘in the name of the Lord of soul and wisdom’).

### The sultan and the silver

By the time Ferdowsi finished, the Samanids had fallen, and he dedicated the book to سلطان محمود غزنوی (*Soltān Mahmud-e Ghaznavi*, ‘Sultan Mahmud of Ghazni’). A famous story, told by Nezami Aruzi more than a century later, says that Mahmud had promised a gold coin for every couplet but sent silver instead. Insulted, Ferdowsi gave the money away to a bath attendant and a sherbet seller, and fled. Years later the sultan repented and sent a caravan of gifts to Tus – but as it entered one gate of the town, the poet's body was being carried out of another.

Historians doubt much of this. Later writers explained Mahmud's coldness in different ways: Ferdowsi was a Shi'i and the sultan a Sunni; the poem glorified Iranian kings at a Turkish court; or jealous courtiers turned the ruler against him.

### A tomb in his garden

![The tomb of Ferdowsi at Tus](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9d/Tomb-of-Ferdowsi_%D8%A2%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85%DA%AF%D8%A7%D9%87-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B3%DB%8C_Davoud-Davoudi_1.jpg/960px-Tomb-of-Ferdowsi_%D8%A2%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85%DA%AF%D8%A7%D9%87-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B3%DB%8C_Davoud-Davoudi_1.jpg)
<sub>The tomb of Ferdowsi at Tus. Photo: [DavoudDavoudi](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tomb-of-Ferdowsi_%D8%A2%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85%DA%AF%D8%A7%D9%87-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B3%DB%8C_Davoud-Davoudi_1.jpg), CC BY-SA 4.0.</sub>

Ferdowsi died around 1020. According to Nezami Aruzi, a local preacher refused to allow his burial in the Muslim cemetery, so he was buried in his own garden. The present آرامگاه (*ārāmgāh*, ‘tomb’) at Tus was first built in 1934 for the thousandth anniversary of his birth, in a style that recalls Achaemenid architecture, and was later rebuilt. Verses and scenes from the Shahnameh are carved on and inside it.

### The saviour of Persian

![Rostam and the White Div, from the Baysunghur Shahnameh](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/16/Rostam_and_the_White_Div_from_Baysanghar_Shahnameh.jpg)
<sub>Rostam and the White Div, from the Baysunghur Shahnameh. Photo: [Herat, court of Baysunghur](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rostam_and_the_White_Div_from_Baysanghar_Shahnameh.jpg), Public domain.</sub>

Iranians call him حکیم (*hakim*, ‘the sage’) Ferdowsi and credit him with saving their language. The Shahnameh uses relatively few Arabic words, and at a time when Arabic was the language of religion and science, it proved that Persian could carry a great epic. A thousand years later a modern reader can still follow much of it.

Its stories live outside books too. In traditional coffee houses a نقال (*naghghāl*, ‘storyteller’) performs them in front of a painted screen, and in the زورخانه (*zurkhāne*, ‘'house of strength'’) athletes train to verses from the epic. Names such as Rostam, Sohrab, Siyavash and Tahmineh are still common, and 25 Ordibehesht (15 May) is celebrated as Ferdowsi Day.

### Facts

- Ferdowsi was born around 940 near Tus in Khorasan and died around 1020.
- The Shahnameh has about 50,000 couplets, all in the same metre, the motaghāreb.
- Ferdowsi finished the final version of the Shahnameh in 1010.
- About a thousand couplets of the Shahnameh, on King Goshtasp and the coming of Zoroaster, were written by the earlier poet Daqiqi.
- The story of the silver coins first appears in Nezami Aruzi's Chahar Maqala ('Four Discourses'), written in the mid-12th century.
- 25 Ordibehesht (15 May) is Ferdowsi Day in Iran, also kept as a day for the Persian language.
- Naqqāli, the dramatic telling of Shahnameh stories, was added to UNESCO's list of intangible heritage in need of urgent safeguarding in 2011.

### Say it in Persian

- فردوسی حدود هزار سال پیش در توس زندگی می‌کرد.  
  *Ferdowsi hodud-e hezār sāl pish dar Tus zendegi mi-kard.* — Ferdowsi lived in Tus about a thousand years ago.
- توس شهری قدیمی نزدیک مشهد است.  
  *Tus shahr-i ghadimi nazdik-e Mashhad ast.* — Tus is an old town near Mashhad.
- فردوسی سی سال روی شاهنامه کار کرد.  
  *Ferdowsi si sāl ru-ye Shāhnāme kār kard.* — Ferdowsi worked on the Shahnameh for thirty years.
- شاهنامه حدود پنجاه هزار بیت دارد.  
  *Shāhnāme hodud-e panjāh hezār beyt dārad.* — The Shahnameh has about fifty thousand couplets.
- شاهنامه داستان پادشاهان و پهلوانان ایران است.  
  *Shāhnāme dāstān-e pādshāhān va pahlavānān-e Irān ast.* — The Shahnameh is the story of the kings and heroes of Iran.
- رستم مشهورترین پهلوان شاهنامه است.  
  *Rostam mashhur-tarin pahlavān-e Shāhnāme ast.* — Rostam is the most famous hero of the Shahnameh.
- فردوسی کتابش را به سلطان محمود غزنوی تقدیم کرد.  
  *Ferdowsi ketāb-ash rā be Soltān Mahmud-e Ghaznavi taghdim kard.* — Ferdowsi dedicated his book to Sultan Mahmud of Ghazni.
- طبق یک داستان، سلطان به جای سکه‌ی طلا به او سکه‌ی نقره داد.  
  *tebgh-e yek dāstān, soltān be jā-ye sekke-ye talā be u sekke-ye noghre dād.* — According to one story, the sultan gave him silver coins instead of gold.
- آرامگاه فردوسی در توس است.  
  *ārāmgāh-e Ferdowsi dar Tus ast.* — Ferdowsi's tomb is in Tus.
- فردوسی زبان فارسی را زنده نگه داشت.  
  *Ferdowsi zabān-e fārsi rā zende negah dāsht.* — Ferdowsi kept the Persian language alive.
- هنوز هم در قهوه‌خانه‌ها داستان‌های شاهنامه را نقل می‌کنند.  
  *hanuz ham dar ghahve-khāne-hā dāstān-hā-ye Shāhnāme rā naghl mi-konand.* — Stories from the Shahnameh are still told in coffee houses.
- روز ۲۵ اردیبهشت روز بزرگداشت فردوسی است.  
  *ruz-e bist-o-panj-e Ordibehesht ruz-e bozorgdāsht-e Ferdowsi ast.* — 25 Ordibehesht is the day Iran honours Ferdowsi.
