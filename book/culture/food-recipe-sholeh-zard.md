# Recipe: sholeh zard
## شله‌زرد
*shole-zard* — [read on the website with audio](https://zhangbing9708-maker.github.io/Learn-Farsi/culture/food-recipe-sholeh-zard.html)

_Cook along with sholeh zard, the golden rice pudding of saffron, sugar and rose water that Iranians cook to share, decorated with cinnamon, almonds and pistachios._

### The golden pudding of vows

![Cooking nazri food to share, Isfahan](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Nazri_boron_in_Isfahan.jpg)
<sub>Cooking nazri food to share, Isfahan. Photo: [Ali Khodaei](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nazri_boron_in_Isfahan.jpg), CC BY 4.0.</sub>

شله‌زرد (*shole-zard*, ‘saffron rice pudding’) is rice cooked so long in water that it melts into a thick, silky pudding, then sweetened with sugar and scented with saffron and rose water (گلاب (*golāb*, ‘rose water’)). زرد (*zard*, ‘yellow’) means yellow, and the colour comes from the saffron.

Sholeh zard is above all a dish for sharing. It is one of the classic نذری (*nazri*, ‘votive food’) dishes: a family that has made a vow (نذر (*nazr*, ‘vow’)) – for a recovery, a new job, a safe journey – cooks a big pot when the wish comes true and sends bowls to neighbours, friends and passers-by. It is cooked especially in the month of Muharram and in Ramadan, and also at home for no reason at all. Each bowl is decorated with lines of cinnamon, slivered almonds and pistachios, and eaten cold or at room temperature.

### Ingredients (for 4)

![Saffron threads](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/Saffron_-_premium_spice.jpg/960px-Saffron_-_premium_spice.jpg)
<sub>Saffron threads. Photo: [Shaharbano](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saffron_-_premium_spice.jpg), CC BY 4.0.</sub>

- 1 cup (200 g) short-grain rice, or broken basmati (برنج (*berenj*, ‘rice’))
- 8 cups (2 litres) water (آب (*āb*, ‘water’))
- 1½ cups (300 g) sugar (شکر (*shekar*, ‘sugar’))
- ½ teaspoon ground saffron (زعفران (*za'farān*, ‘saffron’)) dissolved in ¼ cup (60 ml) hot water
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) rose water (گلاب (*golāb*, ‘rose water’))
- 3 tablespoons (45 g) butter (کره (*kare*, ‘butter’))
- ¼ teaspoon ground cardamom, optional (هل (*hel*, ‘cardamom’))
- ¼ cup (30 g) slivered almonds (خلال بادام (*khalāl-e bādām*, ‘almond slivers’))
- to decorate: ground cinnamon (دارچین (*dārchin*, ‘cinnamon’)) and slivered pistachios (پسته (*peste*, ‘pistachio’))

### Method

1. Wash the rice until the water runs clear and soak it for at least an hour, or overnight.
2. Put the drained rice and the water in a large heavy pot and bring it to the boil. Skim off the foam.
3. Simmer, uncovered, on low to medium heat for 45–60 minutes, stirring now and then, until the grains are completely soft and begin to fall apart. Add hot water if it gets too thick.
4. Add the sugar and stir until it dissolves. Cook for 15 minutes more, stirring often – once the sugar is in, the pudding catches easily.
5. Stir in the saffron water, butter, almonds and cardamom.
6. Cover the pot with a cloth-wrapped lid and leave it on the lowest heat for 30 minutes to thicken and settle.
7. Stir in the rose water for the last 5–10 minutes.
8. Pour into bowls or onto a platter while it is still runny and let it cool. When the surface has set a little, decorate it with cinnamon and pistachios.

### Tips

- Sholeh zard thickens a lot as it cools, so it should still pour easily when you take it off the heat.
- Use a wooden spoon and scrape the bottom of the pot each time you stir.
- Rose water is strong: add it a little at a time and taste.
- To draw with cinnamon, pinch it between your fingers and let it fall in thin lines, or tap it through a paper cone.

### Decoration and variations

The cinnamon decoration is part of the tradition: lattices, flowers, hearts, or religious names on bowls cooked as nazri. Some cooks add a little more butter for richness, or use less sugar for a lighter pudding. Don't confuse it with شله‌ی مشهدی (*shole-ye mashhadi*, ‘Mashhad sholeh’), a thick savoury dish of grains, beans and meat.

### Facts

- Zard means 'yellow': the colour of sholeh zard comes from saffron.
- Sholeh zard is one of the most common nazri foods, cooked to fulfil a vow and shared with neighbours.
- It is cooked especially in the month of Muharram and in Ramadan.
- The cinnamon decoration is drawn by hand, as patterns or as religious names.
- Sholeh zard sets as it cools, so it is poured into bowls while still hot and runny.

### Say it in Persian

- برنج را از شب قبل خیس کنید.  
  *berenj rā az shab-e ghabl khis konid.* — Soak the rice the night before.
- برنج را با آب زیاد بجوشانید.  
  *berenj rā bā āb-e ziyād be-jushānid.* — Boil the rice in plenty of water.
- کف روی آب را با کفگیر بگیرید.  
  *kaf-e ru-ye āb rā bā kafgir be-girid.* — Skim the foam off the top with a skimmer.
- بگذارید برنج آن‌قدر بپزد تا خوب له شود.  
  *be-gozārid berenj ān-ghadr be-pazad tā khub leh shavad.* — Let the rice cook until it is really soft.  
  Spoken: بذار برنج اون‌قدر بپزه تا خوب له بشه. *be-zār berenj un-ghadr be-paze tā khub leh be-she.*
- شکر را اضافه کنید و هم بزنید تا حل شود.  
  *shekar rā ezāfe konid va ham be-zanid tā hal shavad.* — Add the sugar and stir until it dissolves.
- شله‌زرد را مدام هم بزنید تا ته نگیرد.  
  *shole-zard rā modām ham be-zanid tā tah na-girad.* — Keep stirring the sholeh zard so that it doesn't catch.
- زعفران دم‌کرده و کره را بریزید.  
  *za'farān-e dam-karde va kare rā be-rizid.* — Add the brewed saffron and the butter.
- گلاب را آخر کار اضافه کنید تا بویش نپرد.  
  *golāb rā ākhar-e kār ezāfe konid tā bu-yash na-parad.* — Add the rose water at the end so that its scent doesn't fade.
- شله‌زرد را در کاسه‌ها بریزید و بگذارید سرد شود.  
  *shole-zard rā dar kāse-hā be-rizid va be-gozārid sard shavad.* — Pour the sholeh zard into bowls and let it cool.  
  Spoken: شله‌زردو تو کاسه‌ها بریز و بذار سرد بشه. *shole-zard-o tu kāse-hā be-riz o be-zār sard be-she.*
- روی شله‌زرد با دارچین نقش بیندازید.  
  *ru-ye shole-zard bā dārchin naghsh bi-yandāzid.* — Draw patterns on the sholeh zard with cinnamon.
- در محرم همسایه‌ها شله‌زرد نذری پخش می‌کنند.  
  *dar Moharram hamsāye-hā shole-zard-e nazri pakhsh mi-konand.* — In Muharram the neighbours hand out votive sholeh zard.
- شله‌زرد سرد خیلی خوشمزه است.  
  *shole-zard-e sard kheyli khoshmazze ast.* — Cold sholeh zard is delicious.
