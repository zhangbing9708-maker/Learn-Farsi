مشهدMashhad

Mashhad

Iran's second city and its greatest place of pilgrimage, built around the shrine of Imam Reza – and the gateway to the tombs of Ferdowsi and Khayyam, to saffron fields and to the kebab houses of Shandiz.

Facts

  • Imam Reza, the eighth Shi'a imam, died near Tus in AD 818; Mashhad grew up around his tomb.
  • Mashhad is Iran's second-largest city, with about three million people in the 2016 census.
  • The name Mashhad is Arabic for 'place of martyrdom'.
  • The Goharshad Mosque was built in the early 15th century for Goharshad, wife of the Timurid ruler Shahrukh.
  • Ferdowsi's tomb at Tus was built in the 1930s for the millennium of the poet's birth.
  • Omar Khayyam's mausoleum in Nishapur, a lattice of stone designed by Houshang Seyhoun, was completed in 1963.
  • A mesghal, the traditional unit for selling saffron and gold, weighs about 4.6 grams.

The city of the shrine

مشهد Mashhad Mashhad, Iran's second-largest city, grew up around a tomb. In AD 818 the eighth imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam, امام رضا Emām Rezā Imam Reza, died in a village near the old city of Tus – Shi'a tradition says he was poisoned – and the shrine raised over his grave became one of the holiest places in the Shi'a world. The name Mashhad means 'place of martyrdom'.

Today millions of pilgrims come every year. A pilgrim is a زائر zā'er pilgrim, and the visit itself is a زیارت ziyārat pilgrimage.

Visiting the Haram

The shrine complex, simply called the حرم haram sanctuary, is one of the largest religious complexes in the world: a city of courtyards (صحن sahn courtyard), libraries, museums and seminaries around the golden dome over the tomb. Its oldest great building is the مسجد گوهرشاد Masjed-e Gowharshād Goharshad Mosque, built in the early 15th century for the Timurid queen Goharshad.

Dress modestly; women must wear a چادر chādor chador, which can be borrowed at the entrances. Non-Muslim visitors are usually welcome in the courtyards and museums, often with a guide from the shrine's office for foreign visitors, but the inner sanctuary around the tomb is for Muslims only. Pilgrims call Imam Reza ضامن آهو zāmen-e āhu the guarantor of the deer, after a legend in which he stood surety for a captured doe so that she could feed her fawns.

Ferdowsi, Khayyam and Nader

Mashhad is also the gateway to the poets of Khorasan. At توس Tus Tus, half an hour away, the white stone آرامگاه فردوسی ārāmgāh-e Ferdowsi tomb of Ferdowsi was built in the 1930s for the thousandth anniversary of the birth of the author of the شاهنامه Shāhnāme Shahnameh. About two hours west lies نیشابور Neyshābur Nishapur, with the mausoleum of خیام Khayyām Omar Khayyam, a lattice of stone completed in 1963, and the tomb of the mystic poet Attar.

In Mashhad itself, the tomb of نادرشاه Nāder-shāh Nader Shah shows the conqueror on horseback among his soldiers.

Saffron, barberries and shishlik

Khorasan is saffron country. Iran grows most of the world's زعفران za'farān saffron, and most of Iran's comes from Khorasan, where the crocus flowers are picked by hand in late autumn. From South Khorasan come زرشک zereshk barberries, the sour red berries scattered over rice. The classic souvenirs are saffron, barberries, نبات nabāt rock sugar and turquoise from the mines near Nishapur.

For a meal out, people drive to شاندیز Shāndiz Shandiz in the hills west of the city, famous for شیشلیک shishlik grilled lamb chops. In town, try شله‌ی مشهدی shole-ye mashhadi Mashhad sholeh, a thick, slow-cooked stew of meat, grains and pulses.

Mashti and the Mashhadi accent

Someone who has made the pilgrimage to Mashhad may be called مشهدی Mashhadi Mashhadi, or مشتی mashti Mashti for short, just as one who has been to Mecca is called حاجی hāji Hajji. In Tehran slang, mashti came to mean a decent, generous, stand-up kind of man. The Mashhadi accent is easy to recognise, and in the countryside of Khorasan you will also hear Kurdish and Turkic speech.

Getting there and when to go

Mashhad has flights from all over Iran and from neighbouring countries, and overnight trains run from Tehran. Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons; summers are warm and winters cold. The city is at its busiest at Nowruz, in the summer holidays and on the great religious anniversaries, when hotels fill up weeks ahead – so book early.

زیارت قبول!

ziyārat ghabul!

May your pilgrimage be accepted!

What Iranians say to someone who has just been on a pilgrimage.

ببخشید، ورودی خانم‌ها کجاست؟

bebakhshid, vorudi-ye khānom-hā kojāst?

Excuse me, where is the women's entrance?

خانم‌ها باید در حرم چادر سر کنند.

khānom-hā bāyad dar haram chādor sar konand.

Women must wear a chador in the shrine.

می‌توانم چادر امانت بگیرم؟

mi-tavānam chādor amānat be-giram?

Can I borrow a chador?

Spoken می‌تونم چادر امانت بگیرم؟ mi-tunam chādor amānat be-giram?

گنبد طلای حرم از دور پیداست.

gonbad-e talā-ye haram az dur peydāst.

The golden dome of the shrine can be seen from far away.

مسجد گوهرشاد را یک ملکه‌ی تیموری ساخت.

Masjed-e Gowharshād rā yek malake-ye teymuri sākht.

The Goharshad Mosque was built by a Timurid queen.

شب جمعه حرم خیلی شلوغ است.

shab-e jom'e haram kheyli sholugh ast.

On Thursday night, the eve of Friday, the shrine is very crowded.

آرامگاه فردوسی در توس است.

ārāmgāh-e Ferdowsi dar Tus ast.

Ferdowsi's tomb is in Tus.

زعفران را در پاییز با دست می‌چینند.

za'farān rā dar pāyiz bā dast mi-chinand.

Saffron is picked by hand in autumn.

یک مثقال زعفران چند است؟

yek mesghāl za'farān chand ast?

How much is a mesghal of saffron?

Spoken یه مثقال زعفران چنده؟ ye mesghāl za'farān chande?

برای سوغاتی زعفران و زرشک و نبات خریدیم.

barā-ye soghāti za'farān o zereshk o nabāt kharidim.

We bought saffron, barberries and rock sugar as souvenirs.

برای شام به شاندیز رفتیم و شیشلیک خوردیم.

barā-ye shām be Shāndiz raftim va shishlik khordim.

For dinner we went to Shandiz and ate shishlik.
