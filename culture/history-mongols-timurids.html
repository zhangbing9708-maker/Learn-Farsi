Culture / History

مغول‌ها و تیموریانThe Mongols and the Timurids

moghol-hā va Teymuriyān

In the 13th century the Mongols laid waste to Iran's great cities; within a few generations their descendants were building domes and observatories and patronising Persian poets. Timur repeated the terror a century later, and his heirs created one of the most brilliant courts in Persian history.

Facts

  • Hulagu's capture of Baghdad in 1258 ended the Abbasid caliphate after five centuries.
  • The observatory at Maragheh, founded by Nasir al-Din Tusi in 1259, was one of the most important scientific centres of the Middle Ages.
  • The mausoleum of Öljeitü at Soltaniyeh, completed around 1312, became a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2005.
  • Rashid al-Din's Compendium of Chronicles (early 14th century) is often called the first history of the world.
  • Ulugh Beg's star catalogue of 1437 listed about a thousand stars.
  • The Mughal emperors of India were Timurids, and Persian was the language of their court.
  • Behzad's 'Seduction of Yusuf' (1488), painted in Herat for a copy of Saadi's Bustan, is one of the masterpieces of Persian art.

The storm from the steppe

In 1218 a governor of the Khwarazm Shah, who then ruled most of Iran and Central Asia, had a caravan of merchants sent by چنگیز خان Changiz Khān Genghis Khan killed as spies. The Mongol answer came the next year. Between 1219 and 1222 Mongol armies took Bukhara, Samarkand, Merv, Nishapur and Herat and massacred much of their populations. Persian chroniclers give death tolls so huge that historians do not take them literally, but the destruction was real: cities emptied, irrigation canals and قنات ghanāt qanats were ruined, and parts of Khorasan took centuries to recover.

The Ilkhans

Genghis's grandson هلاکو Holāku Hulagu completed the conquest. In 1256 he destroyed the mountain castles of the Ismailis, including Alamut, and in 1258 he sacked Baghdad and put the last Abbasid caliph to death. He and his successors, the ایلخانان Ilkhānān Ilkhans, ruled Iran from Maragheh and Tabriz.

Then the conquerors were conquered by Iranian culture. Hulagu's adviser, the scholar خواجه نصیر Khāje Nasir Nasir al-Din Tusi, built a great observatory at Maragheh in 1259. In 1295 Ghazan Khan became a Muslim, and his Persian vizier Rashid al-Din wrote the جامع‌التواریخ Jāme'-ot-tavārikh Compendium of Chronicles, a history of the known world from China to Europe. Ghazan's brother Öljeitü built the vast dome of سلطانیه Soltāniye Soltaniyeh, one of the largest brick domes in the world. And along the Mongol roads Chinese art reached Iran, leaving its clouds, dragons and lotus flowers in Persian painting.

Poets in a time of ruin

Strangely, the Mongol age was also a golden age of Persian poetry. The family of مولوی Mowlavi Rumi left Balkh before the invasion and settled in Konya in Anatolia, where he wrote his great Masnavi. سعدی Sa'di Saadi travelled for decades and came home to Shiraz, which had been spared because its ruler submitted to the Mongols; there, in 1257–58, he wrote the بوستان Bustān and the گلستان Golestān. A century later حافظ Hāfez Hafez wrote his ghazals in the same city amid the wars of local dynasties – and legend says he was summoned by Timur himself.

Timur

تیمور لنگ Teymur-e Lang Timur the Lame – Tamerlane in English – was a Turkic-Mongol warlord from near Samarkand who saw himself as the heir of Genghis Khan. From 1381 he swept across Iran; at Isfahan in 1387, after a revolt, his soldiers are said to have built towers of skulls. He went on to sack Delhi (1398), Damascus and Baghdad (1401) and to defeat the Ottoman sultan at Ankara (1402), and he died in 1405 on his way to invade China. From every city he took he carried off craftsmen – Persian builders, tile-makers and painters – to adorn his capital, سمرقند Samarghand Samarkand, with turquoise domes.

The Timurid renaissance

Timur's heirs, the تیموریان Teymuriyān Timurids, turned from conquest to culture. His son Shah Rukh ruled from Herat, where his wife Gowharshad was a great builder – her mosque in Mashhad dates from 1418. His grandson Ulugh Beg, the astronomer-king of Samarkand, built an observatory there and catalogued about a thousand stars. In late-15th-century Herat, under Sultan Husayn Bayqara, the poet جامی Jāmi, the Turkic poet and minister Ali-Shir Nava'i and بهزاد Behzād, the greatest master of Persian نگارگری negārgari miniature painting, worked side by side.

Timurid taste in architecture, calligraphy, painting and poetry set the style for the Safavids, the Ottomans and above all the Mughals of India, whose first emperor, Babur, was a direct descendant of Timur.

Say it in Persian

Show

چنگیز خان در سال ۱۲۱۹ به ایران حمله کرد.

Changiz Khān dar sāl-e hezār-o-devist-o-nuzdah be Irān hamle kard.

Genghis Khan attacked Iran in 1219.

مغول‌ها شهرهای بزرگی مثل نیشابور را ویران کردند.

moghol-hā shahr-hā-ye bozorg-i mesl-e Neyshābur rā virān kardand.

The Mongols destroyed great cities such as Nishapur.

هلاکو نوه‌ی چنگیز خان بود.

Holāku nave-ye Changiz Khān bud.

Hulagu was a grandson of Genghis Khan.

هلاکو در سال ۱۲۵۸ بغداد را گرفت.

Holāku dar sāl-e hezār-o-devist-o-panjāh-o-hasht Baghdād rā gereft.

Hulagu took Baghdad in 1258.

خواجه نصیر توسی در مراغه رصدخانه‌ای بزرگ ساخت.

Khāje Nasir-e Tusi dar Marāghe rasadkhāne-i bozorg sākht.

Nasir al-Din Tusi built a great observatory in Maragheh.

گنبد سلطانیه یکی از بزرگ‌ترین گنبدهای آجری جهان است.

gonbad-e Soltāniye yek-i az bozorg-tarin gonbad-hā-ye ājori-ye jahān ast.

The dome of Soltaniyeh is one of the largest brick domes in the world.

سعدی و حافظ در شیراز زندگی می‌کردند.

Sa'di va Hāfez dar Shirāz zendegi mi-kardand.

Saadi and Hafez lived in Shiraz.

تیمور لنگ سرزمین‌های زیادی را از هند تا ترکیه گرفت.

Teymur-e Lang sarzamin-hā-ye ziyād-i rā az Hend tā Torkiye gereft.

Timur the Lame conquered many lands, from India to Turkey.

پایتخت تیمور شهر سمرقند بود.

pāytakht-e Teymur shahr-e Samarghand bud.

Timur's capital was the city of Samarkand.

نوادگان تیمور از هنرمندان و دانشمندان حمایت می‌کردند.

navādegān-e Teymur az honarmandān va dāneshmandān hemāyat mi-kardand.

Timur's descendants were patrons of artists and scholars.

الغ بیگ، نوه‌ی تیمور، ستاره‌شناس بزرگی بود.

Ologh Beyg, nave-ye Teymur, setāre-shenās-e bozorg-i bud.

Ulugh Beg, Timur's grandson, was a great astronomer.

بهزاد بزرگ‌ترین نگارگر هرات بود.

Behzād bozorg-tarin negārgar-e Herāt bud.

Behzad was the greatest painter of Herat.
