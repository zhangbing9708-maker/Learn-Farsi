حلواRecipe: Persian halva

halvā

Cook along with Persian halva: flour slowly toasted in butter or oil, then stirred with a warm syrup of saffron and rose water into a dense, glossy sweet.

Facts

  • Persian halva is made from flour toasted in fat and sweetened with saffron and rose-water syrup; sesame halva is a different sweet, called halva-ye arde.
  • Halva is served at funerals and memorial gatherings in Iran.
  • On Thursday nights, the eve of Friday, many families visit graves and hand out halva in memory of the dead.
  • The longer the flour is toasted, the darker the halva and the deeper its flavour.
  • Halva also appears on many tables in Ramadan.

A sweet for remembering

Iranian حلوا halvā halva is not the crumbly sesame halva of the shops – that is حلوا ارده halvā-ye arde sesame halva in Persian. It is a soft, dense paste of flour toasted slowly in fat and mixed with a syrup of sugar, saffron and rose water, spread on a plate and decorated with nuts.

Halva is closely tied to remembering the dead. It is served at funerals and memorial gatherings (مراسم ختم marāsem-e khatm memorial service), and on شب جمعه shab-e jom'e Thursday night – the eve of Friday, when many families visit the graves of relatives – it is cooked and shared with neighbours and passers-by in their memory. It also appears on many tables in Ramadan. It is a simple sweet, but a good halva takes patience: its colour and flavour come from how long the flour is toasted.

Ingredients (for 4)

  • 1 cup (140 g) plain white flour (آرد ārd flour)
  • ½ cup (120 ml) oil, or 100 g butter or ghee, or a mix (روغن rowghan oil, کره kare butter)
  • ½ cup (100 g) sugar (شکر shekar sugar)
  • ½ cup (120 ml) water
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) rose water (گلاب golāb rose water)
  • ¼ teaspoon ground saffron (زعفران za'farān saffron) dissolved in 2 tablespoons hot water
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cardamom, optional (هل hel cardamom)
  • to decorate: slivered pistachios (پسته peste pistachio) and almonds (بادام bādām almond)

Method

  1. Make the syrup (شربت sharbat syrup): heat the sugar and water in a small pan, stirring until the sugar dissolves, and simmer for 2–3 minutes. Off the heat, add the saffron water, rose water and cardamom. Keep it warm.
  2. Sift the flour into a wide, heavy frying pan and toast it on low to medium heat, stirring all the time, for 10–15 minutes, until it turns the colour of a pale biscuit and smells nutty.
  3. Add the oil or butter and keep stirring for another 10–15 minutes, until the mixture bubbles and turns a deep golden brown, like caramel.
  4. Take the pan off the heat. Pour in the warm syrup slowly and carefully – it hisses and spits – and stir fast.
  5. Put the pan back on low heat and stir for 2–5 minutes, until the halva thickens, turns glossy and comes away from the sides of the pan in one soft mass.
  6. Spread it on a plate and smooth it with the back of a spoon. Press patterns into the top with the tip or the rim of the spoon.
  7. Scatter over the nuts and let it cool a little before serving.

Tips

  • Don't hurry the flour. Toasted on high heat it burns before it browns, and burnt flour tastes bitter.
  • The syrup must be warm when it goes in; cold syrup makes lumps.
  • If the halva looks oily, stir it a little longer. If it is too stiff, add a spoonful of hot water.
  • Some cooks like more saffron and less rose water, others the opposite. Adjust the syrup to your taste.

Serving

Halva is eaten with tea, in small slices or spoonfuls. At memorial gatherings it is often spread on thin bread such as لواش lavāsh lavash, rolled up or cut into small squares, so that it is easy to hand out. It keeps for several days in a cool place.

Say it in Persian

Show

آرد را الک کنید و در تابه بریزید.

ārd rā alak konid va dar tābe be-rizid.

Sift the flour and put it in the frying pan.

Spoken آردو الک کن و تو تابه بریز. ārd-o alak kon o tu tābe be-riz.

آرد را روی حرارت ملایم تفت بدهید تا رنگش عوض شود.

ārd rā ru-ye harārat-e molāyem taft be-dahid tā rang-ash 'avaz shavad.

Toast the flour on a low heat until its colour changes.

آرد را بی‌وقفه هم بزنید تا نسوزد.

ārd rā bi-vaghfe ham be-zanid tā na-suzad.

Stir the flour non-stop so it doesn't burn.

روغن را اضافه کنید و هم زدن را ادامه بدهید.

rowghan rā ezāfe konid va ham zadan rā edāme be-dahid.

Add the oil and keep on stirring.

شکر و آب را بجوشانید تا شربت درست شود.

shekar o āb rā be-jushānid tā sharbat dorost shavad.

Boil the sugar and water to make a syrup.

گلاب و زعفران را به شربت اضافه کنید.

golāb o za'farān rā be sharbat ezāfe konid.

Add the rose water and saffron to the syrup.

شربت را آرام بریزید، چون روغن داغ است.

sharbat rā ārām be-rizid, chon rowghan dāgh ast.

Pour the syrup in slowly, because the oil is hot.

Spoken شربتو آروم بریز، چون روغن داغه. sharbat-o ārum be-riz, chon rowghan dāgh-e.

هم بزنید تا حلوا از تابه جدا شود.

ham be-zanid tā halvā az tābe jodā shavad.

Stir until the halva comes away from the pan.

حلوا را در بشقاب پهن کنید و با قاشق نقش بیندازید.

halvā rā dar boshghāb pahn konid va bā ghāshogh naghsh bi-yandāzid.

Spread the halva on a plate and make patterns with a spoon.

روی حلوا پسته و بادام بریزید.

ru-ye halvā peste va bādām be-rizid.

Scatter pistachios and almonds over the halva.

شب‌های جمعه برای شادی روح درگذشتگان حلوا پخش می‌کنند.

shab-hā-ye jom'e barā-ye shādi-ye ruh-e dargozashtegān halvā pakhsh mi-konand.

On Thursday nights people hand out halva in memory of the dead.

Literally: for the joy of the soul of the departed

حلوا را با چای سرو کنید.

halvā rā bā chāy serv konid.

Serve the halva with tea.
