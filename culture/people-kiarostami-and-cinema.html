کیارستمی و سینمای ایرانKiarostami and Iranian film-makers

Kiyārostami va sinemā-ye Irān

Winding village roads, children on errands, long conversations in cars, families torn by a single lie: the directors who made Iranian cinema famous – Abbas Kiarostami, Bahram Beyzaie, Dariush Mehrjui, Majid Majidi, Asghar Farhadi and others.

Facts

  • Kiarostami's Taste of Cherry shared the 1997 Palme d'Or with Shohei Imamura's The Eel.
  • The title Where Is the Friend's House? (1987) comes from a poem by Sohrab Sepehri.
  • Close-Up (1990) re-enacts a real court case, with the people involved playing themselves.
  • Mehrjui's The Cow (1969) was based on a story by Gholam-Hossein Sa'edi.
  • Children of Heaven (1997) was the first Iranian film nominated for the Oscar for best foreign-language film.
  • A Separation (2011) won the Golden Bear in Berlin and, in 2012, the first Oscar for an Iranian film; Farhadi won a second with The Salesman.
  • Jafar Panahi's It Was Just an Accident won the Palme d'Or in 2025, the second for an Iranian director.

A cinema of the real

Iranian cinema won the world's attention with films that look simple: non-professional actors, real locations, children as heroes and stories that blur the line between documentary and fiction. Many of its directors started at کانون Kānun 'the Centre', the Institute for the Intellectual Development of Children and Young Adults, which from the late 1960s made short films for young audiences and gave beginners a camera.

Abbas Kiarostami

Abbas Kiarostami (1940–2016) studied painting in Tehran and worked as a graphic designer before making his first short film for Kanoon, Bread and Alley (1970). His Where Is the Friend's House? (1987), about a boy searching the next village for a classmate's notebook, began a trilogy filmed around the village of Koker in Gilan; the next two films return to the area after the earthquake of 1990. Close-Up (1990) re-enacts a real court case, with the real people playing themselves.

His Taste of Cherry, about a man driving through the hills outside Tehran looking for someone to bury him, shared the Palme d'Or at Cannes in 1997. Later he filmed abroad, in Italy and Japan. He was also a photographer and a poet, and he died in Paris in 2016.

Beyzaie and Mehrjui

Bahram Beyzaie (born 1938) made his name as a playwright and a historian of Iranian theatre as well as a director. His films draw on myth, ritual and history; Bashu, the Little Stranger (made in 1986), about a boy from the war-torn south taken in by a farming woman in Gilan, though neither speaks the other's language, is often named in critics' polls as the best Iranian film ever made.

Dariush Mehrjui (1939–2023) made The Cow (1969), based on a story by Gholam-Hossein Sa'edi, about a villager who comes to believe he is the cow he has lost. It is seen as a founding film of the Iranian موج نو mowj-e now New Wave. His later films include the comedy The Tenants and Hamoun. He and his wife were killed at their home near Tehran in 2023.

Majid Majidi

Majid Majidi, born in 1959, is a master of films about children and families. In Children of Heaven (1997) a boy loses his little sister's only pair of shoes, and the two secretly share his sneakers, running between their school shifts. It was the first Iranian film nominated for the Oscar for best foreign-language film. The Colour of Paradise and Baran, a love story set among Afghan refugee workers in Tehran, followed.

Asghar Farhadi

Asghar Farhadi, born in 1972, came from theatre and television. His films are tense family dramas set in the Tehran middle class, in which one small دروغ dorugh lie or a single decision grows into a moral crisis with no easy answer. About Elly (2009) won the Silver Bear for directing in Berlin; A Separation (2011), about a couple divorcing and a quarrel with their housekeeper, won the Golden Bear and in 2012 became the first Iranian film to win an Oscar. The Salesman (2016) brought him a second.

And many others

The list is long: Mohsen Makhmalbaf and his daughter Samira, Rakhshan Bani-Etemad, one of the first women to direct feature films in Iran, and Jafar Panahi, whose It Was Just an Accident won the Palme d'Or in 2025. At home, the جشنواره‌ی فیلم فجر jashnvāre-ye film-e Fajr Fajr Film Festival in Tehran is the high point of the cinema year.

Say it in Persian

Show

عباس کیارستمی در سال ۱۹۴۰ در تهران به دنیا آمد.

'Abbās Kiyārostami dar sāl-e hezār-o-nohsad-o-chehel dar Tehrān be donyā āmad.

Abbas Kiarostami was born in Tehran in 1940.

او اول نقاش و طراح گرافیک بود.

u avval naghghāsh va tarrāh-e gerāfik bud.

At first he was a painter and graphic designer.

کیارستمی برای کانون پرورش فکری کودکان فیلم می‌ساخت.

Kiyārostami barā-ye Kānun-e Parvaresh-e Fekri-ye Kudakān film mi-sākht.

Kiarostami made films for Kanoon, the institute for children's development.

در فیلم‌های او جاده‌ها و ماشین‌ها زیاد دیده می‌شوند.

dar film-hā-ye u jādde-hā va māshin-hā ziyād dide mi-shavand.

Roads and cars appear often in his films.

«طعم گیلاس» در سال ۱۹۹۷ نخل طلای کن را برد.

«Ta'm-e gilās» dar sāl-e hezār-o-nohsad-o-navad-o-haft Nakhl-e Talā-ye Kan rā bord.

Taste of Cherry won the Palme d'Or at Cannes in 1997.

بهرام بیضایی نمایشنامه‌های زیادی نوشته است.

Bahrām Beyzā'i namāyesh-nāme-hā-ye ziyād-i neveshte ast.

Bahram Beyzaie has written many plays.

فیلم «باشو، غریبه‌ی کوچک» درباره‌ی پسری از جنوب ایران است.

film-e «Bāshu, gharibe-ye kuchak» darbāre-ye pesar-i az jonub-e Irān ast.

The film Bashu, the Little Stranger is about a boy from the south of Iran.

«گاو» ساخته‌ی داریوش مهرجویی یکی از نخستین فیلم‌های موج نو بود.

«Gāv» sākhte-ye Dāriush Mehrju'i yek-i az nokhostin film-hā-ye mowj-e now bud.

The Cow, by Dariush Mehrjui, was one of the first films of the New Wave.

«بچه‌های آسمان» داستان یک جفت کفش است.

«Bacche-hā-ye āsemān» dāstān-e yek joft kafsh ast.

Children of Heaven is the story of a pair of shoes.

این فیلم مجید مجیدی نامزد جایزه‌ی اسکار شد.

in film-e Majid Majidi nāmzad-e jāyeze-ye Oskār shod.

This film by Majid Majidi was nominated for an Oscar.

اصغر فرهادی دو بار جایزه‌ی اسکار را برده است.

Asghar Farhādi do bār jāyeze-ye Oskār rā borde ast.

Asghar Farhadi has won the Oscar twice.

«جدایی نادر از سیمین» درباره‌ی یک خانواده در تهران است.

«Jodā'i-ye Nāder az Simin» darbāre-ye yek khānevāde dar Tehrān ast.

A Separation is about a family in Tehran.
