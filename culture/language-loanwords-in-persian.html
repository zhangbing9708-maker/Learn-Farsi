Culture / Language

وام‌واژه‌ها در فارسیLoanwords in Persian

vām-vāzhe-hā dar fārsi

Persian has borrowed from everyone it has met: Arabic, Turkic and Mongol, French, Russian and English. Where its loanwords came from, how Persian makes them its own, and how the academies have tried to replace them.

Facts

  • The letters ث، ح، ذ، ص، ض، ط، ظ and ع appear almost only in words borrowed from Arabic.
  • The toman was long a unit of 10,000 dinars; today Iranians count prices in tomans, and one toman is ten rials.
  • Iran's first language academy, the Farhangestan-e Iran, was founded in 1935 under Reza Shah.
  • The present Academy of Persian Language and Literature was founded in 1990.
  • The idea that estekān comes from English 'east tea can' is a folk etymology: the word is Russian stakan, 'glass'.
  • Iranians often say merci, while Afghans more often say tashakor, from Arabic.
  • Persian turns borrowed nouns into verbs with kardan: telefon kardan 'to phone', fekr kardan 'to think'.

Arabic: the biggest lender

Persian has always borrowed freely, and it digests what it borrows. The biggest lender by far is Arabic. After the Islamic conquest Arabic words poured in for religion, law, science and ideas; estimates of their share of the vocabulary vary widely, but you cannot say much without them. کتاب ketāb book, وقت vaght time, فکر fekr thought, ساعت sā'at hour, clock and ممنون mamnun thankful are all Arabic.

Yet they are used the Persian way. They take Persian plurals – کتاب‌ها ketāb-hā books beside the Arabic کتب kotob – and they become verbs with a Persian helper: فکر کردن fekr kardan to think, literally 'to make thought'. Their sounds are Persianised too: ث, س and ص all sound s.

Turkic and Mongol words

For most of the last thousand years Iran's rulers were Turkic, from the Seljuks to the Qajars, and the Mongols ruled for a century. They left words for the household, the army and the state: اتاق otāgh room, قاشق ghāshogh spoon, چاقو chāghu knife and the polite titles آقا āghā Mr, sir and خانم khānom Mrs, lady. تومان tomān toman, the unit Iranians count their money in, comes from a Turkic and Mongol word for 'ten thousand': first an army of ten thousand men, then ten thousand dinars.

Merci: the French century

In the 19th and early 20th centuries French was the language of diplomacy and of Iran's first modern schools, and educated Iranians brought its words home. Many are everyday Persian now: مرسی mersi thanks, ماشین māshin car from machine, مبل mobl sofa from meuble, مانتو mānto women's long coat from manteau, دوش dush shower, and آسانسور āsānsor lift from ascenseur – which happens to begin with آسان āsān easy. Some Iranians frown on mersi and prefer ممنون mamnun, متشکرم motashakkeram or the pure Persian سپاس‌گزارم sepās-gozār-am: every borrowing has its critics.

Russian and English

Russia was Iran's northern neighbour, and the north borrowed from it: سماور samāvar samovar, استکان estekān tea glass from stakan, and اسکناس eskenās banknote, probably from Russian assignatsiya, 'paper money'. English came later, with oil, technology, sport and the internet: فوتبال futbāl football, کامپیوتر kāmpyuter computer, موبایل mobāyl mobile phone and اینترنت internet.

Making Persian words

Purists have long pushed back. In the 1930s, under Reza Shah, the first academy replaced many Arabic and Turkish words with Persian ones, and most of its coinages stuck: هواپیما havāpeymā aeroplane replaced tayyāre, and شهرداری shahrdāri city hall replaced baladiye.

Since 1990 the Academy of Persian Language and Literature, فرهنگستان زبان و ادب فارسی farhangestān-e zabān o adab-e fārsi, has proposed Persian words for new things, with mixed success. یارانه yārāne subsidy won completely – it is the usual word for the government's cash payments – and پیامک payāmak text message is everywhere. رایانه rāyāne computer is common in writing, but people still say kāmpyuter; بالگرد bālgard helicopter is heard on the news, while the street says helikopter. And the story that the Academy renamed pizza کش‌لقمه kesh-loghme stretchy bite is a joke: the Academy has denied ever proposing it.

Say it in Persian

Show

بسیاری از واژه‌های فارسی از عربی آمده‌اند.

besyāri az vāzhe-hā-ye fārsi az arabi āmade-and.

Many Persian words come from Arabic.

Spoken خیلی از کلمه‌های فارسی از عربی اومدن. kheyli az kalame-hā-ye fārsi az arabi umadan.

«کتاب» و «وقت» واژه‌های عربی هستند.

«ketāb» va «vaght» vāzhe-hā-ye arabi hastand.

Ketāb and vaght are Arabic words.

Spoken «کتاب» و «وقت» کلمه‌های عربی‌ان. «ketāb» o «vaght» kalame-hā-ye arabi-an.

«اتاق» و «قاشق» از ترکی آمده‌اند.

«otāgh» va «ghāshogh» az torki āmade-and.

Otāgh and ghāshogh come from Turkic.

Spoken «اتاق» و «قاشق» از ترکی اومدن. «otāgh» o «ghāshogh» az torki umadan.

«تومان» در زبان مغولی یعنی ده هزار.

«tomān» dar zabān-e mogholi ya'ni dah hezār.

In Mongol, toman means ten thousand.

«ماشین» در اصل یک واژه‌ی فرانسوی است.

«māshin» dar asl yek vāzhe-ye farānsavi ast.

Māshin is originally a French word.

Spoken «ماشین» در اصل یه کلمه‌ی فرانسویه. «māshin» dar asl ye kalame-ye farānsavi-ye.

تهرانی‌ها خیلی «مرسی» می‌گویند.

tehrāni-hā kheyli «mersi» mi-guyand.

Tehranis say merci a lot.

Spoken تهرانی‌ها خیلی «مرسی» می‌گن. tehrāni-hā kheyli «mersi» mi-gan.

با آسانسور به طبقه‌ی پنجم بروید.

bā āsānsor be tabaghe-ye panjom be-ravid.

Take the lift to the fifth floor.

Spoken با آسانسور برین طبقه‌ی پنجم. bā āsānsor be-rin tabaghe-ye panjom.

یک استکان چای می‌خورید؟

yek estekān chāy mi-khorid?

Will you have a glass of tea?

Spoken یه استکان چای می‌خورین؟ ye estekān chāy mi-khorin?

«یارانه» واژه‌ای است که فرهنگستان ساخت.

«yārāne» vāzhe-i ast ke farhangestān sākht.

Yārāne is a word that the Academy made.

مردم هنوز به رایانه «کامپیوتر» می‌گویند.

mardom hanuz be rāyāne «kāmpyuter» mi-guyand.

People still call a rāyāne a 'kāmpyuter'.

Spoken مردم هنوز به رایانه می‌گن «کامپیوتر». mardom hanuz be rāyāne mi-gan «kāmpyuter».

برایم پیامک بفرست.

barā-yam payāmak be-ferest.

Send me a text.

Spoken برام پیامک بفرست. barā-m payāmak be-ferest.

بعضی از واژه‌های فرهنگستان جا افتاده‌اند و بعضی نه.

ba'zi az vāzhe-hā-ye farhangestān jā oftāde-and va ba'zi na.

Some of the Academy's words have caught on and some haven't.

Spoken بعضی از کلمه‌های فرهنگستان جا افتادن و بعضی نه. ba'zi az kalame-hā-ye farhangestān jā oftādan o ba'zi na.
