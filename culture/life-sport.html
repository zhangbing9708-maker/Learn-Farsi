Culture / Everyday life

ورزشSport

varzesh

Red against blue in the Tehran derby, wrestlers who become national heroes, the drum and clubs of the zurkhaneh, and Friday mornings on the mountain trails: how Iranians play, watch and talk about sport.

Facts

  • The Tehran derby between Persepolis (red) and Esteghlal (blue) is the shahr-āvard; the two clubs together are the sorkhābi, from sorkh 'red' and ābi 'blue'.
  • Wrestling has won Iran more Olympic medals than any other sport.
  • Gholamreza Takhti, Olympic wrestling champion in 1956, is remembered as a model of javānmardi, chivalry.
  • Ali Daei scored 109 goals for Iran, the men's international record until Cristiano Ronaldo passed it in 2021.
  • UNESCO inscribed the pahlavāni and zurkhaneh rituals on its intangible heritage list in 2010, and chowgān, Iranian polo, in 2017.
  • The stone goalposts of a Safavid polo ground still stand at both ends of Naqsh-e Jahan Square in Isfahan.
  • Damavand, about 5,600 metres high, is Iran's highest mountain and the highest volcano in Asia.

Football first

فوتبال futbāl football is Iran's favourite sport by far. When the تیم ملی tim-e melli national team plays, cafés fill with fans, horns blare in the streets after a win, and every گل gol goal – the same word as 'flower' – is shouted all over the country. Iran first played at the World Cup in 1978 and has qualified many times since. Its greatest goalscorer, Ali Daei, scored 109 goals for his country, a world record in men's international football until 2021.

Red against blue

Club football is a tale of two Tehran teams: پرسپولیس Perspolis Persepolis in red and استقلال Esteghlāl Esteghlal in blue. Their match, the شهرآورد shahr-āvard city derby, is played at the huge ورزشگاه آزادی varzeshgāh-e Āzādi Azadi Stadium, and the two clubs together are nicknamed the سرخابی sorkhābi 'red-blues'. The rivalry divides families, offices and classrooms, and the question پرسپولیسی هستی یا استقلالی؟ Perspolisi hasti yā Esteghlāli? are you Persepolis or Esteghlal? is a serious one.

Wrestling

If football is the passion, کشتی koshti wrestling is the pride. It has won Iran more Olympic medals than any other sport, in both freestyle and Greco-Roman, and its champions are national heroes. The most beloved is Gholamreza Takhti, Olympic champion in 1956, remembered as much for his kindness and modesty as for his victories – a model of جوانمردی javānmardi chivalry. Watch the vowels: the same letters, کشتی, also spell کشتی kashti ship.

The house of strength

Older still is the زورخانه zurkhāne 'house of strength', the traditional gymnasium. Its door is built low, so that everyone must bow on entering. The athletes train in a sunken octagonal pit, the گود gowd pit, while the مرشد morshed master sits on a raised seat, beats a large goblet drum, the ضرب zarb drum, rings a bell and chants verses from the Shahnameh. To his rhythm the men swing heavy wooden clubs, میل mil clubs, lift wooden shields called سنگ sang 'stones', do push-ups on a board – شنا shenā push-ups; also 'swimming' – and whirl on the spot, چرخ charkh spinning. The best of them is honoured as a پهلوان pahlavān champion. In 2010 UNESCO added these rituals to its list of intangible heritage.

Volleyball, polo and more

والیبال vālibāl volleyball fills arenas too, and Iran's men's team has long been one of the strongest in Asia. Iranians also shine at weightlifting, taekwondo and futsal. And one famous game was born here: polo, چوگان chowgān polo, played by Persian kings and by the heroes of the Shahnameh. In Isfahan the stone goalposts of a Safavid polo ground still stand at both ends of Naqsh-e Jahan Square.

Friday on the mountain

On Friday mornings Tehran heads uphill. Families and groups of friends set off early for some کوه‌نوردی kuh-navardi hill walking in the Alborz – up the trails of Darband, Darakeh or Tochal – stopping at tea houses for bread, cheese and tea or a bowl of عدسی adasi lentil soup on the way to the قله gholle summit. Ask a Tehrani what they did at the weekend and the answer is often رفتیم کوه raftim kuh we went to the mountain. The keenest climb Damavand, at about 5,600 metres the highest peak in Iran.

Say it in Persian

Show

پسرم طرفدار پرسپولیس است و من طرفدار استقلال.

pesar-am tarafdār-e Perspolis ast va man tarafdār-e Esteghlāl.

My son supports Persepolis and I support Esteghlal.

Spoken پسرم پرسپولیسیه، من استقلالی‌ام. pesar-am Perspolisi-ye, man Esteghlāli-am.

به بازی پرسپولیس و استقلال شهرآورد می‌گویند.

be bāzi-ye Perspolis va Esteghlāl shahr-āvard mi-guyand.

The match between Persepolis and Esteghlal is called the derby.

Spoken به بازی پرسپولیس و استقلال می‌گن دربی. be bāzi-ye Perspolis o Esteghlāl mi-gan darbi.

دیشب بازی را در ورزشگاه آزادی دیدیم.

dishab bāzi rā dar varzeshgāh-e Āzādi didim.

Last night we watched the match at Azadi Stadium.

Spoken دیشب بازی رو تو ورزشگاه آزادی دیدیم. dishab bāzi ro tu varzeshgāh-e Āzādi didim.

کدام تیم برد؟

kodām tim bord?

Which team won?

Spoken کدوم تیم برد؟ kodum tim bord?

تیم ملی دو بر یک برد.

tim-e melli do bar yek bord.

The national team won two–one.

کشتی یکی از محبوب‌ترین ورزش‌های ایران است.

koshti yek-i az mahbub-tarin varzesh-hā-ye Irān ast.

Wrestling is one of the most popular sports in Iran.

Spoken کشتی یکی از محبوب‌ترین ورزشای ایرانه. koshti yek-i az mahbub-tarin varzesh-ā-ye Irān-e.

در زورخانه، مرشد ضرب می‌زند و شعر می‌خواند.

dar zurkhāne, morshed zarb mi-zanad va she'r mi-khānad.

In the zurkhaneh, the morshed beats the drum and chants verses.

Spoken تو زورخونه، مرشد ضرب می‌زنه و شعر می‌خونه. tu zurkhune, morshed zarb mi-zane o she'r mi-khune.

ورزشکاران با میل‌های چوبی ورزش می‌کنند.

varzeshkārān bā mil-hā-ye chubi varzesh mi-konand.

The athletes train with wooden clubs.

Spoken ورزشکارا با میلای چوبی ورزش می‌کنن. varzeshkār-ā bā mil-ā-ye chubi varzesh mi-konan.

تیم والیبال ایران در آسیا خیلی قوی است.

tim-e vālibāl-e Irān dar Āsiyā kheyli ghavi ast.

Iran's volleyball team is very strong in Asia.

Spoken تیم والیبال ایران تو آسیا خیلی قویه. tim-e vālibāl-e Irān tu Āsiyā kheyli ghavi-ye.

جمعه صبح زود با دوستانم به کوه می‌رویم.

jom'e sobh-e zud bā dustān-am be kuh mi-ravim.

Early on Friday morning my friends and I go up the mountain.

Spoken جمعه صبح زود با دوستام می‌ریم کوه. jom'e sobh-e zud bā dustām mi-rim kuh.

در راه قله، در یک قهوه‌خانه صبحانه خوردیم.

dar rāh-e gholle, dar yek ghahve-khāne sobhāne khordim.

On the way to the summit we had breakfast in a tea house.

Spoken تو راه قله، تو یه قهوه‌خونه صبحونه خوردیم. tu rāh-e gholle, tu ye ghahve-khune sobhune khordim.

تو هم فوتبال بازی می‌کنی؟

to ham futbāl bāzi mi-koni?

Do you play football too?
