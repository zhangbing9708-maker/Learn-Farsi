کباب کوبیدهRecipe: kabab koobideh

kabāb-e kubide

Cook along with kabab koobideh: minced lamb or beef kneaded with grated onion, pressed onto wide flat skewers and grilled over charcoal in a few minutes, then served with buttery rice and sumac.

Facts

  • Kubideh means 'pounded': before mincers existed, the meat was pounded by hand.
  • The onion is grated and squeezed dry, because its juice would make the meat slide off the skewer.
  • Koobideh is grilled on wide flat skewers, which give the soft meat a broad surface to cling to.
  • Chelow kabab, rice with kabab, is often called the national dish of Iran.
  • Kabab soltani, 'the sultan's kabab', is a skewer of koobideh served with a skewer of kabab-e barg.

Iran's everyday kabab

کباب کوبیده kabāb-e kubide minced kabab is the kabab Iranians eat most: long, ridged strips of minced meat and onion, grilled on wide flat skewers until brown outside and juicy inside. Its name means 'pounded', from کوبیدن kubidan to pound – before there were mincers, the meat was pounded by hand.

Served on a mound of buttery rice it becomes چلوکباب chelow-kabāb rice and kabab, often called the national dish of Iran. There are چلوکبابی chelow-kabābi chelow kabab restaurant counters in every bazaar and on every high street, busy at lunchtime with office workers and families, and many people grill koobideh at home and on picnics. A skewer of koobideh beside a skewer of کباب برگ kabāb-e barg 'leaf' kabab, made from marinated fillet, is called سلطانی soltāni 'the sultan's'.

Ingredients (for 4)

  • 1 kg minced lamb, beef or a mix, with about 20% fat (گوشت چرخ‌کرده gusht-e charkh-karde minced meat)
  • 2 medium onions, about 300 g (پیاز piyāz onion)
  • 1½ teaspoons salt (نمک namak salt) and 1 teaspoon black pepper (فلفل سیاه felfel-e siyāh black pepper)
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric, optional (زردچوبه zardchube turmeric)
  • ¼ teaspoon ground saffron in 2 tablespoons hot water, optional (زعفران za'farān saffron)
  • to serve: 6 tomatoes, 30 g butter, sumac (سماق somāgh sumac), raw onion, fresh herbs, lavash and steamed rice
  • 6–8 wide flat skewers, about 2 cm across (سیخ sikh skewer), and charcoal (زغال zoghāl charcoal)

Method

  1. Grate the onions finely, put the pulp in a clean cloth or sieve and squeeze out all the juice. This step matters: wet meat slides off the skewer.
  2. In a large bowl mix the meat with the onion pulp, salt, pepper and turmeric.
  3. Knead it with your hands for 5–10 minutes, until it turns into a smooth, sticky paste that holds together (ورز دادن varz dādan to knead).
  4. Cover and chill for 1–4 hours. Cold meat clings to the skewer better.
  5. Light the charcoal and let it burn down to glowing coals. The skewers should rest across the grill about 10 cm above them.
  6. Wet your hands. Take a ball of meat about the size of an orange (150 g), wrap it round a skewer and squeeze it along into a strip 20–25 cm long. Press your fingers into it every few centimetres to make ridges, and seal the ends well.
  7. Put the skewers on the grill and turn them constantly for the first minute, so the meat sets before it can drop. Then turn them every minute or so; koobideh needs only 6–8 minutes in all.
  8. Grill the tomatoes on separate skewers. Use a piece of lavash to slide the kababs off the skewers onto rice or bread, and brush them with saffron water.

Tips

  • Don't use lean meat: without fat, koobideh is dry and falls apart.
  • If the meat keeps falling, it is too wet or too warm. Squeeze the onion harder and chill the meat longer.
  • No grill? Make کباب تابه‌ای kabāb-e tābe-i pan kabab: press the meat into a frying pan in a thin layer, mark it into strips and cook it on both sides.

Serving

Serve koobideh on چلو chelow steamed rice with a pat of butter melting into it, sumac to sprinkle over everything, grilled tomatoes, raw onion and herbs, and a glass of دوغ dugh doogh. In old-style chelow kabab restaurants some diners also stir a raw egg yolk into their hot rice.

پیاز را رنده کنید و آبش را بگیرید.

piyāz rā rande konid va āb-ash rā be-girid.

Grate the onion and squeeze out its juice.

Spoken پیازو رنده کن و آبشو بگیر. piyāz-o rande kon o āb-esh-o be-gir.

گوشت را با پیاز، نمک و فلفل ورز بدهید.

gusht rā bā piyāz, namak o felfel varz be-dahid.

Knead the meat with the onion, salt and pepper.

Spoken گوشتو با پیاز و نمک و فلفل ورز بده. gusht-o bā piyāz o namak o felfel varz be-de.

آن‌قدر ورز بدهید تا گوشت چسبناک شود.

ān-ghadr varz be-dahid tā gusht chasbnāk shavad.

Knead until the meat becomes sticky.

بگذارید گوشت یک ساعت در یخچال بماند.

be-gozārid gusht yek sā'at dar yakhchāl be-mānad.

Let the meat rest in the fridge for an hour.

دستتان را خیس کنید تا گوشت به آن نچسبد.

dast-etān rā khis konid tā gusht be ān na-chasbad.

Wet your hands so the meat doesn't stick to them.

گوشت را دور سیخ بپیچید و فشار بدهید.

gusht rā dowr-e sikh be-pichid va feshār be-dahid.

Wrap the meat round the skewer and press it on.

سیخ‌ها را اول کار مدام بچرخانید تا کباب نیفتد.

sikh-hā rā avval-e kār modām be-charkhānid tā kabāb na-yoftad.

Turn the skewers constantly at first so the kabab doesn't fall off.

کباب کوبیده زود می‌پزد.

kabāb-e kubide zud mi-pazad.

Koobideh cooks quickly.

روی برنج کره و سماق بریزید.

ru-ye berenj kare va somāgh be-rizid.

Put butter and sumac on the rice.

کباب را با گوجه‌ی کبابی و دوغ سرو کنید.

kabāb rā bā gowje-ye kabābi va dugh serv konid.

Serve the kabab with grilled tomatoes and doogh.

یک پرس چلوکباب کوبیده می‌خواهم.

yek pors chelow-kabāb-e kubide mi-khāham.

I'd like one portion of rice with koobideh.

Spoken یه پرس چلوکباب کوبیده می‌خوام. ye pors chelow-kabāb-e kubide mi-khām.
