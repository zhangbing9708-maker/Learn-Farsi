Culture / Everyday life

مدرسه و دانشگاهSchool and university

madrese va dāneshgāh

The school bell of the first of Mehr, uniforms and marks out of twenty, the dreaded konkur and student life at university – plus the words Iranians use when they talk about exams.

Facts

  • The Iranian school year begins on 1 Mehr, around 23 September.
  • Iranian schools mark out of 20: 10 is the usual pass mark and 20 – bist – is a perfect score.
  • The word konkur comes from French concours, 'competition'.
  • The Dar ol-Fonun, Iran's first modern institution of higher learning, opened in Tehran in 1851; the University of Tehran was founded in 1934.
  • Maryam Mirzakhani, a graduate of Sharif University of Technology, became the first woman to win the Fields Medal, in 2014.
  • In Persian a student 'gives' an exam (emtehān dādan) and a teacher 'takes' one (emtehān gereftan).
  • Most Iranian schools are single-sex, for girls or for boys.

The first of Mehr

The Iranian school year begins on اول مهر avval-e Mehr the first of Mehr, around 23 September, so in Iranian minds autumn and going back to school belong together. The first morning is a small festival: the youngest pupils, starting first grade, are welcomed with a جشن شکوفه‌ها jashn-e shokufe-hā 'festival of blossoms', school bells ring across the country, and the streets fill with children carrying a new کیف kif school bag and a pile of fresh دفتر daftar exercise books. The year runs until early summer, when the exams of خرداد Khordād Khordad, May–June are followed by a long summer holiday.

Uniforms, classrooms and marks

Most schools are for girls or for boys only, and pupils wear a uniform: for girls a long smock, the روپوش rupush school smock, with a headscarf, the مقنعه maghna'e school headscarf; for boys trousers and a shirt or jacket in the school's colours. Children spend six years at primary school, دبستان dabestān primary school, and six at secondary school, دبیرستان dabirestān high school, in two stages of three years. In many classrooms, when the معلم mo'allem teacher walks in, the class monitor, the مبصر mobser class monitor, calls out برپا barpā stand up! and everyone rises.

Marks are given out of twenty. A نمره nomre mark of ten is a pass, and بیست bist twenty is perfect – so perfect that 'bist' also means 'excellent'.

The konkur

At the end of school comes the کنکور konkur university entrance exam, from French concours. A vast number of school-leavers sit it every year, and the رتبه rotbe rank they achieve decides which university and which subject they can enter; medicine and engineering at the top universities demand the very best ranks. For many families the final year is a marathon of private کلاس کنکور kelās-e konkur konkur prep classes and thick books of practice tests, and a student with a single-digit rank may be interviewed on the news. In recent years school grades have been given more weight in the result.

Universities

Iran's first modern college, the دارالفنون Dār-ol-fonun Dar ol-Fonun, opened in Tehran in 1851, and the University of Tehran followed in 1934; its gate is one of the best-known images of the capital. Today almost every province has a public دانشگاه dāneshgāh university, where the best-ranked students study free of charge, and the fee-paying دانشگاه آزاد dāneshgāh-e āzād Azad University has campuses all over the country. Students talk about their ترم term semester, the number of واحد vāhed credits they have taken, life in the خوابگاه khābgāh dormitory and, of course, their استاد ostād professors.

A famous student

Iranian schools are proud of their mathematicians, and Iranian teams do well at the International Mathematical Olympiad. The most famous of these students is مریم میرزاخانی Maryam Mirzākhāni Maryam Mirzakhani (1977–2017). As a schoolgirl in Tehran she won two Olympiad gold medals; after studying at Sharif University of Technology she went on to Harvard and Stanford, and in 2014 she became the first woman ever to win the Fields Medal, the highest honour in mathematics.

Talking about exams

Exam talk has its own vocabulary. A student 'gives' an exam, امتحان دادن emtehān dādan to sit an exam, and the teacher 'takes' it, امتحان گرفتن emtehān gereftan to set an exam. You hope to قبول شدن ghabul shodan pass and dread having to رد شدن rad shodan fail – or, as students say, to افتادن oftādan fall a course. The night before, شب امتحان shab-e emtehān exam eve, is for cramming; the classmate who studies all the time is teased as a خرخون khar-khun swot; and everyone wants to know your معدل mo'addel grade average.

Say it in Persian

Show

مدرسه‌ها در ایران از اول مهر باز می‌شوند.

madrese-hā dar Irān az avval-e Mehr bāz mi-shavand.

Schools in Iran open on the first of Mehr.

Spoken مدرسه‌ها تو ایران از اول مهر باز می‌شن. madrese-hā tu Irān az avval-e Mehr bāz mi-shan.

برادر کوچکم امسال کلاس اول است.

barādar-e kuchak-am emsāl kelās-e avval ast.

My little brother is in first grade this year.

Spoken داداش کوچیکم امسال کلاس اوله. dādāsh-e kuchik-am emsāl kelās-e avval-e.

فردا امتحان ریاضی داریم و من هنوز درس نخوانده‌ام.

fardā emtehān-e riyāzi dārim va man hanuz dars na-khānde-am.

We have a maths exam tomorrow and I still haven't studied.

Spoken فردا امتحان ریاضی داریم و من هنوز درس نخوندم. fardā emtehān-e riyāzi dārim o man hanuz dars na-khundam.

در امتحان ریاضی بیست گرفتم!

dar emtehān-e riyāzi bist gereftam!

I got twenty out of twenty in the maths exam!

Spoken ریاضی رو بیست گرفتم! riyāzi ro bist gereftam!

در درس شیمی رد شدم.

dar dars-e shimi rad shodam.

I failed chemistry.

Spoken شیمی رو افتادم. shimi ro oftādam.

او برای کنکور یک سال تمام درس خواند.

u barā-ye konkur yek sāl-e tamām dars khānd.

She studied for the konkur for a whole year.

Spoken اون واسه‌ی کنکور یه سال تموم درس خوند. un vāse-ye konkur ye sāl-e tamum dars khund.

رتبه‌ات در کنکور چند شد؟

rotbe-at dar konkur chand shod?

What rank did you get in the konkur?

Spoken رتبه‌ت تو کنکور چند شد؟ rotbe-t tu konkur chand shod?

خواهرم در دانشگاه تهران پزشکی می‌خواند.

khāhar-am dar dāneshgāh-e Tehrān pezeshki mi-khānad.

My sister studies medicine at the University of Tehran.

Spoken خواهرم تو دانشگاه تهران پزشکی می‌خونه. khāhar-am tu dāneshgāh-e Tehrān pezeshki mi-khune.

این ترم بیست واحد برداشته‌ام.

in term bist vāhed bardāshte-am.

I've taken twenty credits this semester.

Spoken این ترم بیست واحد برداشتم. in term bist vāhed bardāshtam.

شب امتحان تا صبح بیدار ماندم.

shab-e emtehān tā sobh bidār māndam.

The night before the exam I stayed up until morning.

Spoken شب امتحان تا صبح بیدار موندم. shab-e emtehān tā sobh bidār mundam.

وقتی معلم وارد کلاس شد، همه بلند شدیم.

vaghti mo'allem vāred-e kelās shod, hame boland shodim.

When the teacher came into the classroom, we all stood up.

Spoken وقتی معلم اومد تو کلاس، همه پا شدیم. vaghti mo'allem umad tu kelās, hame pā shodim.
