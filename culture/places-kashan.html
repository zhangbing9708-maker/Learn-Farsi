Culture / Places

کاشانKashan

Kāshān

An oasis town between the mountains and the desert, with the finest merchants' houses in Iran, the oldest surviving Persian garden and a spring festival of rosewater.

Facts

  • The Fin Garden, completed in 1590, is the oldest surviving garden in Iran and part of UNESCO's 'Persian Garden' listing (2011).
  • Amir Kabir, Naser al-Din Shah's reforming chief minister, was murdered in the bathhouse of the Fin Garden in January 1852.
  • The oldest layers of Tepe Sialk go back to the 6th millennium BC.
  • The Persian word kāshi, 'tile', is widely believed to come from the name of Kashan.
  • Shah Abbas I, the greatest of the Safavid kings, is buried in Kashan.
  • The rosewater festivals of Qamsar and Niasar are held in May, when the Damask roses bloom.
  • Both the Tabatabaei and the Borujerdi houses are attributed to the architect Ustad Ali Maryam.

An oasis on the desert's edge

کاشان Kāshān Kashan sits where the کرکس Karkas Karkas mountains meet the desert, about three hours' drive south of Tehran on the road to Isfahan. People have lived here for a very long time: on the edge of town, the mound of تپه‌ی سیلک Tappe-ye Sialk Tepe Sialk holds villages going back to the 6th millennium BC.

In the Middle Ages Kashan was famous for its glazed tiles, and the Persian word for tile, کاشی kāshi tile, is widely believed to come from the city's name. Its carpets and rosewater are still famous – and in Persian sayings it is also the home of fierce scorpions.

The merchants' houses

Kashan's great sights are the homes its merchants built in the 19th century. From the street you see only blank mud walls; inside, sunken courtyards with pools and gardens are framed by carved stucco, mirror-work and stained glass. The خانه‌ی طباطبایی‌ها khāne-ye Tabātabāyi-hā Tabatabaei House was built for a family of carpet merchants. The story goes that when a young Borujerdi merchant asked to marry a Tabatabaei daughter, her family insisted that she must live in a house as fine as her father's – and so the nearby خانه‌ی بروجردی‌ها khāne-ye Borujerdi-hā Borujerdi House was built, by the same architect.

Such houses were divided into the بیرونی biruni outer rooms for guests and the اندرونی andaruni inner rooms for the family.

Fin Garden and the bathhouse

Just outside town, the باغ فین Bāgh-e Fin Fin Garden is the oldest surviving garden in Iran, completed in 1590 and enlarged under the Qajars. Water from the چشمه‌ی سلیمانیه cheshme-ye Soleymāniye Soleymaniyeh spring runs through turquoise-tiled channels and fountains under old cypresses. The garden also has a dark page in its history: in 1852 the reforming chief minister امیرکبیر Amir-kabir Amir Kabir was murdered in its bathhouse on the orders of Naser al-Din Shah.

Back in town, climb onto the domed roof of the حمام سلطان امیر احمد hammām-e Soltān Amir Ahmad Sultan Amir Ahmad bathhouse for a view over the city, and visit the مسجد آقابزرگ Masjed-e Āghā-bozorg Agha Bozorg Mosque, with its sunken courtyard and brick dome.

Rosewater and the desert

In May, when the گل محمدی gol-e Mohammadi Damask rose is in flower, the villages of قمصر Ghamsar Qamsar and نیاسر Niyāsar Niasar in the hills above Kashan hold their گلاب‌گیری golāb-giri rosewater making. Petals are picked at dawn and distilled in copper pots, and visitors come from all over Iran to watch, buy rosewater and eat rose-scented sweets.

To the north-east lies the کویر مرنجاب Kavir-e Maranjāb Maranjab desert, with sand dunes, a salt lake and a Safavid caravanserai. Seventy kilometres south-east, high in the mountains, is ابیانه Abyāne Abyaneh, a village of red-earth houses whose older women still wear long, flower-patterned headscarves.

Words to know, and when to go

Kashan is an easy trip from Tehran or Isfahan by bus or train, and many travellers stop for a night on the way between the two. Its old houses teach you the vocabulary of Persian architecture: the هشتی hashti octagonal entrance hall behind the front door, the حیاط hayāt courtyard, the حوض howz pool at its centre and the بادگیر bādgir windcatcher above.

Spring is the best season, especially the rose weeks of May; autumn is pleasant too, but summer is hot and dry.

Say it in Persian

Show

کاشان در کنار کویر است.

Kāshān dar kenār-e kavir ast.

Kashan is on the edge of the desert.

این خانه برای یک تاجر فرش ساخته شده است.

in khāne barā-ye yek tājer-e farsh sākhte shode ast.

This house was built for a carpet merchant.

اتاق‌های اندرونی مخصوص خانواده بود.

otāgh-hā-ye andaruni makhsus-e khānevāde bud.

The inner rooms were for the family.

امیرکبیر در حمام باغ فین کشته شد.

Amir-kabir dar hammām-e Bāgh-e Fin koshte shod.

Amir Kabir was killed in the bathhouse of the Fin Garden.

آب باغ فین از یک چشمه می‌آید.

āb-e Bāgh-e Fin az yek cheshme mi-āyad.

The water of the Fin Garden comes from a spring.

پشت‌بام حمام باز است؟

posht-bām-e hammām bāz ast?

Is the roof of the bathhouse open?

فصل گلاب‌گیری در اردیبهشت است.

fasl-e golāb-giri dar Ordibehesht ast.

The rosewater season is in Ordibehesht (late April to May).

گل‌ها را صبح زود می‌چینند.

gol-hā rā sobh-e zud mi-chinand.

They pick the flowers early in the morning.

یک شیشه گلاب قمصر، لطفاً.

yek shishe golāb-e Ghamsar, lotfan.

A bottle of Qamsar rosewater, please.

ابیانه یک روستای قدیمی با خانه‌های قرمز است.

Abyāne yek rustā-ye ghadimi bā khāne-hā-ye ghermez ast.

Abyaneh is an old village with red houses.

شب در کویر مرنجاب خیلی سرد می‌شود.

shab dar Kavir-e Maranjāb kheyli sard mi-shavad.

At night it gets very cold in the Maranjab desert.

Spoken شب تو کویر مرنجاب خیلی سرد می‌شه. shab tu Kavir-e Maranjāb kheyli sard mi-she.

می‌گویند کلمه‌ی «کاشی» از نام کاشان آمده است.

mi-guyand kalame-ye «kāshi» az nām-e Kāshān āmade ast.

They say the word 'kāshi' (tile) comes from the name of Kashan.
