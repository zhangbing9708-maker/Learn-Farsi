سلجوقیان و نظام‌الملکThe Seljuks and Nizam al-Mulk

Saljughiyān o Nezām-ol-molk

In the eleventh century Turkish horsemen from Central Asia built an empire from Afghanistan to Anatolia – and ran it in Persian. Their great vizier Nizam al-Mulk founded famous colleges, their architects reshaped the Friday Mosque of Isfahan, and Omar Khayyam gave them a calendar that Iran still uses.

Facts

  • The Seljuks defeated the Ghaznavids at Dandanaqan in 1040, and Tughril entered Baghdad in 1055.
  • Nizam al-Mulk (1018–1092) served Alp Arslan and Malik-Shah as vizier and was assassinated near Nahavand in 1092.
  • The Nizamiyya of Baghdad opened in 1067; al-Ghazali taught there and Saadi later studied there.
  • The south dome of the Friday Mosque of Isfahan was built for Nizam al-Mulk; the north dome, for Taj al-Mulk, is dated 1088–89.
  • The Friday Mosque of Isfahan was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 2012.
  • The Jalali calendar, prepared by Khayyam and other astronomers, was adopted in 1079 and begins the year at the spring equinox.
  • Iran's official Solar Hijri calendar is a modern form of the Jalali calendar.

Turks from the steppe

The سلجوقیان Saljughiyān Seljuks were a clan of Oghuz Turks, nomads from the steppes east of the Caspian, named after their chief Seljuk. In 1040 the brothers Tughril and Chaghri defeated the Ghaznavid army at Dandanaqan, near Merv, and took Khorasan. In 1055 طغرل Toghrol Tughril entered Baghdad, and the Abbasid caliph recognised him as سلطان soltān sultan, 'holder of power'.

His nephew Alp Arslan defeated the Byzantine emperor at Manzikert in 1071, opening Anatolia to Turkish settlement. Under Alp Arslan's son ملکشاه Malekshāh Malik-Shah (ruled 1072–1092) the empire reached its height, with its capital at اصفهان Esfahān Isfahan.

Nizam al-Mulk, the vizier

The Seljuk sultans were warriors; the man who governed for them was a Persian from Tus, Abu Ali Hasan, known by his title نظام‌الملک Nezām-ol-molk Nizam al-Mulk, 'order of the realm'. He was وزیر vazir vizier to Alp Arslan and Malik-Shah for nearly thirty years, kept the old Persian system of offices and taxes running, and made Persian the language of the court.

He also wrote, or dictated, the سیاست‌نامه Siyāsat-nāme Book of Government, a guide for rulers full of stories about just and unjust kings. In 1092 he was stabbed to death on the road near Nahavand – the murder was blamed on the Nizari Ismailis, the group Europeans later called the Assassins – and Malik-Shah died only weeks afterwards. The empire soon split between rival princes.

The Nizamiyya colleges

Nizam al-Mulk's most lasting work was a network of colleges named after him, the نظامیه Nezāmiyye Nizamiyya. The first and most famous opened in Baghdad in 1067; others followed in Nishapur, Isfahan, Balkh, Herat and other cities. Each مدرسه madrese madrasa had paid teachers, a library and lodging for students, who studied law and theology for free.

The great thinker al-Ghazali taught at the Baghdad Nizamiyya, and more than a century later Saadi of Shiraz studied there. The model – a permanent college with endowed income – spread across the Islamic world.

The Friday Mosque of Isfahan

Seljuk Isfahan's grandest building is the مسجد جامع masjed-e jāme' Friday mosque. Two of its domes are Seljuk masterpieces in plain brick: the great south dome, built for Nizam al-Mulk around 1086–87, and the small, perfectly proportioned north dome, the گنبد خاکی gonbad-e khāki 'earthen dome', built for his rival Taj al-Mulk in 1088–89.

In the twelfth century the courtyard was given four great ایوان eyvān iwans – high vaulted halls open on one side – facing each other across the court. This four-iwan plan became the classic form of the Iranian mosque and madrasa. The mosque has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2012.

Khayyam's calendar

Today عمر خیام Omar Khayyām Omar Khayyam is known worldwide for his quatrains, but in his own time he was famous as a mathematician and ستاره‌شناس setāre-shenās astronomer. Malik-Shah invited him to Isfahan, where he and other astronomers worked at a new observatory and reformed the solar calendar.

The result, adopted in 1079, was named تقویم جلالی taghvim-e Jalāli the Jalali calendar after the sultan's title Jalal al-Dawla. Its year began at the spring equinox – نوروز nowruz Nowruz – and its leap years followed the real movement of the sun so closely that it is more accurate than the Gregorian calendar used in Europe. The official calendar of Iran today is its direct descendant.

Turkish rulers, Persian culture

The Seljuk story shows a pattern that repeats through Iranian history: conquerors from outside took power but adopted Persian language, administration and taste. The Seljuks of Anatolia, at Konya, made Persian their court language too – which is why Rumi, who lived there, wrote his poetry in Persian. For a learner this is good news: the زبان فارسی zabān-e fārsi Persian language of the Seljuk chronicles, of the Siyasatnameh and of Khayyam's رباعی robā'i quatrains is close enough to modern Persian that, with some help, you will one day be able to read it.

سلجوقیان ترک‌هایی از آسیای میانه بودند.

Saljughiyān tork-hā-yi az Āsiyā-ye miyāne budand.

The Seljuks were Turks from Central Asia.

طغرل در سال ۱۰۵۵ وارد بغداد شد.

Toghrol dar sāl-e hezār-o-panjāh-o-panj vāred-e Baghdād shod.

Tughril entered Baghdad in 1055.

نظام‌الملک نزدیک به سی سال وزیر سلطان‌های سلجوقی بود.

Nezām-ol-molk nazdik be si sāl vazir-e soltān-hā-ye Saljughi bud.

Nizam al-Mulk was vizier to the Seljuk sultans for nearly thirty years.

او در شهرهای مختلف مدرسه‌هایی به نام نظامیه ساخت.

u dar shahr-hā-ye mokhtalef madrese-hā-yi be nām-e Nezāmiyye sākht.

He built colleges called Nizamiyya in different cities.

نظامیه‌ی بغداد یکی از مشهورترین مدرسه‌های جهان اسلام بود.

Nezāmiyye-ye Baghdād yek-i az mashhur-tarin madrese-hā-ye jahān-e eslām bud.

The Nizamiyya of Baghdad was one of the most famous colleges of the Islamic world.

سیاست‌نامه کتابی درباره‌ی شیوه‌ی حکومت است.

Siyāsat-nāme ketāb-i darbāre-ye shive-ye hokumat ast.

The Siyasatnameh is a book about the art of government.

مسجد جامع اصفهان دو گنبد زیبا از دوره‌ی سلجوقی دارد.

masjed-e jāme'-e Esfahān do gonbad-e zibā az dowre-ye Saljughi dārad.

The Friday Mosque of Isfahan has two beautiful domes from the Seljuk period.

این مسجد چهار ایوان دارد.

in masjed chahār eyvān dārad.

This mosque has four iwans.

ملکشاه از خیام خواست که تقویم تازه‌ای بسازد.

Malekshāh az Khayyām khāst ke taghvim-e tāze-i besāzad.

Malik-Shah asked Khayyam to make a new calendar.

سال در تقویم جلالی با نوروز شروع می‌شد.

sāl dar taghvim-e Jalāli bā nowruz shoru' mi-shod.

In the Jalali calendar the year began with Nowruz.

تقویم امروز ایران از تقویم جلالی آمده است.

taghvim-e emruz-e Irān az taghvim-e Jalāli āmade ast.

Iran's calendar today comes from the Jalali calendar.

خیام هم ریاضی‌دان بود و هم شاعر.

Khayyām ham riyāzi-dān bud va ham shā'er.

Khayyam was both a mathematician and a poet.
