صدای دوستFriend's voice

sedā-ye dust

Do you know someone who speaks Persian? Ask them to read the lines of a lesson aloud. Next to every speaker icon on the site you get two more buttons: a microphone to record your friend reading that line, and a person button to hear the recording. You can also choose to hear your friend's voice instead of the computer voice everywhere – in dialogues, Play all and the listening exercises.

My recordings

Privacy

  • With Google, each recording is saved as a small audio file in the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. This site asks only for access to that folder (drive.appdata): it cannot see, open or change anything else in your Drive, and the files don't appear among your documents.
  • The site has no server. Your recordings go straight from your browser to your Google Drive and back – they are never sent to the site's authors, never added to the site's code on GitHub and never shared with other learners.
  • On this device only keeps the recordings in this browser's storage instead; clearing the browser's site data deletes them.
  • Delete any recording above. To remove everything, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Learn Farsi → Delete hidden app data.
  • Always ask your friend before you record them.
